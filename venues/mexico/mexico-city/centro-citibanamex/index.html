About

Mexico City exhibition and convention centre at Hipódromo de las Américas, also still referred to as Centro Banamex on some atlas and organiser strings; both names are this one campus. Official capacity table (100% occupancy) gives a combined Salas A–B–C–D area of 34,283 m² and auditorium capacity of 31,632, used as exhibition_sqm and capacity_max_delegates. The official rooms webpage says ‘más de 34,000 m²’. Official address: Av. del Conscripto 311, Lomas de Sotelo, 11610 Ciudad de México, CDMX. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
