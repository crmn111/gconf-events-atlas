About

VIPARIS exhibition centre at Le Bourget, known for the International Paris Air Show and other large outdoor-capable events. Official figures are 86,242 m² in five halls and 250,000 m² outdoors. The atlas lists the Paris Nautic Show here (city field Paris; the grounds are in Le Bourget / Dugny). Coordinates follow the atlas's precise geocode for this venue.
