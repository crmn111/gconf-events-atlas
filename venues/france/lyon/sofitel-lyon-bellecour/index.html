About

Official Atout France 5-star hotel on the Rhône at 20 quai Gailleton, Lyon (classified 23 July 2024, 164 rooms). Official meetings page: largest theatre capacity is 300 in Ampère A-D (270 m²); Confluent is the largest room by area at 317 m², with a published cocktail capacity of 300. Property mailbox on the official meetings page: H0553@sofitel.com. The page states both 6 and 11 meeting rooms; the published configuration table names six main spaces plus the 45 m² Rhône room.
