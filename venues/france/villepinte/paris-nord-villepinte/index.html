VIPARIS describes this as France's largest exhibition centre. The spaces page states 243,515 m²; the homepage rounds the interior to 250,000 m² of modular interior space, plus 360,000 m² outdoor space, 9 halls, 200,000 m² of green space, 2 amphitheatres of up to 628 seats and 38 meeting rooms. Official hall areas: Hall 1 16,455 m²; Hall 2 16,389 m²; Hall 3 19,347 m²; Hall 4 19,316 m²; Hall 5A 49,734 m²; Hall 5B 26,611 m²; Hall 6 46,598 m²; Hall 7 34,065 m²; Hall 8 15,000 m². exhibition_sqm uses the detailed 243,515 m² spaces-page total. A reliable hall-centroid coordinate was not obtained (Nominatim returned the RER station rather than the halls), so lat/lon are null.

About

France's largest exhibition park, in Villepinte near Paris-Charles de Gaulle Airport and on RER B at Parc des Expositions, operated by VIPARIS. Official pages call out nine halls and a detailed interior area of 243,515 m² (the homepage also says 250,000 m²). No atlas-listed event is currently linked; included as the largest remaining French centre.
