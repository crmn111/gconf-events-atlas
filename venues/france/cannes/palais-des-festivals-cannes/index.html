About

Cannes' seafront palais of festivals and congresses, used for film, tech and professional events as well as the festival. The atlas lists ETHCC, WAICF and IPEM Wealth here. Official hall-area and delegate totals were not stored pending a fully retrieved capacities sheet; the address and coordinates follow the atlas's precise venue-level geocode and published Croisette address.
