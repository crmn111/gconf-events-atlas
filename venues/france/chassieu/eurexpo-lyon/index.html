About

Lyon's main exhibition park at Chassieu, operated by GL events, which the official site calls the largest exhibition park in France outside Paris. Official figures include 150,000 m² of surface area, seven ground-floor halls and a 4,200-seat auditorium. The atlas lists SIDO Lyon here. Coordinates follow the atlas's precise Eurexpo geocode; the city is recorded as Chassieu, where the grounds stand.
