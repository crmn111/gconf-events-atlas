About

VIPARIS congress venue at Porte Maillot, combining a 3,723-seat Grand Amphitheatre with stacked exhibition halls and conference rooms. Official space figures on the VIPARIS spaces page sum to 17,280 m² of exhibition area across levels 1–3. Several atlas conferences, including NeurIPS 2026, Devoxx France, RAISE Summit and SIGNAL Week, list this building as their main venue (some editions also name Château de Versailles for a secondary programme, which is not given its own record here).
