Halls
VIPARIS venue page (current): 169,400 m² of exhibition space across 5 pavilions, to reach 210,970 m² across 8 pavilions by 2028; 28 conference rooms and 48 meeting rooms. Pavilion 7 is the Paris Convention Centre: 70,163 m² on several levels, with a 5,200-seat plenary hall and modular partitions for around 30 rooms. Other official pavilion areas include Pavilion 1 44,936 m², Pavilion 4 19,240 m², Pavilion 5 18,257 m² and Pavilion 6 15,358 m². A technical sheet and a January 2025 VIPARIS news item also cite 212,545 m² across seven pavilions; that higher figure is noted as a source discrepancy and is not stored as exhibition_sqm. The current official homepage figure of 169,400 m² is used.

About

Paris's inner-city exhibition park at Porte de Versailles, operated by VIPARIS, including the Paris Convention Centre in Pavilion 7. The official venue page currently states 169,400 m² in five pavilions (with a published 2028 expansion). Atlas events VivaTech, Learning Technologies France, Open Source Experience, Big Data & AI Paris and Educatech Expo are listed across several halls here.
