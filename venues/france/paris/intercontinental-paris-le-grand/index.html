About

Official Atout France 5-star hotel facing the Palais Garnier (classified 9 March 2022, 2 rue Scribe, 75009 Paris, 446 rooms). Official event-venues page: Salon Opéra 450 m², theatre 556-580, classroom 350-370; Salon Berlioz also 450 m², theatre 370-450. The official 2025 meeting brochure lists higher single-room figures for Opéra (theatre 600) and combined configurations up to 1,200 cocktail; those brochure figures are recorded in spaces/notes rather than used as the headline.
