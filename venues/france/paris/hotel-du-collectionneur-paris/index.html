About

Large Art Deco hotel near Parc Monceau with a substantial official meetings offer. Official Atout France classification lists it as a 5-star Hôtel de tourisme, classified 28 November 2022, at 51/57 rue de Courcelles, 75008 Paris (496 rooms). Official meetings pages give the Normandie room as 648 m², with cocktail 650, theatre 550, banquet 480 and classroom 380. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for the named hotel at that address.
