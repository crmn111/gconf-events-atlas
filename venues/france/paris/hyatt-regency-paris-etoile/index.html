About

Large conference hotel at Porte Maillot, officially connected to the Palais des Congrès de Paris. The official Atout France classification (export of classified establishments) lists it as a 4-star Hôtel de tourisme, classified 6 March 2024, at 3 place du Général Koenig, 75017 Paris (995 rooms). Official Hyatt fact sheet: more than 3,000 m² of flexible meeting space and events for up to 1,800 people. The official capacity chart gives the combined Regency ABC space 1,400 theatre and a 2,800 reception figure marked with an asterisk; the 1,800 fact-sheet maximum is stored as the hotel delegate figure. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for the named hotel building.
