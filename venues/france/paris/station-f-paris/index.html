About

Startup campus in the former Halle Freyssinet in the 13th arrondissement, which the official FAQ calls the world's largest startup campus (opened 29 June 2017). The official address is 5 Parvis Alan Turing, 75013 Paris. The atlas lists FFT27 and France Digitale AI Day here.
