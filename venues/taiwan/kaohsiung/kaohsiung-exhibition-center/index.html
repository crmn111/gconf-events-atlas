About

Kaohsiung waterfront exhibition centre, included as a remaining major Taiwanese centre. The venue's own website pages requested in this pass returned only a JavaScript shell, so figures are taken from the official MEET TAIWAN bureau page. capacity_max_delegates is 20,000, the official exhibition-venue maximum on that page. A Dome- or hall-level indoor exhibition total was not clearly labelled, so exhibition_sqm is null. Coordinates are the Nominatim exhibition-centre pin (not the nearby station or bus-stop pins of the same name). A complete official street address was not copied from the bureau page, so address is null.
