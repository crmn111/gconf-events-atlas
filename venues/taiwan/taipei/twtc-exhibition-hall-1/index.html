About

TAITRA exhibition hall in Xinyi, Taipei, at the official address No. 5, Section 5, Xinyi Road (信義路五段五號). exhibition_sqm is 23,269, the official first-floor showground total used as the Hall 1 headline. A people-capacity headline is not published (only booth counts), so capacity_max_delegates is null. Official contact. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Book, comic, EdTech and Innotech events, and the TWTC portion of COMPUTEX, are linked here.
