About

Expo Dome (爭艷館) in Taipei Expo Park, recorded as type other. MEET Taipei, AI Expo Taiwan and Fancy Frontier are linked here from the atlas venue string. Official English venues page and the Chinese venue-detail page were retrieved, but a clean official area or capacity figure for the Dome was not extracted from those captures, so numeric fields are null rather than taken from unofficial directories. Official foundation contact and a foundation address at No. 1, Yumen St., Zhongshan Dist., Taipei City 104 appear on the official site; the Dome-specific street address was not clearly separated, so address is null. The atlas pins for these events are not marked precise, and Nominatim did not return a named Expo Dome pin, so coordinates are null.
