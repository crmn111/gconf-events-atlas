About

Malmö events and conference venue officially at Carlsgatan 12E, 211 20 Malmö (atlas listings also mention Jörgen Kocksgatan 7 as a second entrance, not copied as the official postal address). Official capacity figures are Teatern 921, Restauranggatan 1,020, Södra Hallen 350, Norra Hallen 250 and Saluhallen 1,500, the last of which is stored as capacity_max_delegates.
