About

Stockholm’s official exhibition and congress centre at Mässvägen 1, 125 80 Älvsjö. The official November 2025 overview gives a total exhibition area of 70,000 m², stored as exhibition_sqm, 17,000 seats, stored as capacity_max_delegates, and 78 meeting rooms. Official hall areas are Hall A 25,925 sqm, Hall B 11,330 sqm, Hall C 19,448 sqm and Victoria Hall 2,032 sqm (these four sum to 58,735 sqm; the official 70,000 m² headline includes further exhibition area). A broader “area 114,000” figure appearing in some materials is not stored as exhibition space.
