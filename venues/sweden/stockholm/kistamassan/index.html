About

Exhibition and congress venue in Kista, officially at Arne Beurlings Torg 5, 164 40 Kista, between Stockholm and Arlanda. Official copy states that the two halls have a total area of 9,000 square metres, stored as exhibition_sqm. Official facilities copy says conference hall M12 can accommodate up to 1,100 people and that the main hall can be configured for up to 2,500, stored as capacity_max_delegates, with 18 meeting rooms.
