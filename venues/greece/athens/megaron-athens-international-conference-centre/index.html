About

Athens concert hall and international conference centre officially at Vas. Sofias & Kokkali, 115 21 Athens, with public email. Official ICC page: Christos Lambrakis Hall 1,960 (stored as capacity_max_delegates), Alexandra Trianti Hall 1,500, Dimitris Mitropoulos Hall 450, Nikos Skalkotas Hall 380, and a Banqueting Hall 650, plus conference rooms of 25 to 180. Used by Devoxx Greece and ECAI. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin; Nominatim did not return a named pin in this pass.
