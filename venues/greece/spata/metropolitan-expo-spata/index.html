About

Airport-side exhibition centre officially at Athens International Airport Eleftherios Venizelos, Spata, with public email. Official facilities page: 50,000 sqm gross covered space, stored as exhibition_sqm, and hall areas Hall 1 12,000; Hall 2 12,500; Hall 3 13,050; Hall 4 6,200 sqm, plus conference halls C1+C2 760 sqm. Used by BEYOND Expo and the Athens International Boat Show. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
