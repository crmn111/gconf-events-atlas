About

Ho Chi Minh City exhibition and convention centre officially at 799 Nguyen Van Linh, Tan My Ward, with public email. Official Hall A and Hall B pages each publish 9,000 m² of exhibition space; those two official hall figures are stored as exhibition_sqm 18,000. The official outdoor venue of 22,000 sqm is excluded.
