Halls
Official AU facilities page: the centre can accommodate from a few people for small committee meetings to 2,500 persons for major conferences. Official named spaces include the Nelson Mandela Conference Hall (AU Summit plenary), a multipurpose room for 600 seated, a VIP conference hall for 36, a VIP lounge for 9, and VIP meeting rooms for 6 to 8. No official exhibition-hall total is published on the retrieved page.

About

Official African Union Conference Centre at AU Headquarters, Roosvelt Street (official spelling), Addis Ababa. Official facilities page: up to 2,500 persons for major conferences (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Coordinates are the named Nominatim public-building pin for African Union headquarters. The United Nations Conference Centre (UNCC-AA) is a separate official venue and was left for a later pass (retrieved home page did not publish a clear hall schedule in this extract). No atlas events are linked. Official-star hotels were not added in this pass.
