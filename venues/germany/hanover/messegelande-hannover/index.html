About

Deutsche Messe's Hannover exhibition grounds, among the largest trade-fair sites in the world. Official venue pages and the 2026 Hannover location guide give 30 halls and pavilions and 392,453 m² of hall area, with 58,000 m² of outdoor space. Hannover Messe and didacta are among the atlas events staged here.
