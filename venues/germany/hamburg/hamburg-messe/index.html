11 halls with 87,000 m² of exhibition space plus 10,000 m² outdoor area (97,000 m² total exhibition area). Hall sizes are published from 2,878 m² to 13,175 m², with ceiling heights up to 17 m. About

The inner-city exhibition grounds of Hamburg Messe und Congress, next to the television tower in the Schanze district. Official venue pages state 11 halls, 87,000 m² of indoor exhibition space and 10,000 m² outdoors. Atlas events 40th Chaos Communication Congress and OMR Festival are listed here.
