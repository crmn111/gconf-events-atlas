Halls
Official overview: five exhibition halls, 111,900 m² of covered exhibition space, 70,000 m² of outdoor areas, the Glass Hall and the Congress Center Leipzig (CCL). The company also operates KONGRESSHALLE am Zoo Leipzig in the city centre. A flexible grandstand system of 12 blocks of 360 seats (4,320 seats) is published for hall events.

About

Leipzig's exhibition grounds north of the city, with five halls, a glass entrance hall and an on-site congress centre. Official location pages state 111,900 m² of covered exhibition space and 70,000 m² outdoors. Included in this batch as one of the largest German centres not yet tied to an atlas-listed conference or expo (EuroSciPy is listed at Leipzig University of Applied Sciences, not here).
