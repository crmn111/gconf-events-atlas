Halls
Officially 50 halls spanning 23,000 m² for events of up to 9,000 participants. Largest hall: Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart (1,768 m², up to 2,255 row seats; unseated up to 3,000 guests is also published). Other major rooms include Musensaal (up to 1,367 row seats), Gustav Mahler (1,100), Ignaz Holzbauer / Vario hall (800), Alice Bensheimer and connected Dorint Kongresshotel meeting rooms.

About

Mannheim's main congress centre, operated by m:con – mannheim:congress GmbH beside the Friedrichsplatz parks. Official pages state 50 halls over 23,000 m² and a maximum of 9,000 participants, with the Mozart Hall as the principal plenary room. The atlas lists Developer Week here.
