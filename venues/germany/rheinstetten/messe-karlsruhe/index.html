About

The Karlsruhe trade-fair campus in Rheinstetten, opened in 2003. Official location pages list four 12,500 m² halls (including the darkenable dm-arena for up to 11,200 people), a 1,800 m² events hall, conference rooms and a large outdoor exhibition area of 105,000 m². The atlas lists LEARNTEC here. exhibition_sqm records the summed official indoor hall areas only.
