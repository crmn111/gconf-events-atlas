Halls
16 exhibition halls with 180,000 m² gross indoor exhibition space and up to 50,000 m² gross open-air space. Published hall areas include Hall 1 (10,800 m²), Hall 3 (12,730 m²), Hall 3A (8,400 m²), Hall 3C (9,600 m²), Hall 7 (16,410 m²) and Halls 12.0/12.2 (13,730 / 13,330 m²). Three NürnbergConvention Centers are published with convention space for up to 12,800 attendees. Six entry areas; 13,500 parking spaces.

About

Nuremberg's compact exhibition centre, including Zaha Hadid-designed Halls 3A and 3C and three convention centres. Official halls-and-site pages give 16 halls, 180,000 m² of gross indoor exhibition space and convention capacity of up to 12,800 people. Atlas events embedded world and it-sa are held here.
