About

Lake Constance exhibition centre operated by Messe Friedrichshafen GmbH. The company's official facts page states 12 halls with 87,500 m² of indoor exhibition space on a 123,000 m² site, plus outdoor areas and a demo lake. The atlas lists Interboot here. A precise official coordinate was not independently verified in this pass, so lat/lon are left null.
