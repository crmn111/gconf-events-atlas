About

Europe's largest hotel, congress and entertainment complex, according to the venue's official press kit, with a 1,125-room hotel and the Estrel Congress Center (ECC Berlin). The official press kit lists First Class Superior (4+) stars for the hotel, and Estrel Hotel Berlin appears on the Deutsche Hotelklassifizierung 4-star list for Berlin, so star_rating is stored as 4 (Superior add-on noted). Official figures: 25,000 m² of congress event space, 75 rooms, Convention Hall I and II each up to 5,000 in row seating, banquet 3,500 and reception 6,000. The official capacity chart dated 26 January 2026 gives Convention Hall I 4,700 m² (theatre 5,000, classroom 2,700, banquet 3,500) and Convention Hall II 4,600 m² (theatre 5,000, classroom 3,000, banquet 3,000). Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for Estrel Berlin at the official Sonnenallee 225 address.
