About

Landmark luxury hotel at the Brandenburg Gate with a dedicated events offer. It appears on the official Deutsche Hotelklassifizierung list of 5-star hotels in Berlin, so star_rating is 5. The official press kit describes two ballrooms and 15 function rooms, events from 1 to 500 people, and a main 500 m² ballroom on the ground floor. Official meetings pages likewise describe 16 function rooms and two ballrooms. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for Adlon Kempinski at Unter den Linden 77.
