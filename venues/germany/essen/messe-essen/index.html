About

Essen's exhibition centre, which the operator describes as a top-10 German trade-fair venue. Official figures on the current homepage are 110,000 m² of hall space in 10 halls, 20,000 m² outdoors, three congress centres and 28 event rooms. No atlas-listed conference or expo is currently linked here; included as one of the largest remaining German grounds.
