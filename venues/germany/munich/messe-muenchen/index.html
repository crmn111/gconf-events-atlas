About

Munich's trade-fair campus in Messestadt Riem. The operator's locations pages describe 18 barrier-free, column-free halls with about 200,000 m² of indoor exhibition space and 414,000 m² of open-air ground, plus the on-site ICM congress centre for plenary sessions of up to 6,000 people. Atlas-listed trade fairs Automatica, productronica and LASER World of PHOTONICS are held here.
