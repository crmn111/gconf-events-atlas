About

Official Serviceplan Group House of Communication Munich campus in the Werksviertel, listed in the atlas as the venue for DLD Munich. Official English locations page: Friedenstrasse 24, 81671 Munich. Official Munich office page: pedestrian access via Friedenstraße 24; car access via August-Everding-Straße 25 to the on-site garage. No official event-capacity figures were published on the retrieved pages, so those fields are null. Coordinates already stored for this named campus are retained.
