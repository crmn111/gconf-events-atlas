About

Official Deutsche Hotelklassifizierung 5-star hotel (Hotelstars Union Munich 5-star list). Official address Maximilianstrasse 17, Munich; official hotel geo on the official site 48.1389, 11.5815. Official Ballroom Maximilian up to 600 guests (stored as capacity_max_delegates) and 510 m² / 500 theatre. The site’s “five-star luxury hotel” wording is a self-description; the stored star_rating is the official Hotelstars listing, not that marketing line. Hotelstars Superior, if used, is not a sixth star.
