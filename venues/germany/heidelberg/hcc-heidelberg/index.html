About

Purpose-built congress centre next to Heidelberg Hauptbahnhof, run by Heidelberger Kultur- und Kongressgesellschaft. Official facts give 6,633 m² of dialogue space, 10 halls or rooms and 3,800 seats in total, with a 14-metre-high Hall 1 for up to 1,800 people (1,693 in the published theatre plan).
