About

Stuttgart's airport-adjacent exhibition and congress campus, operated by Landesmesse Stuttgart. Official company facts give 120,000 m² of exhibition space across the hall mix above, including the ICS International Congress Center. No atlas-listed conference or expo was linked to this venue in the current unique event set, so it is included as one of Germany's largest remaining centres.
