About

One of Germany's larger purpose-built convention centres, on the former Bundestag campus beside the United Nations organisations in Bonn. The official site states events for 200 to 7,000 people, a total building capacity of 7,000, a 1,230-seat plenary hall and a 3,730-person New York Hall. No atlas-listed event is currently linked; included as a major remaining congress venue.
