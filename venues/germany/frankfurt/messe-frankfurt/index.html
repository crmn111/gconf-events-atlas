On-site congress and event buildings include the Congress Center (15,000 m² over four levels, nine congress halls) and Kap Europa (7,700 m² over four levels, 14 conference rooms), plus the historic Festhalle (Hall 2, 5,600 m²) and Halls 1 and 3–12.

About

Frankfurt's inner-city exhibition grounds, among the largest worldwide. A 24 June 2026 Messe Frankfurt press briefing puts hall area at around 392,000 m², with more than 64,000 m² of open space, and names the Congress Center and Kap Europa as the two congress centres on the campus. The atlas lists Cloud & AI Infrastructure Frankfurt in Hall 3.
