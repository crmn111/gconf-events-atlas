About

The Kingdom’s principal exhibition and convention venue, opened in 2022. Official BTEA (Bahrain Tourism and Exhibitions Authority) page locates it in Sakhir. Official ewbahrain.com contact page gives the street address Road 62, Block 1062, Hawrat Ingah, Kingdom of Bahrain. Official Exhibition Halls Fact Sheet: 10 halls; each single hall is 8,880 m² with banquet layout 2,570; combined double halls are 17,760 m² with banquet 5,150 (stored as capacity_max_delegates; no theatre column is published). The same official fact sheet also states a combined area of 95,000 square meters of exhibition space, while the ten individual hall areas sum to 88,800 m² — because those two official figures disagree, exhibition_sqm is left null and both are noted. An official Google embed on the contact page is a wide (~14 km) named view of Exhibition World Bahrain and is not stored as a building-level pin. The BTEA footer address in Sanabis and a Google pin for the older Bahrain International Exhibition & Convention Centre at 26.2298608, 50.5398291 are the authority headquarters / former centre, not this Sakhir venue, and are not used.
