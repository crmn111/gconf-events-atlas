About

Seoul convention and exhibition centre in Gangnam, recorded as one venue for all COEX hall variants in the atlas (Halls A–E references, Grand Ballroom and Hall C 3F). Official indoor exhibition total for Halls A–D is 36,007 sqm; THE PLATZ is an additional official 2,224 sqm event hall and is listed as a space, not added into that A–D total. capacity_max_delegates is 7,000, the official Hall D people figure. The official address 513 Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu, Seoul 06164 matches the atlas venue string. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
