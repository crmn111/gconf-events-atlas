About

Korea International Exhibition Center in Goyang, recorded as one campus covering Exhibition Center 1 (Halls 1–5) and Exhibition Center 2 (Halls 6–10), which official pages describe as connected by a moving walkway. exhibition_sqm is 108,011, the official indoor hall total; the official 2,849 sqm outdoor area is excluded. A campus people-capacity headline was not published on the overview tables captured here, so capacity_max_delegates is null. Official convention enquiry address (department mailbox). The about-page footer also prints a named staff mailbox, which is not used. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for KINTEX Exhibition Center 1; Center 2 events in the atlas use a nearby but distinct pin and are still linked to this single campus record.
