About

Cotai Expo, the official exhibition centre at The Venetian Macao, officially addressed as Estrada da Baía de N. Senhora da Esperança, s/n, Taipa, Macao SAR, with public email. The current official venue page states “approximately 71,000 square metres” of exhibition space; the official MICE factsheet states 75,000 sq.m. Neither figure is stored as exhibition_sqm (approximate wording on the current page, and a disagreement with a possibly older factsheet). Coordinates are the named Nominatim hotel pin for The Venetian Macao (no distinct Cotai Expo hall pin was returned). The sports-only Guia Circuit atlas event is not linked. Leftover national exhibition centre, included as requested.
