About

Provincial convention centre in the former Mitre railway station, officially at Perú 511, Santiago del Estero, with public email and access also described from Avenida Roca. The official venue site states a covered event surface of 4,000 m² (stored as exhibition_sqm) and says the principal hall exceeds 2,500 m² (that “exceeds” figure is not stored as an exact area) and can gather 3,000 people seated, also published as “up to 3,000 chairs with a stage” (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Used by Smart City Expo Santiago del Estero; the organiser’s official venue page repeats Perú 511, G4200, access via Av. Roca. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s events-centre pin for Fórum.
