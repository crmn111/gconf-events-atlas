About

Buenos Aires exhibition grounds officially at Sarmiento 2704, C1425, with public email. The official 2026 commercial presentation states 45,000 m² of covered/built area (stored as exhibition_sqm) plus 10,000 m² of outdoor/green area, which is not included in that total, on a 12-hectare site with 1,000 underground parking spaces. The same PDF gives the Convention Center (main room and annex rooms) a capacity of 2,000 attendees, stored as capacity_max_delegates; larger pavilion audience figures were not stored from extractable official text in this pass. Used by Feria del Libro Buenos Aires and arteBA. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for the named grounds; a Nominatim house pin on Avenida Presidente Sarmiento 2704 in Palermo is nearby but was not used.
