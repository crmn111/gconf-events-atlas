About

Buenos Aires exhibition centre on the former Costa Salguero site, officially at Av. Costanera Rafael Obligado 1221, with sales email. The official 19 June 2026 launch page states 20,500 m² of surface, six pavilions, five auditorium rooms and about 1,300 parking spaces; 20,500 is stored as exhibition_sqm with the caveat that the current homepage also markets “+20,000 m² cubiertos”. Used by Argentina Comic Con 2026. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for the named grounds; a Nominatim house pin at street number 1221 is nearby but was not used.
