About

Official Rainbow Tourism Group page for the Harare International Conference Centre (also written Harare International Convention Centre, HICC) at the Rainbow Towers Hotel & International Conference Centre, 1 Pennefather Avenue, Harare. Official facilities: a multi-functional 4,500-seater auditorium (stored as capacity_max_delegates) and ‘Exhibition space of 2000 square metres’ (stored as exhibition_sqm). Official table heading ‘HICC (Total capacity 4500 delegates)’ agrees with the 4,500-seater wording. A separate table heading ‘Hotel (Total Capacity 10 000 delegates)’ is the hotel section of the same chart and is not used as the HICC campus maximum. Incomplete official table rows (including Jacaranda 1 & 2 & 3, several committee rooms and foyers) are not stored because not all layout columns were published for those rows.
