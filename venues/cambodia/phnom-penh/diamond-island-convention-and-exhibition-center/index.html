About

Official OCIC project page: Diamond Island Convention and Exhibition Center (DICE) was established in October 2009 and is part of Canadia Integrated Enterprise Group. The official page identifies it as including the largest international convention and exhibition facilities on Diamond Island but does not publish a hall-by-hall capacity or area schedule in the fetched text, so capacity and exhibition fields are null. Unofficial organiser floorplans and press figures were not used.
