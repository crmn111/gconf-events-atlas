About

Official Punta Cana International Convention Center in Bávaro, Punta Cana, within walking distance of the Hard Rock Hotel. Official figures: 6,083 m² for congresses, conventions and exhibitions (stored as exhibition_sqm) and meeting space for up to 3,500 participants (stored as capacity_max_delegates). The 3,500-person figure attached to Hard Rock Hotel rooms on the same page is the hotel, not this centre, and was not used. No official street address was published on the retrieved home/facilities copy, so address and coordinates are null.
