About

Central Oslo offices, meeting rooms and conference venue at Universitetsgata 2, beside St. Olavs plass. Official copy says one hall holds up to 350 people and parallel sessions can reach 800 participants in total, stored as capacity_max_delegates. Official space figures include Rebel Expo up to 350, SKRAA up to 200 seated, Rebel Hangout up to 345 across two floors, and Rebel Roof up to 120.
