About

Kilden performing arts centre (Kilden Theatre and Concert Hall) in Kristiansand, officially at 2 Sjølystveien. It is one of the named sites for Techpoint, which is also listed at Kunstsilo and Knuden; those other sites are not recorded as separate venues in this pass and no combined venue was invented.
