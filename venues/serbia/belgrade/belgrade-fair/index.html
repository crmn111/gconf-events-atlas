About

Official Belgrade exhibition complex at Bulevar vojvode Mišića 14, 11040 Belgrade, with public email. Official hall pages give exhibiting areas hall by hall (stored in spaces). The official 2025 corporate presentation’s “approx. 100,000 m²” indoor hall total and “approx. 35,000 m²” open space are not stored as exhibition_sqm. Coordinates are the named exhibition-centre Nominatim pin, matching the atlas precise pin. Linked: GITEX AI Belgrade, Belgrade Book Fair, DSC Europe and Belgrade Tango Encuentro (the last also uses Dorćol Platz as a separate additional venue, which is not merged into this record).
