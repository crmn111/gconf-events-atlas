About

Official Porto Montenegro historic auditorium in Tivat, added as a principal existing indoor conference/event space after Sea Dance 2027 was left TBC and the planned Podgorica congress centre is not yet open. Official venues page and campus address Blaza Jovanovica No 1, Tivat 85320;. Official copy says up to 400 guests, while the specifications table lists reception 560; neither is stored as an exact capacity_max_delegates. Coordinates are null: Nominatim returned only a marina-wide pin, which was not used.
