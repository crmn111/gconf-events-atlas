About

Valencia's main trade-fair complex. Current official and Comunitat Valenciana tourism / economic-impact materials describe more than 230,000 m² of event space, 19 pavilions, 48 rooms, three auditoriums and events for up to 20,000 people. The official address is Avenida de les Fires, s/n, 46035, and is published. A 2015 Foro Centro PDF is archived but states 152,600 m² and 12 pavilions; those figures are treated as superseded. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for the named Fira València complex.
