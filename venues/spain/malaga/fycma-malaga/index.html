About

Málaga's official trade-fair and congress palace. Current official pages describe 60,000 m² of facilities, 19,500 m² of exhibition space (17,000 m² in two pavilions), events for up to 20,000 guests, and auditoriums of 900 and 600 seats. The published enquiry address is info@fycma.com. An official 2018 brochure is archived; headline totals follow the current website because room counts in the older PDF may be dated.
