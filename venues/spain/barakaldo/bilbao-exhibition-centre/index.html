About

The Basque Country's main exhibition and events centre, in Barakaldo. Official about-us pages state 150,000 m², six halls, Bizkaia Arena for up to 20,000 people and BEC Convenciones of 18,000 m². The official address is Ronda de Azkue 1, 48902 Barakaldo; bec@bec.eu and convenciones@bec.eu are published. A 2021 official dossier is archived; it uses older hall-by-hall figures (including four halls of 15,000 m², one of 20,000 m² and an Arena of 26,000 m²) that do not sum to the current 150,000 m² about-us total, so headline figures follow the current official pages. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for the named Bilbao Exhibition Centre.
