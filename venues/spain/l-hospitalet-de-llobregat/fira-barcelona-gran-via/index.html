About

Barcelona's main exhibition precinct at Gran Via, in L'Hospitalet de Llobregat. Current official Fira Barcelona pages state 240,000 m², eight halls, and a Convention Centre in Hall 8 seating 3,000 to 12,000. Official GPS published by Fira is 41.35448, 2.12698, and infofira@firabarcelona.com is the published information email. An official January 2022 venue overview PDF is archived; it uses older totals (198,500 m²) and different Hall 8 figures, so headline numbers follow the current website. The atlas event smart-city-expo-2027 is linked here because the organiser's practical-information page places Smart City Expo World Congress in Gran Via Halls 2 and 3; that page titles a 2027 edition but mixes 2026 dates, which is noted in NOTES_ES.md.
