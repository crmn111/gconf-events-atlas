About

International convention centre in Barcelona's Diagonal Mar area, managed with Fira Barcelona. Official Fira venue pages describe 100,000 m² of floorspace. Coordinates and the Passeig del Taulat 11-14 address come from the Nominatim named-building pin for the Centre de Convencions Internacional de Barcelona. Hall-by-hall capacities were not stored in this pass.
