About

Fira Barcelona's historic Montjuïc exhibition site. Official Fira venue pages state 153,000 m² and a Palau de Congressos auditorium seating more than 1,600. The official address is Avinguda de la Reina Maria Cristina, s/n, 08004 Barcelona. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for the named Fira de Montjuïc complex.
