About

Classic Paseo de la Castellana conference hotel. The official Comunidad de Madrid tourist-accommodation register lists it as a 5-star hotel (categoria 5-HOTEL) at Paseo de la Castellana 49, 28046 Madrid. The official hotel fact sheet describes 16 meeting rooms and the Plata ballroom of 608 m² (table 608.8 m²), theatre 600, cocktail 630 and banquet 600, divisible into Albéniz and Granados. Coordinates are left null: a Nominatim query for the official street and postcode returned only a street-level pin on a different stretch of Paseo de la Castellana, which was not used.
