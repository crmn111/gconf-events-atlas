About

Madrid's main trade-fair and congress estate (Recinto Ferial). Official IFEMA pages describe 240,000 m², 13 halls, 85 rooms and two convention centres, with a North auditorium of 1,100 seats and a South auditorium of 600. The published contact address is Avenida del Partenón, 5, 28042 Madrid.
