About

Riga’s official international exhibition centre at Kipsalas str 8, LV-1048, with public administrator email (published as info(at)rigaexpo.lv). Official rooms page: the complex includes two pavilions of 5,700 m² and 9,200 m² (stored as exhibition_sqm 14,900, the sum of those two official pavilion figures) and Hall II capacity 6,500 persons (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Conference Room 1 is 250 m² / 150 persons and Conference Room 3 is 680 m² / 350 persons; the same official page also says “two fixed conference rooms for 220 and 500 persons,” which is noted and not stored. Coordinates are the named exhibition-centre Nominatim pin, matching the atlas precise pin. Linked atlas events: Unicon Winter, Skola and Riga Comm 2027. The official pages sit on a dated RE2017 path (copyright 2016); figures are taken from the still-served official rooms page.
