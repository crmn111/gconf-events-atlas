About

Gaborone International Convention Centre (GICC) at The Grand Palm Resort. Official contact page gives the physical address Plot 17989 Bonnington Farm, Molepolole Road, and also 'The Grand Palm Hotel Casino Convention Resort, Cnr Bonnington Farm and Molepolole Road, Gaborone, Botswana'. Banqueting email. Official Tsodilo Suite table: 1,550 m², cinema 1,807 (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Cinema columns are stored as capacity_theatre with a note. Star icons on some hall names are not treated as a property-level hotel classification for the convention centre, so star_rating is null. An official 'DOWNLOAD FACT SHEET' link did not retrieve a PDF in this pass; the Peermont group brochure was retrieved but is not used to override the venue-page table.
