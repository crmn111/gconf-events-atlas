About

Victoria Island exhibition and events centre officially at Plot 3 & 4 Water Corporation Road, Victoria Island Annex, Lagos, with public email. Official floor plans: Hall ABC 2,230 m², theatre 2,500 / banquet 1,500; Hall 2 1,452 m², theatre/conference 1,500 / banquet 850. The two official hall floor areas sum to 3,682 m², stored as exhibition_sqm (mezzanine figures are noted and not added). The largest published theatre figure is 2,500 for Hall ABC, stored as capacity_max_delegates. Used by GITEX Nigeria and as the main-event hall for Lagos Tech Fest (the Civic Centre day is a separate named venue and is not combined here). Lagos Comic Con is linked as last officially at this centre. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
