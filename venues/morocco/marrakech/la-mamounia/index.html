About

Historic palace hotel officially at Avenue Bad Jdid, 40040 Marrakech-Medina, with public email. The official 2025 French e-brochure (retrieved 2026-11-24) states that the Grand Salon has a superficie of 450 m², can be divided into three 150 m² spaces, and 'peut accueillir jusqu’à 300' people (stored as capacity_max_delegates with an official 'up to' note). A further salon of 80 m² 'peuvent recevoir jusqu’à 40'. No official Moroccan star listing was retrieved, so star_rating is null. Coordinates are the previously stored official/atlas pin for the named hotel. Used by 1-54 Marrakech.
