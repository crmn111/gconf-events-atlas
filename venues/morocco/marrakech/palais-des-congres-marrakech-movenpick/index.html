About

Hotel-attached congress complex officially on Boulevard Mohamed VI, 40000 Marrakech, with reservations email. Official corporate-events page: 5,600 m² of multipurpose space, including the Ministers’ Auditorium (1,700 m² / theatre 1,489), the Ambassadors’ Auditorium (500 m² / theatre 432) and the Royal Ballroom (1,800 m² / theatre 2,000, stored as capacity_max_delegates). The Marrakech International Film Festival is listed at the Palais des Congrès and cinemas across the city, so the Palais is linked as one named venue only. GITEX Africa’s official plan-your-trip page names Place Bab Jdid rather than this hotel campus, so GITEX is not linked. No official Moroccan star listing was retrieved; star_rating is null. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin used for the Palais des Congrès; Nominatim did not return a named hotel pin in this pass.
