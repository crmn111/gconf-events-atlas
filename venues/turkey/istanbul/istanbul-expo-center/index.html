About

Yeşilköy exhibition centre officially at Yeşilköy Mah. Atatürk Cad. Hol 1 Blok No:5/2, İç Kapı No:1, Bakırköy, 34149 Istanbul, with public email. The official May 2025 technical information PDF states a total indoor area of 96,000 m², stored as exhibition_sqm, and a total space of 304,000 m² which is not stored. Official hall indoor areas include Hall 1 10,800; 2 9,250; 3 10,800; 4 11,200; 5 9,250; 6 8,450; 7 5,500; 8 8,450; 9 5,400; 10 5,400; 11 5,000 m², plus foyers. Used by WIN Eurasia and GITEX AI Türkiye. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s exhibition-centre pin.
