About

Istanbul exhibition and congress campus officially at Cumhuriyet Mahallesi, Eski Hadımköy Yolu Caddesi 9/1, 34500 Büyükçekmece. Official venue page: total exhibition area of 145,000 sqm with 120,000 sqm indoor (stored as exhibition_sqm) and 25,000 sqm outdoor, which is excluded; the same page also states a net exhibition capacity of 62,000 sqm plus a 10,000 sqm congress centre and 8,000 sqm common area. Meetings and conventions for up to 10,000 people are stored as capacity_max_delegates. Used by Automechanika Istanbul, Turkeybuild Istanbul and the Istanbul Book Fair. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin; Nominatim did not return a named pin in this pass.
