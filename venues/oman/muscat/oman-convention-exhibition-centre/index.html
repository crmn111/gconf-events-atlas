About

Muscat convention and exhibition centre officially on Sultan Qaboos Street, Al Athaiba, in the Madinat Al Irfan precinct, with public email. Official halls and about pages: 22,396 sqm of column-free indoor exhibition space (stored as exhibition_sqm); Halls 1 and 2 combined can seat 10,000 delegates in theatre style (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Official space-inclusions PDF: Halls 1 and 5 4,880 m² (theatre 3,744); Halls 2, 3 and 4 4,212 m² (theatre 3,648). COMEX and GEDEX are linked as named at OCEC; the atlas pins for those events are imprecise Muscat city coordinates and were not used, so venue coordinates are left null pending a named-venue geocode.
