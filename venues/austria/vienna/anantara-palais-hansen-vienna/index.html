About

Official WKO Hotelsterne directory (stand 17.08.2026 on the fetched page) lists Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel at Schottenring 24, 1010 Wien as a 5 Sterne Superior hotel. Superior is an add-on within the five-star category, not a sixth star. Official meetings page max capacity 265; Theophil Ballroom 277.45 sq.m., theatre 265, classroom 160, banquet 220, cocktail 265. Official factsheet says 'up to 265 guests combined' for Theophil I and II.
