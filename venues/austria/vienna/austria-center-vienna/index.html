About

Austria's largest congress centre, included as a remaining major centre (no atlas event in this country file is hosted here). capacity_max_delegates is 23,122, the official non-public maximum; the official public figure is 21,162. exhibition_sqm is 26,000 from the official trade-fair page. The official about page wording '56,000 m² of exhibition space' is superseded for this field by the more specific official location/trade-fair distinction between 56,000 sqm flexible total space on five levels and up to 26,000 sqm combined exhibition-hall space. Official contacts office@acv.at and sales@acv.at; sales@acv.at is stored as the public sales address. Official address Bruno-Kreisky-Platz 1, 1220 Vienna is confirmed by the Nominatim conference-centre pin used for coordinates.
