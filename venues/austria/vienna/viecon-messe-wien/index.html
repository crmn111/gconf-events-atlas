About

Vienna congress and exhibition centre, recorded as one venue for all atlas strings that name Messe Wien, VIECON Messe Wien or individual halls C and D. Official exhibition headline is up to 55,000 sqm gross hall area; the published hall areas sum to 55,156 sqm and are listed in spaces rather than averaged into a third figure. capacity_max_delegates is 29,000, the official congress-participant figure. The stored address Messeplatz 1, 1020 Vienna follows the atlas venue string. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Buch Wien, BEST Vienna, Ferien-Messe Wien and Wohnen Interieur / Bauen Exterieur are linked here.
