About

Salzburg exhibition centre at the official imprint address Am Messezentrum 1, 5020 Salzburg, included as a remaining major Austrian centre. exhibition_sqm is 39,257 from the official halls table. A campus delegate headline for the halls themselves was not used (the official 4,900-seat salzburgarena figure is an arena, not the exhibition-hall total), so capacity_max_delegates is null. Official contact office@mzs.at. Coordinates are the Nominatim exhibition-centre pin.
