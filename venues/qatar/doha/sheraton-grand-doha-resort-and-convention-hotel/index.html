About

Official Marriott events schema: Al Dafna Convention Center maximumAttendeeCapacity 3,000 and 36,167 sq ft (3,360 m²). Other official schema spaces include Al Majlis Conference Hall (900 / 11,840.4 sq ft), Salwa Banquet Hall (1,000 / 10,898.5 sq ft) and Al Rayyan Conference Hall (300 / 5,425.1 sq ft). An official Qatar Tourism factsheet (also used as a source) describes 25 meeting rooms; layout-by-layout theatre figures were not extractable from that PDF in this pass (image-heavy). The factsheet path sits under a '5-star-hotels' folder on the tourism-board site; that folder name is not treated as a property-level official classification, so star_rating is null.
