About

Doha’s exhibition and convention centre officially described as being in West Bay. Official organizers page: arranged over 47,700 m², with a pillar-free exhibition space of 29,035 m², stored as exhibition_sqm, divisible into five halls of 5,368 m² to 7,160 m², 96 m wide, 302 m long and 18 m high, plus a 9,000 m² concourse/foyer. The 47,700 m² figure is a broader arranged area and is not stored as exhibition space. Official copy also refers to gala dinners for up to 7,500 guests, stored as capacity_max_delegates. A complete official street address was not published on the retrieved pages. The atlas Web Summit pin is not marked precise and Nominatim returned no named-venue pin, so coordinates are left null.
