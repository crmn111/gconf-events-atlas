About

Qatar’s national convention centre in Education City, Ar-Rayyan, Doha (official postal P.O. Box 34195). Official exhibition halls page: nine halls providing 40,000 m² of indoor exhibition space, stored as exhibition_sqm, of which Halls 3–9 offer 35,000 m² and Halls 1–2 offer 4,100 m² (those two figures sum to 39,100 m²). A separate official 3,000 m² outdoor space is not included. The venue overview also describes “40,000 m² of indoor and outdoor column-free exhibition space” and a total venue area of 200,000 m² (the latter is not stored as exhibition area). Official Conference Hall page: up to 4,000 delegates, stored as capacity_max_delegates; the venue overview states a Conference Hall for 3,800 delegates — the dedicated hall page is preferred, with the 3,800 figure noted. Official theatre 2,300 seats; 52 meeting rooms for 20 to 150 seats.
