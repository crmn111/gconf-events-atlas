About

Kuala Lumpur convention and exhibition centre officially at No.41, Jalan Tun Ismail, 50480 Kuala Lumpur. Official venues page: Dewan Merdeka spans over 2,000 m² (17.7 m height) and accommodates up to 3,000 guests, stored as capacity_max_delegates; Tun Dr. Ismail 1,003 m² / 1,000 guests, divisible into 500 m² halls; Tun Hussein Onn 1,003 m² / 1,000 guests, similarly divisible; Tun Razak 3 is a large-scale exhibition hall spanning over 4,500 m² (3.5 m ceiling) for up to 3,000 guests; Tun Razak 4 spans over 2,700 m² (8.08 m ceiling) for up to 2,500 guests; Mawar Hall banquet up to 500. The “over 2,000 / 4,500 / 2,700 m²” figures are not stored as exact exhibition_sqm, and a complete official indoor exhibition total was not published on the retrieved pages. Used by the Kuala Lumpur International Book Fair, Malaysia Technology Expo and the Malaysia Art Ecosystem Fair (Dewan Tun Razak 3 & 4). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s named commercial pin.
