About

Purpose-built convention and exhibition centre beside the PETRONAS Twin Towers in Kuala Lumpur City Centre, with public email info@klccconventioncentre.com. Official about page: more than 38,670 sqm of flexible event space (not stored as exhibition_sqm); Plenary Hall approximately 2,991 delegates; Large Hall up to 4,500 attendees, stored as capacity_max_delegates. Official technical specifications (gross floor area): Hall 1 2,502 m², Hall 2 2,332 m², Hall 3 560 m², Hall 4 2,071 m², Hall 5 2,245 m², Halls 6, 7 and 8 each 2,828 m², which sum to 18,194 m², stored as exhibition_sqm. A complete official street address was not copied from the retrieved pages. The atlas Comic Fiesta pin is not marked precise, and the Nominatim result for “Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre” resolved to a different building in Bangsar, which was discarded, so coordinates are left null.
