About

Malaysia’s international trade and exhibition centre in KL Metropolis, officially at Kompleks MITEC, No.8, Jalan Dutamas 2, 50480 Kuala Lumpur, with public email info@mitec.com.my. Official homepage and about page: 52,000 sqm and 11 exhibition halls, stored as exhibition_sqm, including Malaysia’s largest pillar-less hall of 12,960 sqm on a single level. Official venues page: Level 3 pillarless halls can be combined for a theatre setup of up to 18,000 pax (stored as capacity_max_delegates) or banquet 6,700; Level 1 and Level 2 are published per hall as theatre up to 4,000 / banquet 1,500 / up to 200 booths of 3 × 3 m. Used by SEMICON Southeast Asia, Smart Nation Expo, CyberDSA, Infocomm Asia, APAIE and Nijigen Expo. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s exhibition-centre pin.
