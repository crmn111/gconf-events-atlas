About

San Isidro convention hotel officially at Calle Las Begonias 450, San Isidro, Lima. Official events page: 2,835 sq m of total event space and functions from 10 to nearly 1,600 guests; the official FAQ names 18 event rooms and gives the Limatambo Ballroom a capacity of 1,600 (stored as capacity_max_delegates). A marketing line also mentions “over 2,500 meters” of event space and 19 venues; the exact 2,835 sq m and 18-room FAQ figures are preferred, and neither event-space total is stored as exhibition_sqm. Used by the Data & AI Summit Peru. No official Peruvian star listing was retrieved, so star_rating is null. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s hotel pin.
