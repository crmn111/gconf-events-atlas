About

National convention centre in San Borja, included as the largest remaining purpose-built centre in Peru without a current atlas event in this file. An official PROINVERSIÓN announcement on gob.pe describes more than 15,000 m² of infrastructure, 18 multifunctional rooms on eight levels, and capacity for nearly 10,000 people simultaneously; because both headline figures are approximate, exhibition_sqm and capacity_max_delegates are left null. The street address Avenida De la Arqueología 206 is the named-venue Nominatim pin for Centro de Convenciones de Lima and matches official government location descriptions of the San Borja campus. A standalone venue website was not confirmed in this pass.
