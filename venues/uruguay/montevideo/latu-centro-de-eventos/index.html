About

Events centre at LATU’s Parque de Innovación, officially at Avda. Italia 6201, Montevideo, with public email. Official page: halls of 500 m² and 1,700 m² (sum 2,200 m² stored as exhibition_sqm), usable banquet for up to 1,400 people and conference format for up to 1,700 (stored as capacity_max_delegates), plus exclusive parking for 250 cars. Used by IEEE DemoCon. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin; a Nominatim school pin at the same avenue number was discarded.
