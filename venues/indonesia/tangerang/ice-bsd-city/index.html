About

Purpose-built exhibition and convention centre in BSD City, officially at Jl. BSD Grand Boulevard Raya No.1, BSD City, Tangerang, 15339, with sales email. Official about and exhibition-hall pages: 10 halls with a total indoor area of 50,000 sqm, stored as exhibition_sqm; an additional 50,000 sqm of outdoor exhibition space is excluded. A typical hall is officially 4,860 m² (54 m × 90 m × 12 m), with a concert layout of 4,750 seats and an exhibition layout of 320 stands. No safe named-venue Nominatim pin was returned for the official street address, so coordinates are left null. Included as the largest remaining national centre without a current atlas event in this country file.
