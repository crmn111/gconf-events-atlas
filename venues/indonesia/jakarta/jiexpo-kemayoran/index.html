About

Jakarta exhibition campus at Kemayoran, officially at the Trade Mart Building (Gedung Pusat Niaga), Arena JIEXPO Kemayoran, Jakarta Pusat 10620. Official about page: 50,000 m² indoor exhibition space, stored as exhibition_sqm; outdoor open space of 40,000 m² is excluded. Official venues page hall floor areas: Hall A 8,295; D1 4,752; D2 4,608; B 3,994; C 3,888; B3 5,150; C3 4,800 sqm. Used by Djakarta Warehouse Project, Art Jakarta and Data Center Indonesia. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for the expo grounds; a Nominatim office pin slightly to the north was not used.
