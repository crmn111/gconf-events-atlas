About

Sofia exhibition and congress centre officially at 147 Tsarigradsko shose blvd, Sofia 1784. Official about page: six exhibition halls totalling 13,600 sq.m (stored as exhibition_sqm) and eight congress halls with capacity for up to 1,000 people simultaneously (stored as capacity_max_delegates). The same page’s 42,000 sq.m “exhibition space” figure includes outdoor area and is not stored. Used by Aniventure Comic Con. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s exhibition-centre pin (an official page also prints N 42.649206 / E 23.395404, and the contact header has a 23.394504 variant that was not used).
