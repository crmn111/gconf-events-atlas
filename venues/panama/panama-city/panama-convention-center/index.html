About

Convention centre on the Amador Causeway, officially at Calle General Juan D. Perón, with public email. The current official homepage and the introductory text of the official factsheet state a 15,400 m² exhibition hall (stored as exhibition_sqm); the same factsheet’s capacity table lists the Salón de Exhibiciones Canal as 15,434 m² (the 34 m² difference is noted; current homepage 15,400 is preferred). Official factsheet theatre capacity for the exhibition hall is 14,500 (stored as capacity_max_delegates), with classroom 8,000, banquet 7,600 and reception 13,000. The banquet hall is officially 4,000 m² / 2,000 banquet on the homepage. Used by Expo Tech Panama. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin; Nominatim returned only bus-stop pins, which were discarded.
