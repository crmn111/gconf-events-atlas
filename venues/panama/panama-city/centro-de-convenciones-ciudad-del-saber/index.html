About

Convention centre on the Ciudad del Saber campus. Official commercial page: an adaptable auditorium of 465 m² with capacity for up to 500 people (stored as capacity_max_delegates), plus smaller meeting rooms. Used by KCD Panama; other atlas events listed only as “Ciudad del Saber” (jazz festival, Macrofest, Futuro AI) are campus-wide or multi-space and are not automatically linked. Coordinates are Nominatim’s conference-centre pin for the named Centro de Convenciones (not the imprecise campus pin used by those other events).
