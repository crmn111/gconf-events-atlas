About

Monaco’s official congress and cultural centre at 10 Av. Princesse Grace, 98000 Monaco. Official homepage: 85,000 m² total surface and 41,000 m² of modular indoor space; the spaces page also calls this “modular exhibition spaces.” Because official planner wording also describes 41,000 m² of versatile surface, including 12,000 m² of exhibition and catering space, 41,000 is not stored as exhibition_sqm (nor is the mixed 12,000 exhibition-and-catering figure). Official Ravel Hall theatre capacity is 2,650 (stored as capacity_max_delegates), larger than Salle des Princes at 1,864. Coordinates are the named events-venue Nominatim pin for Grimaldi Forum; the atlas FundForum pin is imprecise and coincides with a bus-stop/bike-rental pin, so it was not used. Linked: FundForum International 2027.
