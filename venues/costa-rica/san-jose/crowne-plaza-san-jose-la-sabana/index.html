About

Atlas-hosting hotel for ALTEC 2027, listed by the official Costa Rica Tourism Board as Crowne Plaza Corobicí (the same La Sabana property) at Mata Redonda, north side of Parque La Sabana, 10108 San José, with 14 meeting rooms and banquet facilities accommodating up to 1,000 people (stored as capacity_max_delegates). The official IHG hotel pages returned HTTP 403 on retrieval, so room-by-room figures were not taken from IHG. Coordinates are the atlas precise hotel pin. No official Costa Rican hotel-classification listing was retrieved, so star_rating is null.
