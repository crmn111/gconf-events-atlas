About

Costa Rica’s national convention centre, officially addressed on the March 2025 capacity-chart footer as General Cañas Highway, San José, Costa Rica, with public email. Official rooms page: the Talamanca, Central and Guanacaste halls are the largest areas, with capacity for 5,800 people (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Official chart: Talamanca Main Hall 3,739.42 m², theatre 4,000. No official campus-wide exhibition total is published, so exhibition_sqm is left null rather than summing the halls. Nominatim did not return a named convention-centre pin (only a bus stop and unrelated buildings), so coordinates are null. Leftover national centre, not an atlas-hosting venue in this pass.
