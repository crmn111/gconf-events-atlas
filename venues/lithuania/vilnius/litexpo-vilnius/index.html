About

Vilnius exhibition and congress centre officially at Laisvės pr. 5, 04215 Vilnius, with public emails and. Official premises page hall areas: Hall 1 2,300 m² / theatre 1,600; Hall 2 720 / 350; Hall 3 2,600 / 1,800; Hall 4 2,670 m² (parts A 770, B 1,040, C 460); Hall 5 3,850 m², with a published 4,500 figure for the combined hall (stored as capacity_max_delegates) and 3,060 in the compact theatre column. The five official exhibition halls sum to 12,140 m², stored as exhibition_sqm. Used by the Vilnius Book Fair, Comic Con Baltics, LOGIN, ArtVilnius and Studijos. Karjera. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s LITEXPO pin; bus-stop pins were discarded.
