About

Dubai World Central airshow site, named by the official Dubai Airshow organiser (Informa Markets) as “DWC, Dubai Airshow Site” for Dubai Airshow 2027 (15–19 November 2027). Official FAQ: the show is located at DWC, Airshow Site, with Park & Ride from Al Maktoum Airport. No official headline exhibition-area or delegate-capacity figures were published on the retrieved organiser home, FAQ or contact pages, so those fields are null.
