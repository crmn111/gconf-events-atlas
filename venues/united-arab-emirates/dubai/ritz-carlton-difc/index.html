About

Luxury hotel in Dubai International Financial Centre with a large official events offer, published as the venue for SuperReturn Middle East 2027. Official events pages state a largest-space capacity of 1,513 in the Samaya Ballroom, 15 event rooms and 3,078 m² of total event space (the same page also quotes 2,625 m² of conference and catering space, which is noted as a discrepancy). The ballroom is officially described as about 15,070 sq ft and divisible by four. An official Dubai DET star classification was not confirmed in a public register in this pass, so star_rating is null. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for the named Ritz-Carlton and Limestone House building in DIFC (the atlas event pin was not precise).
