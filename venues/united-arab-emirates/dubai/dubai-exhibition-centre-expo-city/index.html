About

Purpose-built exhibition centre at Expo City Dubai, managed with Dubai World Trade Centre. Official January 2026 North and South hall factsheets state 110,000 m² of flexible permanent event space, 21 halls, and capacity for more than 70,000 people (the 70,000 figure is stored as the published maximum). North halls total 46,749 m² (up to 30,600 guests) and South halls 62,818 m² (up to 44,100 guests). An older February 2025 Expo City venue booklet cited about 58,000 m² and 32,000 people; those figures are treated as superseded by the January 2026 sheets. The factsheets publish. Shared-venue atlas events with DWTC are also linked here. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. A street address was not copied from an official contact page in this pass.
