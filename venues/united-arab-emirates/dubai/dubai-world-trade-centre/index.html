About

Dubai's main exhibition and convention complex on Sheikh Zayed Road. Official DWTC host pages state 145,000 m² of flexible indoor event space across 21 halls. Official 2026 hall factsheets give Halls 1–8 more than 31,000 m² (individual halls 1,800–4,752 m², plus The Pavilion 3,427 m²), Za’abeel Halls 1–3 15,254 m² and Halls 4–6 15,552 m², Sheikh Saeed Halls 1–3 combined 15,081 m², and the Trade Centre Arena 9,100 m² (14 m high). Events that the atlas lists at both DWTC and Dubai Exhibition Centre (GITEX Global 2026, Gulfood 2027 and World Health Expo Dubai 2027) are linked here and on the Expo City record. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
