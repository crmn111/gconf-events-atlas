About

Festival Arena at Dubai Festival City, operated with InterContinental / IHG Hotels at Dubai Festival City, published as the venue for Blockchain Life 2026. Official operator page: 9,000 maximum capacity (stored as capacity_max_delegates) and 7,200 sq.m of pillarless customisable event space (noted; not stored as exhibition_sqm because it is labelled event space, not exhibition-only). Meetings email. Official address is given as Dubai Festival City (PO Box 45777 on the contact block). Coordinates are left null: the atlas pin was imprecise, and Nominatim returned only the InterContinental hotel pin, not a distinct Arena pin.
