About

Palm Jumeirah hotel and conference centre (official address Crescent Road, The Palm, Dubai). Official MICE brochure capacity chart: Diamond Ballroom 1,018.80 m², 1,200 reception and 1,173 theatre, divisible into three sections. The brochure's earlier narrative rounds this to approximately 1,000 m² and a seating capacity of approximately 660 (matching the chart's banquet figure). Official property schema on the hotel site lists 795 rooms; the brochure lists 760 — both official figures are noted. No public Dubai DET hotel-level classification was confirmed, so star_rating is null.
