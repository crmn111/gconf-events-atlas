About

Jumeirah's Madinat resort in Dubai, which includes a substantial official conference and events centre and is the published venue for TOKEN2049 Dubai 2027 and Art Dubai 2027. Official events pages describe the Joharah Ballroom as 1,880 m², divisible into seven event spaces, with a lagoon-view terrace and a grand pre-function space for up to 1,700 guests (that 1,700 figure is the official pre-function capacity, not a separately labelled theatre total for the ballroom). An official Dubai DET classification listing was not confirmed in the public register in this pass, so star_rating is null. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. A street address was not copied from the official contact page in this pass.
