About

Abu Dhabi Corniche hotel and conference centre in the Etihad Towers complex (official fact-sheet address West Corniche, P.O. Box 111929). Official Hilton events page and fact sheet: 13 meeting rooms and a 1,920 m² Mezzoon Ballroom. The prose on both official sources says the ballroom accommodates up to 1,000 guests; the official fact-sheet capacity chart also lists 2,000 reception and 1,400 theatre for the same room. Headline capacity follows the official chart (2,000 reception), with the 1,000-guest prose figure noted. No public DCT hotel-level classification was confirmed, so star_rating is null.
