About

Abu Dhabi Corniche palace hotel (official press-kit address West Corniche Road, P.O. Box 39999). Official Mandarin Oriental press kit: more than 7,000 m² of meeting and conference space, an auditorium of 3,000 m² for 1,100 seated guests, and a ballroom for up to 2,400 guests, plus more than 40 meeting rooms. No public Abu Dhabi DCT hotel-level classification was confirmed, so star_rating is null.
