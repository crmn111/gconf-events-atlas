About

Outdoor events space outside The Galleria on Al Maryah Island. Official Galleria page (managed by Aldar): 107 Hamouda Bin Ali Al Dhaheri Street, Al Maryah Island, Abu Dhabi;. The official ADGM events listing names The Grand Steps, Al Maryah Island, Abu Dhabi as the venue for ADFW 2026 (7–10 December 2026), matching the atlas event Abu Dhabi Finance Week 2026. No official capacity figures are published. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for The Grand Steps; Nominatim returned a The Galleria mall pin nearby, which was not substituted.
