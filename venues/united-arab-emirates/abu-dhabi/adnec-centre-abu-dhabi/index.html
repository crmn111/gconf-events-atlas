About

Abu Dhabi's main exhibition and convention complex. Official space pages list 55,000 m² of exhibition and event space, 12 halls ranging from 3,167 to 7,919 m², the International Convention Centre (ICC) of 7,919 m² seating up to 6,000, and the 10,000 m² Marina Hall on the waterfront. A campus-wide total of 153,678 m² is also published, but that figure includes concourse, plaza, grandstand and marina-waterfront areas and is not stored as indoor exhibition_sqm. Conference Halls A (1,125 m²) and B (741 m²) combine for 1,600 people. The Abu Dhabi International Boat Show is linked here because the atlas places it in Marina Hall at this centre. Official contact pages give Khaleej Al Arabi Street and. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for the main centre.
