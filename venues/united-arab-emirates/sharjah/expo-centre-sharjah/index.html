About

Sharjah's official exhibition centre, operated with the Sharjah Chamber of Commerce. Official organise pages state 42,000 m² of exhibition space indoor and outdoor, made up of six 4,000 m² indoor halls (with interconnection options) and 6,000 m² of tiled outdoor exhibition space. Indoor exhibition_sqm is stored as 24,000. The published enquiry address is. Official pages give a P.O. Box rather than a street address, so address is left null.
