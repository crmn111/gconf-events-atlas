About

Downtown convention and exhibition centre at 1 Raffles Boulevard. Official pages state 42,000 m² of flexible event space and a capacity to host 10 to 10,000 guests. Level 4 is given as 12,000 m² of multi-purpose hall / exhibition space, which is stored as exhibition_sqm; Level 6 can be converted into 10,000 m² of column-free exhibition space and also houses an auditorium seating more than 4,200 plus an adjacent 850-seat auditorium. The spaces page also states two Level 6 auditoriums with a total capacity of 6,850 seats, which does not equal 4,200 plus 850; both official figures are noted. Headline capacity_max_delegates follows the official 10,000-guest figure. Coordinates are a Nominatim result for the named building at the official address (bus-stop pins were not used).
