About

Convention centre in the Raffles City complex, presented on the official Fairmont Singapore meetings page (the centre also serves Swissôtel The Stamford in the same complex). Official figures include more than 108,000 square feet of function space, 34 meeting rooms and three ballrooms. The Fairmont Ballroom is listed at 2,257 m² and 3,000 theatre / 1,465 classroom / 1,800 banquet. Headline capacity_max_delegates is that official ballroom theatre figure. Singapore has no official government hotel star rating, so star_rating is null. Coordinates are a Nominatim result for Fairmont Singapore at the official 80 Bras Basah Road address.
