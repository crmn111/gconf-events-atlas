About

Singapore’s largest hotel-integrated exhibition and convention venue, in the Marina Bay Sands complex. The official exhibition-halls page gives 31,750 m² of exhibition space (Level 1 14,560 m² and Basement 2 17,190 m²), 9.45 m hall height, and capacity for up to 2,000 booths and 45,000 guests. exhibition_sqm is that official exhibition-hall total, not a combined figure for all meeting rooms and ballrooms. Atlas events at Marina Bay Sands, Marina Bay Sands Convention Centre and Sands Expo are linked here as one complex. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Singapore has no official government hotel star rating (Hotels Licensing Board classes hotels by room count, not stars), so star_rating is null.
