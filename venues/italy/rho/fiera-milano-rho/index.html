About

Italy's largest exhibition precinct, in Rho on the edge of Milan. Fondazione Fiera Milano official materials state 345,000 m² of indoor exhibition space plus 60,000 m² outdoors, in 20 halls across eight Fuksas pavilions. An investor presentation repeats the 345,000 m² indoor figure; one brochure also states 325,676 m², which is noted as a discrepancy, and the 345,000 official Fondazione figure is stored.
