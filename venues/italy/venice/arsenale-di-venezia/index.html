About

Historic Venetian Arsenale, operated for events by VELA S.p.A. (Comune di Venezia / AVM group). Official VELA events page and public email. The official Salone Nautico Venezia organiser site names the historic Arsenale as the venue for the 26–30 May 2027 edition, matching the atlas Venice Boat Show 2027. Official “approximately 1,000 sqm” wording is not stored as an exact exhibition_sqm. VELA’s legal address at Isola Nova del Tronchetto is the company seat, not the Arsenale, and is not stored as the venue address. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin published with the boat show.
