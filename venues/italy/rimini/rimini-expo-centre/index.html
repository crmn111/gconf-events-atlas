About

Italian Exhibition Group's Rimini exhibition centre. Official IEG page body text states 197,300 m² of usable area, of which 137,300 m² is exhibition space, and mentions a temporary 8,300 m² addition from November 2024. Some widgets on the same site show 189,000 / 129,000; the body-text 137,300 exhibition figure is stored, with the discrepancy noted. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for the named Fiera di Rimini. A full official street address was not copied in this pass.
