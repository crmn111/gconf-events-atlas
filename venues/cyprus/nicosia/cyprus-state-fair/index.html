About

Named Nicosia exhibition grounds (Kratiki Ekthesi / ExpoCyprus) hosting Cyprus Comic Con 2027. The official organiser location and 2027 event pages give the address as 6 Georgiou Seferi Street, Egkomi, 2415 Nicosia, Cyprus. No official State Fairs Authority website or hall-area figures were confirmed on a live official page in this pass (csfa.org.cy was not confirmed live), so exhibition_sqm and capacity_max_delegates are null. Coordinates are the named Nominatim pin for Κρατική Έκθεση Κύπρου, matching the atlas precise pin. The Warehouse, Limassol, and Kolla, Limassol, are named atlas venues with precise pins but were left for a later pass (no official venue pages retrieved). Filoxenia Conference Centre was not added: its reported official site fcc.com.cy did not respond.
