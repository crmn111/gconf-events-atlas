About

Mozambique’s national conference centre in Maputo, officially built by the Government of Mozambique through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Cooperation and inaugurated in June 2003. Official address Av. Marginal 4441, Maputo-Moçambique. Official Sala Multiuso cinema capacity 2,000 (stored as capacity_max_delegates) and 1,590 m². Official Hall de Exposição area 680 m² (stored as exhibition_sqm). Official Sala Plenária auditorium capacity 750 / 725 m². No official map pin was published on the retrieved pages; lat/lon left null.
