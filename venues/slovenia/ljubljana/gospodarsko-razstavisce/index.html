About

Ljubljana Exhibition and Convention Centre (Gospodarsko razstavišče), officially at Dunajska cesta 18, 1000 Ljubljana, with public email info@gr.si. Official halls page: 23 multipurpose halls with a total of 12,323 m² of indoor space, stored as exhibition_sqm, and 8,844 m² of outdoor areas, which are not included. Official about page also cites 21,162 m² total event space (indoor plus outdoor; 12,323 + 8,844 = 21,167 m², a 5 m² rounding difference) and 2,500 seats in the largest hall, stored as capacity_max_delegates, matching official Kocka Hall A2 (2,456 m² / 2,500). Used by the Slovenian Book Fair and Na meji nevidnega. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s events-centre pin.
