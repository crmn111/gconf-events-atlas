About

Lisbon hotel facing Eduardo VII Park (official press-kit address Rua Rodrigo da Fonseca, 88, 1099-039 Lisbon). Official ballroom page: Pedro Leitão Ballroom 456 m², maximum occupancy 750 (reception 750 / theatre 450 / banquet rounds 360). Official press facts give a meeting-room total of 1,838 m² and a banquet / meet-and-feed capacity of 320, which differs from the ballroom page's 360 banquet figure; headline capacity follows the official ballroom max occupancy of 750. No public Turismo de Portugal RNET confirmation was recorded, so star_rating is null.
