About

Lisbon's international exhibition park in Parque das Nações. Official pavilion pages state four multifunctional pavilions of 10,000 m² each (40,000 m² in total) inside a complex of more than 100,000 m². Each pavilion is about 142 m by 72 m; Pavilion 1 is 15 m high and Pavilions 2–4 are 12 m high. Official floor plans for the site and each pavilion are stored. The official address is Rua do Bojador, Parque das Nações, 1998-010 Lisbon, and is the published venue enquiry address (shared with the separately recorded Lisbon Congress Centre, which is in Alcântara). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
