About

Historic multi-use pavilion in Lisbon, published as the venue for Building the Future 2027. Official Visit Lisboa venues page describes a multipurpose hall of about 1,900 m² and several smaller official rooms (atrium 180 m², two foyers 280 m², Salão Nobre 180 m², Sala dos Azulejos 123 m²). The “cerca de 1,900 m²” wording is not stored as exact exhibition_sqm. No official street address was published on the retrieved information page, so address is null. Coordinates are the named exhibition-centre Nominatim pin, matching the atlas precise pin.
