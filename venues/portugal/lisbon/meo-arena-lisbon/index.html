About

Lisbon's main indoor arena in Parque das Nações, published as the venue for Web Summit 2027. Official MEO Arena pages describe the Atlântico room as a 5,200 m² arena with capacity for 12,500 people seated, the Tagus Room as 2,200 m², a Business Centre with a 100-seat auditorium and 11 rooms, and the Arena Box as 2,000 m² for 500 to 4,000 people. The official address is Rossio dos Olivais, Lote 2.13.01A, 1990-231 Lisbon. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
