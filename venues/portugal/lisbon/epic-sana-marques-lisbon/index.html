About

Five-star conference hotel at Av. Fontes Pereira de Melo, 8, officially classed as 5 stars on the Turismo de Lisboa / Visit Lisboa venue listing (retrieved 2026-11-24). Official hotel schema gives the same street address, postcode 1069-310 and coordinates 38.72735, -9.14836. The official meetings page and business portfolio describe a 2,142 m² conference and event centre; the portfolio labels Floor -1 cocktail capacity as 1,800 pax and the meetings page states 'Max Capacity: 1,800 people' (stored as capacity_max_delegates). The same official portfolio also refers to a capacity of up to 1,500 participants and lists Gala Ballroom I+II cocktail at 1,500 — both figures are noted on the spaces. RNET 1519 appears on the hotel site as a registration number, not as a star class by itself. 2,142 m² is the official event-centre area, not stored as exhibition_sqm.
