About

Lisbon's purpose-built congress centre in Alcântara, distinct from FIL in Parque das Nações though both are operated under Lisbon Venues. Official pages state 29,000 m², with 5 pavilions, 8 auditoriums, 6 foyers and 34 meeting rooms, plus a restaurant for 400 people. The official address is Praça das Indústrias 1, 1300-307 Lisbon, and is the published enquiry address. The 29,000 m² figure is a total floor area for the centre, not stored as indoor exhibition_sqm alone. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
