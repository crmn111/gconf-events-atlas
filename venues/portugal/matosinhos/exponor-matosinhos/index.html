About

The main exhibition venue for the Porto area, in Leça da Palmeira, Matosinhos. Official homepage wording describes 180,000 square metres of space as the largest event venue in Portugal; that figure is treated as a campus / venue-space total (the site also has gardens and about 2,250 parking spaces) and is not stored as indoor exhibition_sqm. Official organiser pages confirm a 945-seat auditorium. The official address is Avenida Dr. António Macedo, 4454-515 Leça da Palmeira, Matosinhos, and info@exponor.pt is the published general enquiry address. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
