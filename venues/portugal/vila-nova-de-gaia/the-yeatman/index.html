About

Hotel and conference venue in Vila Nova de Gaia overlooking Porto and the Douro (official address Rua do Choupelo, Santa Marinha, 4400-088). Official events page: the combined Alentejo, Bairrada and Madeira rooms form the largest conference space, maximum 350 people. Porto + Douro combine to 165 m² / 180 people on the webpage (PDF 167 m² / 180). Official events contact: events@theyeatman.com. Room areas on the current webpage and the official MICE fact sheet differ in several cases; spaces below use the current official webpage, with PDF figures in the notes. No public RNET confirmation was recorded, so star_rating is null.
