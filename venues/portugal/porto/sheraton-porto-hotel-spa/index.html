About

Hotel in Porto with a substantial official meetings offer, published as the venue for Linux Storage Summit 2027. Official Marriott events pages list 8 event rooms, 1,630 m² of event space and a largest-space capacity of 850 in the Apollo room (the same page also mentions 1,750 m² of meeting rooms, which is noted as a discrepancy). The official capacity chart gives Apollo 10,172 sq ft (about 945 m²), theatre 850, classroom 530, reception 830 and banquet 630. An official Turismo de Portugal RNET star classification was not confirmed in this pass, so star_rating is null. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
