About

Porto's main historic congress centre in the former customs house on the Douro. Official spaces pages describe the Archive Hall as 1,200 m², the East and West Ground Floor as two symmetrical 2,000 m² spaces, Infante Hall as 394 m², and the Alfândega Porto Auditorium as a contemporary amphitheatre with more than 300 seats. No atlas conference is linked yet; it is included as the largest remaining purpose-built congress venue in Porto alongside Exponor. Coordinates are the Nominatim pin for the named Alfândega do Porto building (Cais da Alfândega, 4050-388). A parking-lot POI labelled for the congress centre was not used.
