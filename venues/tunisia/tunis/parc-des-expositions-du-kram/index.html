About

Official Parc des Expositions du Kram (Foire Internationale de Tunis), described on its official site as Tunisia’s largest exhibition centre. Official address Le Kram, Tunis;. Official three-hall total 23,000 m² stored as exhibition_sqm; outdoor 15,000 m² excluded. Official largest named conference room 400 seats (stored as capacity_max_delegates). The homepage’s 40,000 m² covered-exhibition figure is noted and not stored. Coordinates are null (no named Nominatim pin in this pass). No atlas events are linked.
