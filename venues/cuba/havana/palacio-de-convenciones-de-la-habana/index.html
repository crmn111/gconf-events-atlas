About

Official Gobierno de La Habana page: the Palacio de Convenciones is on calle 146 entre 11 y 13, reparto Siboney, La Habana, about 8 km from the city centre, on the banks of the Quibú river. The official page says the main plenary hall has capacity for 'alrededor de 1500 personas' and that 11 other rooms hold 'desde 70 hasta 400 personas'. Those figures are approximate or ranges and are not stored as exact capacity_max_delegates. The previously official operator domain cpalco.com was fetched in this pass and returned unrelated gambling-spam content, so it is not used as website or source.
