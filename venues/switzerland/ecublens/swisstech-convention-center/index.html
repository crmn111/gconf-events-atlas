About

EPFL campus convention centre on the official contact address Quartier Nord EPFL, Route Louis-Favre 2, 1024 Ecublens (the municipality on the official site; the atlas event city is Lausanne). Official current pages give a maximum of 3,000 people in the combined Auditorium ABC, which is stored as capacity_max_delegates. Official Garden-level open exhibition space is 3,460 sqm and an auditorium/foyer exhibition layout of 3,000 sqm is also published; no single current official indoor-exhibition campus total was taken from the venue site, so exhibition_sqm is null (an older May 2021 official brochure and third-party association factsheets quote other totals and are not used for the headline). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches the Nominatim conference-centre pin. Official contact is a form and named staff mailboxes only, so contact_email is null. AMLD is linked here.
