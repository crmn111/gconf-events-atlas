About

Davos congress centre, host of the World Economic Forum Annual Meeting since 1971 according to the official history on the same page. Official total capacity is 5,000 people across 34 rooms and 12,000 sqm of room space in three buildings. The 12,000 sqm official figure is total room area, not labelled as indoor exhibition area, so exhibition_sqm is null. The largest official room capacity is 1,800. Official contact email. The stored street address follows the atlas venue string Talstrasse 49A; coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
