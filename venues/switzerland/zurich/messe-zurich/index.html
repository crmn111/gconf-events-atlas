About

Zurich exhibition centre at Wallisellenstrasse 49 in Oerlikon. Official exhibition total is 30,000 sqm across seven halls; the published individual hall areas sum to 26,961 sqm and are listed in spaces rather than averaged into a third headline. capacity_max_delegates is the official Arena / plenary figure of 15,000; the official 2,000-person plenum is noted. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches the Nominatim exhibition-centre pin. Zurich Pop Con is linked here.
