About

Industrial event and convention hall on the former ABB site near Zurich Oerlikon, with official address Elias-Canetti-Strasse 146, 8050 Zürich. Official April 2026 venue documentation states setups for up to 2,000 people, stored as capacity_max_delegates. No single official indoor exhibition-hall total is labelled, so exhibition_sqm is null; the official 817 sqm open hall area is recorded as a space. Official enquiry address info@stage-one.ch.
