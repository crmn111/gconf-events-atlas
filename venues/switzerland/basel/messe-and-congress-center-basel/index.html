About

Basel exhibition and congress campus operated by MCH, recorded as one venue because the official site presents the halls and Congress Center Basel as a single connected complex. The official 141,000 sqm figure is worded as exhibition and event space, not a confirmed indoor-exhibition-only total, so exhibition_sqm is left null rather than storing that combined figure. Current official venue-page hall floors (preferred over the older venue-overview PDF where they differ): Hall 1.0 20,000, 1.1 32,000, 1.2 14,500; Event Hall 3,300 (and 2,600 also listed); Hall 2.0 14,000, 2.1 15,000, 2.2 15,500; Hall 4.U 4,100, 4.0 3,000, 4.1 4,000 sqm. The official PDF lists Hall 1.0 as 20,250, 1.1 as 31,300 and 1.2 as 14,700 sqm. capacity_max_delegates is the official main-hall figure of 10,000; the official 5,000-participant congress and 3,000-person Event Hall/plenary figures are noted. Official contact addresses include Messeplatz 10, 4005 Basel and Congress Center Basel at Messeplatz 21, 4058 Basel; the stored address follows Messeplatz 10, which is also the atlas venue string. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Igeho, Fantasy Basel and Liste Art Fair are linked here.
