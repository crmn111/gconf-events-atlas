About

Bern exhibition and events campus. The official 150,000 sqm marketing total includes outdoor area, so exhibition_sqm is null rather than storing a combined indoor-plus-outdoor figure. capacity_max_delegates is 9,000, the official capacity published on the STAGE multifunctional hall card. Official contact address is BERNEXPO AG, Mingerstrasse 6, Postfach, CH-3000 Bern 22 (street and the official postal designation are both retained). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Official enquiry address. Swissdidac Bern is linked here.
