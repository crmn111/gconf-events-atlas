About

Geneva international exhibition and congress centre next to the airport. Official indoor exhibition area is 106,000 sqm in seven combinable halls (all pillar-free except Hall 3). Official organiser page states conference-room capacities from 200 to 2,500 people, and up to 10,000 in custom-designed spaces; capacity_max_delegates follows that 10,000 custom-space figure. Hall areas on the official halls page: Hall 1 15,500; Hall 2 (and offices) 16,500; Hall 3 (and rooms) 1,500–2,250; Hall 4 15,500; Hall 5 15,500; Hall 6 27,000; Hall 7 13,000 sqm. The atlas events Art Genève, the Geneva book fair and Watches and Wonders are linked here; Watches and Wonders also uses official In The City sites across Geneva, which are not recorded as a single invented venue. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Official enquiry address.
