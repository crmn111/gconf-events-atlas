About

Historic Brussels events site whose official Sheds pages describe a converted events venue for exhibitions, fairs and shows, officially at Avenue du Port 86C, 1000 Brussels (deliveries Rue Picard 3). Official dimensions: Shed 1 3,787 m² / 3,500 people, stored as capacity_max_delegates; Shed 1 Bis 1,568 m²; Shed 2 2,740 m² / 3,000 people; Shed 2 Bis is published as 1,188 m³ (cubic metres), so it is not added into exhibition area. Exhibition_sqm 8,095 is the sum of the three official floor areas (Shed 1, 1 Bis and 2).
