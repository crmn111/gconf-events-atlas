About

Ghent exhibition and events centre officially at Maaltekouter 1, 9051 Ghent. Official halls page: eight halls and 54,000 m² of space, stored as exhibition_sqm (also described as “around 54,000 m² of event space”). Official indoor hall areas: Hall 1 19,152 m² (9,000 theatre, 9,000–20,000 reception/party, stored as capacity_max_delegates 20,000); Halls 2–7 each 4,032 m² / 3,500 persons / 2,688 theatre; Hall 8 10,735 m² / 10,000 persons / 8,000 theatre. Those eight halls sum to 54,079 m², consistent with the official 54,000 headline. Official outdoor areas (PA4 5,600, Hall 3 outdoor 1,500, Hall 8 outdoor 5,000, PA1 10,000 m²) are not included in exhibition_sqm.
