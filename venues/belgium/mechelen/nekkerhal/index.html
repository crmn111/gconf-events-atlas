About

Mechelen exhibition and events hall officially at Plattebeekstraat 1, 2800 Mechelen, also styled Nekkerhal Brussels-North. Official halls page: total 18,454 m² (60 × 300 m, height 4–13 m), stored as exhibition_sqm, divisible into six sections (5,673; 2,157; 1,729; 864; 2,160 and 5,871 m²) with seven access gates. A published maximum delegate figure was not taken from the official pages. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for SETT Mechelen.
