About

Centrul Internațional de Expoziții „MOLDEXPO” S.A., Moldova’s official international exhibition centre. Official moldexpo.md footer: C.I.E. MOLDEXPO S.A., MD-2008, Chisinau, str. Ghioceilor, 1, Republica Moldova. The official Public Property Agency (app.gov.md) listing confirms the same name, address mun. Chișinău, str. Ghioceilor, 1. No official hall-area total or delegate capacity was published on the retrieved pages, so exhibition_sqm and capacity_max_delegates are left null. A Google pin on the APP page is for the Agency office at str. Vasile Alecsandri 78, not Moldexpo, and is not used.
