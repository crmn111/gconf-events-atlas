Halls
Official SEC Centre page: five interconnected exhibition halls with a combined area of over 22,000 m². Published hall floor areas: Hall 1 775 m²; Hall 2 2,315 m²; Hall 3 5,095 m²; Hall 4 10,065 m² (theatre 10,000 / reception 12,000); Hall 5 4,105 m². Those five hall areas sum to 22,355 m², which is stored as exhibition_sqm. Additional rooms include the Lomond Auditorium (490 m², 624 theatre) and suites such as Alsh, Boisdale, Carron and the SEC Meeting Academy. The wider Scottish Event Campus also includes the Armadillo and OVO Hydro, which are not given separate records here. No official simultaneous campus-wide delegate total is stored; Hall 4's 12,000 reception figure is the largest published single-hall capacity.

About

The exhibition halls of Glasgow's Scottish Event Campus (formerly the SECC), on the Clyde. Official specifications give five interconnected halls totalling just over 22,000 m², with Hall 4 alone published at 10,065 m². No atlas-listed conference or expo is currently linked; included as one of the UK's largest remaining exhibition centres (it already appears on the atlas research venue-watch list).
