About

The National Exhibition Centre east of Birmingham, which the operator calls the UK's largest event venue. Official organiser pages state 18 halls, 32 conference suites and 186,000 m² of covered space. The atlas lists Smart Manufacturing Week here. A full street address/postcode was not copied from an official contact page in this pass, so the address is the official venue name only.
