About

Birmingham's International Convention Centre on Centenary Square, a short walk from New Street Station. The official find-us page gives the address as 8 Centenary Square, B1 2EA. The atlas lists the European Robotics Forum and Digifest here. Estate-wide capacity figures were not taken from unofficial directories.
