Official campus site describes four venues on the waterfront: Exhibition Centre (flexible halls for exhibitions and trade shows), Convention Centre (purpose-built conference spaces), M&S Bank Arena (published as up to 11,000-capacity flexible space for conferences, exhibitions and live entertainment) and The Auditorium (tiered theatre-style space). About

Liverpool's waterfront conference, exhibition and arena campus at King's Dock, now branded Liverpool Experience Campus (the former ACC Liverpool site; accliverpool.com currently presents this campus). The official location page gives King's Dock, Port of Liverpool, Kings Dock St, Liverpool L3 4FP. The largest official capacity published on the homepage is the M&S Bank Arena at up to 11,000. No atlas-listed event is currently linked; included as a major remaining UK convention campus.
