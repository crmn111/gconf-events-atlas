About

Large South Bank conference hotel opposite the Houses of Parliament. Official current meetings pages: pillarless ballroom described as 1,200 m², spaces for 8 to 1,400 people (stored as capacity_max_delegates), 32 rooms and 3,400 m² of meeting space (combined meeting-space total, not exhibition_sqm). The June 2025 official factsheet’s 1,166.90 m² and 1,500 theatre/reception figures are noted and not stored because they disagree with the current official webpages. star_rating null (no official UK property-level listing retrieved).
