About

Westminster conference and exhibition centre opposite Westminster Abbey. The official about page states 32 rooms and a self-contained floor for up to 1,300 people, and gives the Broad Sanctuary address. The atlas lists NDC London and QCon London here. capacity_max_delegates records the official largest-floor figure, not a verified simultaneous whole-building total.
