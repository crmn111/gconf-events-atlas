Halls
Heritage halls: official conference pages state that the Grand Hall and National Hall together provide 20,000 m²+ for large congresses. Individual hall pages (not summed into exhibition_sqm because a single official estate total was not published): Grand Hall 14,000 m² / up to 7,000; National Hall 8,730 m² / up to 5,000; West Hall ground 3,400 m² / up to 2,250, with West Hall also published at 2,400 seated when used with the ICC. New International Convention Centre at Olympia: main auditorium 800+ theatre seats, secondary auditorium 400, 2,700 m² Central Hall, seven breakout and seminar rooms, business lounges. Henley Suite: eight meeting rooms from 8 to 144 delegates.

About

Historic exhibition halls in West Kensington, now expanded with a new International Convention Centre, West Hall and on-site hotels. Official organiser pages describe the Grand and National Halls as more than 20,000 m² of heritage exhibition space, alongside the ICC's 800-plus-seat auditorium. Atlas events including London Tech Week, TechEx, Big Data LDN and SuperAI are listed here. No single official estate-wide delegate or square-metre total is stored.
