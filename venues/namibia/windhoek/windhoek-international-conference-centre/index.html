About

Windhoek International Conference Centre (WICC), officially described as Namibia’s largest conference centre, at the corner of Auas and Aviation Road, Windhoek. Official 2025 factsheet and venue page: Grand Ballroom 1,565 m². Official prose: seating for up to 2,400 delegates in theatre style, 1,800 conference, 1,200 banqueting. The official MAXIMUM SEATING CAPACITIES table on the same factsheet lists Grand Ballroom theatre 2,000, conference 1,700, banquet 1,200 and cocktail 3,000. Because the theatre and conference figures disagree, those two layouts are not stored as exact fields on the Grand Ballroom; banquet 1,200 (agreed) and table cocktail 3,000 are stored, and 3,000 is used as capacity_max_delegates with the 'up to 2,400' theatre wording noted. Contact published with the centre includes. No official exhibition-hall total is published, so exhibition_sqm is null. The smaller historic Windhoek Country Club / Oryx rooms are a different, smaller venue and are not used here.
