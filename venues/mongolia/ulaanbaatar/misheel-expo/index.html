About

Official exhibition venue in Ulaanbaatar. Official contact copy gives the address as Misheel Expo, Sükhbaatar district (Сүхбаатар дүүрэг), Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, email. Official English/Mongolian copy refers to a 2,511 m² facility with indoor and outdoor spaces and uses 'орчимд' (approximately) in the Mongolian text, so 2,511 is not stored as exact exhibition_sqm. A '1,200+' figure on the official site refers to exhibiting companies, not delegate capacity, and is not stored as capacity_max_delegates. Both capacity and exhibition area are therefore left null pending a hall-by-hall official schedule.
