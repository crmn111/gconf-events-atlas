About

Thailand’s main exhibition and convention campus at Muang Thong Thani, Pak Kret, Nonthaburi, officially described as comprising IMPACT Arena, IMPACT Exhibition and Convention Center, IMPACT Challenger, IMPACT Forum Hall 4 and The Portal Lifestyle Complex. Official Exhibition Center page: eight multi-purpose halls with a combined 47,000 square metres (Halls 5–8 are pillar-free at 5,000 sqm each / 20,000 sqm; Halls 9–12 encompass 27,000 sqm). Official Challenger page: three interconnected column-free halls totalling 60,000 square metres (131.2 × 459 × 25.7 m). Exhibition_sqm 107,000 is the sum of those two official exhibition-hall totals; Forum Hall 4 and the Arena are additional and are not included. Official contact address: 10th Fl., Bangkok Land Building, 47/569-576 Popular 3 Road, Banmai, Pakkred, Nonthaburi 11120. A published campus delegate maximum was not taken from the retrieved official pages, and contact emails were obfuscated. Used by THAIFEX – Anuga Asia and by events in Challenger Hall. Coordinates are Nominatim’s exhibition-centre pin (station and stop pins of the same name were discarded).
