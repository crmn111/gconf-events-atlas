About

Bangkok’s national convention centre officially at 60 Queen Sirikit National Convention Center, Ratchadaphisek Road, Khlong Toei, Bangkok 10110, with public email. Official venue-space pages: eight event halls, four plenary halls, four ballrooms and 50 meeting rooms, within 78,500 sq.m of event space (that broader event-space figure is not stored as exhibition_sqm; the glance page also describes a 300,000 square-metre total space). Official Level G: Exhibition Halls 1–4 total 22,335 sq.m (theatre 22,494). Official Level LG: Exhibition Halls 5–8 total 22,750 sq.m (theatre 21,251). Exhibition_sqm 45,085 is the sum of those two official combined hall totals. Capacity_max_delegates 22,494 is the official Halls 1–4 theatre figure, the largest published hall capacity. Foyers are listed separately and are not included in exhibition_sqm. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s conference-centre pin.
