About

Official 2025 brochure and official BCC webpage: Level 22 Convention Centre 5,250 m², theatre 5,000, classroom 2,500, banquet 3,800, cocktail 7,000. Official webpage also states a 'total capacity of 5,000 persons' and, in another sentence, a 'maximum capacity of 7,000 persons' (matching official cocktail). capacity_max_delegates stores the official 5,000-person / theatre figure; the 7,000 cocktail figure is in spaces[]. No official Thai property-level star listing was retrieved, so star_rating is null.
