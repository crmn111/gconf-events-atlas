About

Bangkok exhibition centre officially at 88 Debaratna Road (km. 1), Bangna Tai, Bangna, Bangkok 10260. Official venue and plan pages name exhibition halls EH98–EH104. Official homepage also describes BITEC Live as 7,000 sq.m. of concert hall with 10,000 standing or 6,500 seated attendees; that entertainment hall is not stored as exhibition_sqm. Animated official counters for column-free exhibition space rendered as zero in the retrieved HTML, and a confirmed indoor exhibition campus total was not copied, so exhibition_sqm and capacity_max_delegates are left null. Used by Manufacturing Expo and Together Festival (EH 103). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
