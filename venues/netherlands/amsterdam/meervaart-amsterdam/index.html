About

Theatre and meetings venue in Amsterdam Nieuw-West. The official 2026 meetings and events information pack lists the Rode zaal for 800 people (523 in the stalls and 277 on the balcony) and the Blauwe zaal for 265 people, plus ten meeting rooms. The venue publishes as its events contact.
