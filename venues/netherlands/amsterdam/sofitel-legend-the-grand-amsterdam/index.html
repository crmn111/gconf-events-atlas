About

Canal-side luxury hotel with a substantial meetings offer. The official Dutch Hotelstars register last classified it on 28 April 2025 as Superior and describes five-star luxury, with 19 meeting and event spaces. Official Sofitel meetings pages publish a largest capacity of 320 guests in the Council Chamber. The official 2025 hotel brochure is freely linked but exceeds the 25 MB download limit, so only the URL is recorded.
