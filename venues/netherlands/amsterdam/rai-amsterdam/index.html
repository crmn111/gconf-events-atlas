About

Amsterdam's main exhibition and conference complex on Europaplein. Official venue pages put event space at 116,200 m² across 12 halls, with three conference centres, two auditoriums and a ballroom. Official hall factsheets give Hall 1 15,000 m² (plus a 3,240 m² balcony), Hall 3 3,920 m² and Hall 12 9,035 m². The 2025 annual report is kept as a facilities source; it also cites 70 rooms where the complex page says 61, so the room count is recorded with that caveat.
