About

Five-star Superior hotel with a dedicated events floor. The official Dutch Hotelstars register (hotelsterren.nl) last classified the hotel on 14 October 2024 as Superior and notes it was the first hotel in the Netherlands to receive the European Five-Star Superior shield. Official meetings pages and the same register describe 2,700 m² and 20 rooms for events of up to 2,500 guests. The Grand Ballroom is published at 902 m², with theatre 1,015, banquet 650 and standing 1,600.
