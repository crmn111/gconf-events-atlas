About

Utrecht's exhibition and events complex next to the central station. The official 2025 Royal Jaarbeurs brochure describes 10 halls totalling up to 86,000 m². Hall 12 is given as 11,383 m² with a theatre-style capacity of about 10,357–10,360. Some hall figures conflict across brochure pages (notably Hall 8), so only clearly consistent spaces are stored.
