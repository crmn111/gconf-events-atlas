About

Regional exhibition and events venue in Houten, near Utrecht. The official 2026 event brochure states 10,000 m² across four halls plus ten meeting rooms. The combined Wilgenzaal is published at theatre-style capacity of 350. The venue's own pages describe events from 8 to 2,000 people.
