About

Purpose-built congress centre in The Hague. Official venue and The Hague convention-bureau pages describe up to 5,000 guests, a 2,161-seat auditorium and 12,000 m² of exhibition space. The official address is Churchillplein 10, 2517 JW, and is published as a venue contact.
