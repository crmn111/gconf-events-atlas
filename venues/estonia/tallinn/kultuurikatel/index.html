About

Tallinn Creative Hub (Kultuurikatel), used by Latitude59, Nordic Tech Days and Baltic Fintech Days. Official 2025 rooms capacity PDF: Black Box Hall 537 m² / theatre 600 / gala 320 / reception 900, stored as capacity_max_delegates; Cauldron Hall 780 m² / theatre 250 / reception 700; Small Hall 175 m² / theatre 220; Stalker Hall 163 m² / theatre 150; Atrium 551 m² / reception 500. A complete official street address was not copied from the retrieved pages (the contact page returned 404). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s events-venue pin at Põhja pst 27a (that street number is from Nominatim, not stored as the official address).
