About

Waterfront convention centre at Canada Place. Official exhibition total is 311,500 ft² (28,940 m²); exhibition_sqm uses the official metric figure 28,940 rather than a freshly converted 28,939. Official West Exhibition Halls A, B & C theatre and reception are both 14,933, which is used as capacity_max_delegates. East halls are smaller (official East Exhibition Hall A theatre 2,250). Atlas strings ‘Vancouver Convention Centre’ and ‘Vancouver Convention Centre West’ are the same campus and are linked here. Official address: 1055 Canada Place, Vancouver, BC V6C 0C3. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Canada no longer has a current national official hotel star scheme (Canada Select was discontinued); this is not a hotel, so star_rating is null. Competitor-platform PDFs were not used.
