About

Montréal’s downtown convention centre. Official floor-plans PDF: exhibition venues 36,863 m²; capacity_max_delegates is the official 13,863-person figure for rooms 210–220–230 (18,493 m²). The 47,265 m² rental-space total and 32,798 m² meeting-room total on the same official PDF are not used as exhibition_sqm. Public email on the official site is. A public street address for the hall entrance was not clearly separated, in the captured official text, from a 9th-floor office-style schema address on rue Saint-Antoine Ouest, so the stored address is the city only rather than an unverified office floor. Coordinates are the Nominatim named events-venue pin for Palais des congrès de Montréal (OSM places it on Avenue Viger Ouest; that street label is not stored as the official address because it was not taken from the official site).
