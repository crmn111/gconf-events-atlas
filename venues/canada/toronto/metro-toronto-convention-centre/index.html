About

Toronto’s downtown convention centre on two connected buildings. Official exhibit total is 442,000 square feet; exhibition_sqm is 41,063 using 1 sq ft = 0.09290304 m². capacity_max_delegates is the official Theatre seating figure of 1,232 from the 2025 At a Glance PDF; official exhibit-hall people capacities were not taken from the short At a Glance sheet alone. Public email on the official exhibit-halls page is info@mtccc.com. PDAC 2027 is officially/atlas-named at both MTCC (222 Bremner Blvd) and Fairmont Royal York, so the event is linked here and also to the Fairmont record. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
