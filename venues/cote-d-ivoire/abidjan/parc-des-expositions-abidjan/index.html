About

Official Parc des Expositions d'Abidjan spaces page: the park covers 16 hectares. Exhibition hall 6,500 m² (exact), 17 m clear height, divisible into two or three. Le Dôme Convention Center has 5,000 m² of usable area and 'peut accueillir jusqu'à 5 023 personnes assises et 9 588 personnes debout'; the official seated 'jusqu'à 5 023' figure is stored as capacity_max_delegates. exhibition_sqm stores the official 6,500 m² exhibition hall only; the Dome's 5,000 m² and outdoor Parvis 11,000 m² plus two 28,000 m² esplanades are not added into that total. Official access page address: Boulevard de l'aéroport, Abidjan; telephone +225 27 21 71 09 97. Coordinates are from the official access page's schema.org latitude/longitude 5.2641801, -3.9460482.
