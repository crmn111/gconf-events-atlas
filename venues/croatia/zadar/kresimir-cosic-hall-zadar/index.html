About

Multi-purpose indoor hall at Športski Centar Višnjik, officially at Splitska 3, 23000 Zadar, with public email. Official English hall page: 6,503 fixed seats in the central hall and a published total capacity of 8,500 seats (stored as capacity_max_delegates), plus VIP/press and family sections. Auxiliary hall 3 has a 300-seat auditorium. The first level is described as approximately 14,300 m², which is not stored as exact exhibition_sqm. Used by Infobip Shift. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Official Croatian ministry hotel registers were not applied (this is not a hotel).
