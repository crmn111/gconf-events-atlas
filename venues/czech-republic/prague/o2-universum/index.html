About

Prague congress and multifunctional centre officially at Českomoravská 2345/17a, 190 00 Prague 9, with public email. Official 2024 brochure: overall capacity up to 10,000, stored as capacity_max_delegates, and 38 halls with capacities from 40 to more than 4,500 people. Official Hall A: 4,500 seats and less than 1,800 m² (the area is a less-than figure and is not stored as an exact area_sqm). Official Halls B1–B3: 1,300 seats / less than 1,400 m²; Halls C1–C3: 1,000 seats / less than 1,000 m²; Meeting Hub 17 halls, 30–110 m², listed with a 1,335 figure in the brochure table. Used by Comic-Con Prague and Machine Learning Prague. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching the Nominatim named building pin.
