About

Prague Exhibition Grounds (Výstaviště Praha / Na Výstavišti), used by Svět knihy in the Průmyslový palác and by the Prague Quadrennial. Official English rental pages and the 2024 grounds PDF did not retrieve successfully in this pass, so indoor exhibition area, hall breakdowns and a street address are left null rather than taken from secondary reports. Coordinates are Nominatim’s exhibition-centre pin (not the theme-park or ticket POIs of the same name). The Průmyslový palác is treated as part of this campus, not a second venue.
