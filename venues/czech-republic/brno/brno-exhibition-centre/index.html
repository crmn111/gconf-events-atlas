About

Brno’s official exhibition grounds, operated by Veletrhy Brno, at Výstaviště 405/1, 603 00 Brno. Official about page: more than 130,000 sq m of net exhibition area, stored as exhibition_sqm with that “more than” caveat. Official rental pages: the compound covers 450,000 m² (site area, not stored as exhibition) with 14 halls and 19 conference halls. Official hall table (gross floor area / maximum capacity): Hall P 15,085 m² / 12,500 persons, stored as capacity_max_delegates; Hall V 12,020 m² / 4,592; Hall Z 9,017 m² / 3,266; Hall F 7,788 m² / 2,748; Hall B 6,791 m² / 4,597, and other named halls as listed in spaces. Hall P’s official net exhibition area is separately described as over 10,000 sq m. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for MSV and Amper.
