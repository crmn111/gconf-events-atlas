About

Reykjavík concert hall and conference centre officially at Austurbakka 2, 101 Reykjavík, with public email. Official conferences page: over 6,600 m² of conference space, stored only in halls_or_rooms, and capacity for up to 3,500 participants (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Official halls page: Eldborg 1,008 m² / up to 1,734 seats; Silfurberg 735 m²; Norðurljós 540 m²; Kaldalón 198 m² / 165–195 seats; Flói 1,000 m² open exhibition/reception space. No official campus exhibition total was published on the retrieved current pages, so exhibition_sqm is null. Used by UTmessan and EVE Fanfest. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin; Nominatim did not return a named pin in this pass.
