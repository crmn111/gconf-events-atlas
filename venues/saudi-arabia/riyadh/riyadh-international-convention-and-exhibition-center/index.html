About

Riyadh International Convention & Exhibition Center (RICEC), named for SADEX 2027, Industrial Transformation Saudi 2027 and Saudi Makes Future 2027. Recorded separately from Riyadh Front and from the newer Malham campus. Official hall-area figures were not confirmed from a successfully fetched official RICEC page in this pass (the official domain did not respond here), so those fields are null. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin used for SADEX and Industrial Transformation; the Saudi Makes Future atlas pin was not precise and was not stored.
