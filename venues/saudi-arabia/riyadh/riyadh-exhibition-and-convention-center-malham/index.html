About

Riyadh Exhibition and Convention Center at Malham, named by official LEAP organiser pages as the venue for LEAP (and by the atlas for Black Hat MEA and MONEY20/20 Middle East). This is a different campus from Riyadh Front (RFECC) and from Riyadh International Convention & Exhibition Center (RICEC) and is not merged with them. An official venue-operator hall-area total for Malham itself was not confirmed from a dedicated official venue site in this pass, so exhibition_sqm and capacity_max_delegates are null rather than borrowed from RFECC’s 39,350 m² chart or from unofficial directories. The LEAP FAQ is an official organiser source for the venue name, not for hall areas. The atlas pin is not marked precise, so coordinates are left null.
