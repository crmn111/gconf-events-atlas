About

Riyadh Front Exhibition & Conference Center (RFECC). Official capacity chart gives a combined hall area of 39,350 m², used as exhibition_sqm. The official homepage ‘RFECC in numbers’ block prints placeholder-like figures (including 10,000 m² of exhibition space and a 1 m ceiling height) that contradict the detailed official capacity chart; the chart is preferred and those homepage icon figures are not used. Official people capacities for the halls were not published on the captured capacity-chart page, so capacity_max_delegates is null. This venue is recorded separately from Riyadh Exhibition and Convention Center, Malham, and from Riyadh International Convention & Exhibition Center (RICEC). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
