About

Isle of Man Government (VillaGaiety) entertainment and conference complex on Harris Promenade, Douglas, IM1 2HP, added as the island’s principal indoor conference venue after skipping the sports-only Snaefell Mountain Course atlas event. Official operator PDF: Royal Hall seated theatre 1,360 (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Official Visit Isle of Man listing publishes Royal Hall theatre 1,340, which is noted and not stored. Coordinates are the named theatre Nominatim pin for Villa Marina. No atlas conference event is linked.
