About

Palace of Congresses in Tirana, published as an official point of interest by Bashkia Tiranë (tirana.al). Official POI record: address Bulevardi Dëshmorët e Kombit; main hall capacity 2,100 seats (stored as capacity_max_delegates); three smaller halls of 150, 280 and 300 seats; the building is 70 m long and 18–23 m high. Official POI latitude 41.31916507777535, longitude 19.82151632661834 are stored as the official municipal pin. The site footer address Sheshi Skënderbej, Nd. 2 is the municipality headquarters, not the palace, and is not used. No official exhibition-hall total was published (exhibition_sqm null).
