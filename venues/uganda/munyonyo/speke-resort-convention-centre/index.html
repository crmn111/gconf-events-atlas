About

National-scale convention centre at Speke Resort Munyonyo. Official contact pages give the address as Speke Resort Munyonyo and email [email removed]. The official indoor/outdoor capacity chart (retrieved 2026-11-24 from spekeresort.com) lists Rwenzori A+B at 3,200 m² with theatre 3,200 (stored as capacity_max_delegates). No official combined indoor exhibition total is published, so exhibition_sqm is null; outdoor areas (Lakeside 14,400 m² and others) are stored with outdoor notes and are not summed into exhibition_sqm. Official srcc-ug.com also lists Rwenzori A & B seating 3,200 / 3,200 m², consistent with the chart.
