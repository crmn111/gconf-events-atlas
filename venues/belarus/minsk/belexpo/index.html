About

Official English about page: Republican Unitary Enterprise National Exhibition Centre BelExpo of the Belarus President Property Management Directorate, ‘the only state exhibition company in Belarus’ and ‘Leading and largest specialized exhibition enterprise of the Republic of Belarus.’ Official contact page: 24 Pavliny Medelki Street, 220080, Minsk. Official Yandex map on the contact page publishes a placemark at lat 53.912755597227, lon 27.562126093254 (used here); the map camera centre is a few metres away at 53.912887032438, 27.562126093254. No official hall-area total or delegate capacity was published on the retrieved pages.
