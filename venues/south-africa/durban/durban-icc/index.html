About

Durban’s official international convention centre (also identified on mapping data as the Inkosi Albert Luthuli International Convention Centre), at 45 Bram Fischer Road, jointly managed with the Durban Exhibition Centre at 11 Walnut Road immediately opposite. Official copy says the main ICC convention area is 11,600 sqm and that the full venue can accommodate 12,000 delegates for a plenary or a banquet of up to 5,000; 12,000 is stored as capacity_max_delegates. Official DEC copy gives two large halls with a combined 9,270 sqm of flexible event space and a plenary audience of over 8,000. No single official indoor exhibition total covering both buildings was published, so exhibition_sqm is left null and the official hall figures are recorded as spaces. The two centres can function independently or as one combined island site by closing Walnut Road.
