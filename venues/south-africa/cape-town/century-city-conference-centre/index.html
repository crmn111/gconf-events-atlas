About

Conference and exhibition venue in the Century City precinct, officially at No. 3 Energy Lane. The official Fast Fact Guide (created February 2025) says 21 versatile spaces accommodate up to 2,000 people, stored as capacity_max_delegates, and that four large halls are available, three of which can be combined. The official combined Halls A–C layout is 1,029 sqm, stored as exhibition_sqm; Hall D is an additional official 394 sqm. No official total for all four halls together was published, so that sum is not stored.
