About

Cape Town’s official convention centre, operated as CTICC 1 at Convention Square, 1 Lower Long Street, and CTICC 2 at the corner of Heerengracht and Rua Bartholomeu Dias, Foreshore. Official floor plans give CTICC 1 Hall 1 as 2,029 sqm (Halls 1A+1B), Hall 2 2,118, Hall 3 2,258, Hall 4A 2,982 and Hall 4B 1,267 (10,654 sqm without double-counting Hall 1 subdivisions) and CTICC 2 halls 5–10 as 1,533, 1,767, 1,537, 1,993, 1,933 and 1,459 sqm (10,222 sqm, excluding Hall 8.1–8.3). Those official hall areas sum to 20,876 sqm, stored as exhibition_sqm. Official marketing also refers to “over 21,000 m²” of exhibition space; the homepage “140,855 m² floor space” figure is building floor space and is not stored as exhibition area. A published campus delegate maximum was not taken from the official CTICC site in this pass.
