About

Nasrec exhibition, conference and events venue, marketed as South Africa’s largest purpose-built exhibition, conference, convention and events centre, on a site the official about page describes as stretching over 50 hectares. Official hall pages give gross areas for Hall 5 (7,362 sqm), Hall 6 (12,304), Hall 7 (4,890), Hall 8 (4,890), Hall 9 (6,532) and Hall 10 (2,420). Those six halls sum to 38,398 sqm but are not stored as exhibition_sqm because no official indoor exhibition campus total was published for the full venue (the homepage “150,000 square metres of space” is a broader campus figure). Official copy also refers to large meetings for 20,000 delegates, stored as capacity_max_delegates, and 7,500 sqm of paved outdoor festival space, which is not included in exhibition_sqm. Also known as Expo Centre Nasrec.
