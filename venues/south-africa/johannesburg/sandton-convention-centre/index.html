About

Johannesburg convention and exhibition centre on Maude Street, Sandown. The official November 2023 capacity chart gives Exhibition 1 as 5,430 sqm (cinema 4,100, cocktail 4,200) and Exhibition 2 as 5,050 sqm (cinema 3,800, cocktail 3,900); those two halls sum to 10,480 sqm, stored as exhibition_sqm, and match the venue’s “more than 10,000 m²” exhibition wording. Official copy also refers to 22,000 m² of flexible event space, which is not stored as exhibition area. The June 2022 fact sheet says the centre can host 10,000 visitors at once (stored as capacity_max_delegates) and gives slightly different Pavilion figures; the 2023 chart is preferred for room sizes. The largest listed cinema layout on the 2023 chart is the Pavilion at 5,000.
