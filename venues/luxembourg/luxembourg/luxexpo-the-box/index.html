About

Kirchberg exhibition and events campus officially at 10, Circuit de la Foire Internationale, L-1347 Luxembourg-Kirchberg. Official why-exhibit page: 7 modular halls, 30,000 m² combined (stored as exhibition_sqm). The same official site also states 34,250 m² of total modular indoor space; 34,250 is noted and not stored as exhibition_sqm because it is the broader modular-indoor total. Outdoor 45,000 m² is excluded. Official capacity is given as “from 100 to 3,000+” / “over 3,000 participants,” which is not stored as an exact capacity_max_delegates. Coordinates are the named exhibition-hall Nominatim pin, matching the atlas precise pin. Linked: Nexus Luxembourg 2027.
