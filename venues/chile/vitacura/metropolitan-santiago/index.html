About

Vitacura convention and events centre officially at Costanera Sur S.J.E. de Balaguer 5600, also styled Metropolitan Santiago Convention & Event Center. Official homepage: 12 halls from 152 m² to 1,435 m², plus more than 16,000 m² of park and esplanade for outdoor or hybrid events; that outdoor figure is not stored as exhibition_sqm. A published indoor exhibition total and a campus delegate maximum were not taken from the retrieved official pages. The atlas CHACO pin is not marked precise, and Nominatim returned only a parking POI of the same name, so coordinates are left null.
