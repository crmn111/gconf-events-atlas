About

Chile’s main convention and events centre in Huechuraba, officially at Avenida El Salto 5000, Santiago de Chile. Official Spanish homepage: 27,000 m² of covered halls and pavilions on a 160,000 m² site. Official exhibition buildings are the Pabellón Ferial (12,000 m² available, 13 m height on the dedicated hall page) and the Expocenter (5,000 m² constructed surface); those two official areas sum to 17,000 m², stored as exhibition_sqm. The convention centre is published as 10,000 m² of constructed surface, including a 1,620 m² main ballroom, and is not added into exhibition_sqm. Official copy also says events of 50 to 10,000 people, stored as capacity_max_delegates; the 10,000 figure includes indoor or outdoor use. Official open areas are 57,500 m² in Spanish and 57,200 m² in English, used mainly as parking or outdoor exhibition, and are not included in exhibition_sqm. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin; Nominatim returned only bus-stop pins of the same name, which were discarded.
