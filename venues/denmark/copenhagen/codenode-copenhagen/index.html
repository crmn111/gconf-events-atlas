About

Conference and events space in Nordhavn, officially at Gdanskgade 2, 2150 Copenhagen. Official copy says the venue offers conferences for up to 150 attendees, stored as capacity_max_delegates, along with large-scale hackathons, exhibitions and meetups, and describes three large breakout rooms for multi-track events and one big conference room.
