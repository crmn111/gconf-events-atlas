About

São Paulo exhibition and convention centre operated with GL events, officially located at Rodovia dos Imigrantes, km 1,5, CEP 04329-900. exhibition_sqm is the official indoor pavilion total of 77,305 sqm from the official technical tables, not the webpage’s ‘100 thousand m² of modulated structures’ (which is not broken down as indoor exhibition) and not the official 50,000 m² external area. Official people capacities for a headline auditorium or campus maximum were not confirmed from the official technical PDF or trade-shows page captured in this pass (third-party and operator-store figures of 7,800 were not used), so capacity_max_delegates is null. Atlas strings ‘São Paulo Expo’, ‘São Paulo Expo, São Paulo, SP’ and ‘São Paulo Expo (plus a second venue TBA)’ are this campus; VTEX Day’s unnamed second venue is not invented. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
