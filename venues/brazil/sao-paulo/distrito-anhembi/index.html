About

São Paulo exhibition and convention campus at official address Av. Olavo Fontoura, 1209, Santana, including the exhibition pavilions and, on the same official site, a separate Sambódromo (not used here as a conference hall). exhibition_sqm is 67,523, the sum of the five official Expo hall areas (14,547+14,723+12,692+12,681+12,880), consistent with the official wording ‘mais de 67 mil m² de área de exposição’. The official marquise figures are not included in exhibition_sqm. An older 2019 pavilion leaflet quoting more than 76,000 m² predates the current official hall table and was not used. Atlas address variants are merged. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Official people capacities for a campus headline were not published on the captured exhibition page, so capacity_max_delegates is null.
