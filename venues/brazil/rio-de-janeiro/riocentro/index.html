About

Rio de Janeiro convention and exhibition centre at official address Av. Salvador Allende, 6555, Barra Olímpica, managed with GL events. exhibition_sqm is 58,317, the sum of the official exhibition-salon areas published for Pavilhões 2, 3 and 4, Pavilhão das Artes (2,354+4,688) and the Anfiteatro salon (10,706+15,725+21,453+6,821+3,612). No official single campus exhibition total was published on the pages captured, and a Pavilhão 1 exhibition salon was not listed, so this is a sum of the official salon figures rather than a venue-stated grand total. Official site-wide text of more than 500,000 m² (of which 98,000 m² built) is not used as exhibition_sqm. capacity_max_delegates is the official Pavilhão 4 figure of 16,750 people (variable with layout), the largest official single-hall people capacity; the Anfiteatro official total is 12,000. One official overview page states Anfiteatro auditorium capacity as 8,000, while the official Anfiteatro space text used here says 9,000; the discrepancy is noted and not averaged. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
