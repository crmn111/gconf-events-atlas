About

Israel’s official leading exhibition and convention centre at 101 Rokach Boulevard, Tel Aviv. Official pavilion areas are stored hall by hall. Coordinates are the named conference-centre Nominatim pin for אקספו תל אביב. No atlas events are linked (Israel is outside the current atlas set). Official-star conference hotels were not added in this first expansion pass pending a property-level Ministry of Tourism listing.
