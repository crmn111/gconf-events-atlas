About

MTA official 5-star hotel on High Street, Sliema (licence HTL-21867 / H/0422, 140 bedrooms), also presented as AX The Palace on the official brand site. Official venue pages: the largest published capacity is 600 reception for State Hall & Alexandra Gardens (the page covers the hall with the gardens). The largest indoor theatre figure is 225 in the Royal Hall, which can be partitioned into two areas. Events team: events.palace@axhotelsmalta.com.
