About

Harbourfront hotel at the edge of Valletta and Floriana with a purpose-built conference wing. The official meetings page gives the Grand Ballroom 730 m² (5.2 m ceiling) and Floriani Hall 917 m², plus Andrea Belli at 226 m² and Bellavanti for sessions of up to 140 persons, but does not publish a single theatre maximum for the largest halls. VisitMalta Incentives & Meetings, the official convention bureau, states a meeting capacity of 1,200 people in cocktail layout; that official bureau figure is used for capacity_max_delegates. Classified 5-star on the MTA hotels licence CSV (HTL-04355 / H/0003, 2026 licence year). Coordinates are a Nominatim result for the named hotel.
