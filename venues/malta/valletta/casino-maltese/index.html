About

Historic private members’ club at 247 Republic Street, Valletta, used as the venue for the atlas event Gaming Pulse 2026. The official private-events page lists hireable rooms and capacities: the Ballroom 180 pax at round tables, 210 at long tables or 300 standing; the Presidents' Room 120 / 180 / 280; the Dining Room 140 / 140 / 200; and the Princess' Room 50 / 60 / 80. The Crown Princes' Room is described as a private board room for up to 25 people. Headline capacity_max_delegates is the official Ballroom standing figure of 300. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches a Nominatim result for the named venue.
