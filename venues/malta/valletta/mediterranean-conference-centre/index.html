About

Historic congress centre in the former Sacra Infermeria in Valletta. The official hall-capacities table gives Sacra Infermeria Hall 1,705 m² and 1,920 theatre / 1,200 banquet / 2,500 reception, La Valette Hall 1,500 m² and 1,440 theatre / 900 banquet / 1,500 reception, and Republic Hall 728 m² and 1,400 theatre. Headline capacity_max_delegates is the largest official theatre figure (1,920). A single official indoor exhibition total is not published; Sacra Infermeria is described on the hall page as able to take 100 stands of 9 m², with two further halls available for additional exhibition space. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Public sales email is sales@mcc.com.mt.
