About

Golden Bay resort with a pillarless ballroom and adjoining terraces. The official meetings page gives the Ballroom 591 m² and 650 theatre / 400 classroom / 350 rounds / 800 reception, and lists an 800-person maximum among key features. Headline capacity_max_delegates is the official ballroom theatre figure; 800 is the official reception/key-features maximum. Classified 5-star on the MTA hotels licence CSV (HTL-04361 / H/0012, 2026 licence year). Coordinates are a Nominatim result for the named hotel.
