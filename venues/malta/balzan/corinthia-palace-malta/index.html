About

MTA official 5-star hotel in Balzan (licence HTL-04353 / H/0001, 158 bedrooms), opposite San Anton Gardens. Official meetings and events page: Corinthia Ballroom up to 450 guests; outdoor Secret Garden up to 650 guests; Caprice Garden up to 200; Caprice Lounge up to 100; Veuve Clicquot Lounge up to 80; Vogue Room up to 50; Executive Lounge up to 40; Balzan Room up to 30; Mdina and Lija rooms up to 18 each.
