About

Peninsula resort in St Julian’s with a conference centre. The official Marriott events page gives the Dragonara Point Ballroom 600 theatre, 400 seated or 1,000 standing, divisible into St Julian's Bay, Spinola Bay and St George's Bay. The same page’s summary statistics list 1,500 as the capacity of the largest space, identified in the FAQ as the outdoor Reef Club Sun Deck; headline capacity_max_delegates is the largest indoor theatre figure (600). The page also refers both to 17 meeting rooms / 1,400 m² and to 15 event rooms / 2,001 m² total event space. Classified 5-star on the MTA hotels licence CSV (HTL-04354 / H/0002, Westin Dragonara Hotel, 2026 licence year). Coordinates are a Nominatim result for the named hotel.
