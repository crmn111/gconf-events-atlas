Halls
Official Marriott events page: an entire floor dedicated to meetings, with figures given both as 13 meeting rooms covering 1,363 m² and, elsewhere on the same page, 12 event rooms / 1,358 m² total event space. The largest published space is The Gallery, capacity 650. The Ballroom is described as the largest meeting space with natural daylight, for meetings of up to 300 people and a gala dinner for 150. MTA licence HTL-04467 is listed as "Marriot Hotel & Spa" at 39 Triq il-Kbira, St Julian's, STJ 1017 (301 bedrooms, official 5-star); the official website on the MTA CSV is the former Le Méridien St Julian's site. The current official hotel and events pages use the Malta Marriott Resort & Spa name.

About

MTA official 5-star hotel on Balluta Bay, St Julian's, recorded on the MTA register as "Marriot Hotel & Spa" (licence HTL-04467 / H/0393, 301 bedrooms) and operated under the current official name Malta Marriott Resort & Spa. Official events page: The Gallery is the largest-capacity space at 650; the Ballroom hosts meetings of up to 300 and gala dinners for 150. Same-page totals of 13 rooms / 1,363 m² and 12 rooms / 1,358 m² are both published; both are recorded rather than reconciled.
