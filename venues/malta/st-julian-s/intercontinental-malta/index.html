About

St George’s Bay hotel with the InterContinental Arena Conference Centre. The current official conferences page states 24 meeting rooms totalling 6,000 m² and events from 10 to 1,700 delegates; it also describes the Arena itself as spanning 3,600 m². The hotel’s 2019 MICE factsheet likewise gives 6,000 m² of meeting space and 1,700 theatre / 960 gala in the Arena, while the factsheet room chart lists Maurice De Cesare at 1,215 m² and 1,500 theatre / 2,000 reception / 960 banquet (European thousands punctuation in the PDF). Headline capacity follows the current website’s 1,700 figure; chart figures are kept on spaces with a 2019 date note. Classified 5-star on the MTA hotels licence CSV (HTL-04397 / H/0093, 2026 licence year). Public contact is; named staff mailboxes on the events page were not used. Coordinates are a Nominatim result for the named hotel.
