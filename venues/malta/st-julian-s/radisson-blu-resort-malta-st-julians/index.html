Halls
Official meetings page publishes a seven-room capacity table. The Grand Ballroom is 585 m² (ceiling 5.5 m) with 550 theatre / 300 classroom / 250 rounds / 800 reception. The same page's narrative also says the event facilities can accommodate as many as 1,500 guests, that there are five meeting rooms in one passage and seven in another, and that the Grand Ballroom can host 500-700 guests. Headline capacity uses the official room table (800 reception in the Grand Ballroom). MTA licence HTL-20226, 243 bedrooms, official 5-star.

About

MTA official 5-star resort on the St Julian's seafront (licence HTL-20226 / H/0247, 243 bedrooms). Official meetings capacity table: Grand Ballroom 585 m², 550 theatre and 800 reception; Carlson Suite 236 m² and Marie Louise (full) 230 m², each 200 theatre. Sales contact on the official page:. The 1,500-guest narrative figure is recorded as a same-page discrepancy and is not used as the headline room capacity.
