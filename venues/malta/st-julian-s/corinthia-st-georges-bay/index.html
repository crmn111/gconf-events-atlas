About

Seafront Corinthia hotel at St George’s Bay with an in-house conference centre. The current official in-house venues page gives the Fortress Suite up to 600 guests in theatre or cocktail layouts (about 4,596 sq ft) and 225 banquet. The official events brochure’s capacity chart lists the Fortress Suite as 322 m², 550 theatre, 400 banquet and 800 reception, and states 14 meeting rooms with a 550-seat main room; those brochure figures are recorded on spaces with a note that the current page lists 600 theatre/cocktail. Headline capacity follows the current official page. Classified 5-star on the MTA hotels licence CSV (HTL-04454 / H/0307, Corinthia Hotel St. George's Bay Malta, 2026 licence year). Coordinates are a Nominatim result for the named hotel.
