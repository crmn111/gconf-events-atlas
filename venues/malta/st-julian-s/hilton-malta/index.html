About

Portomaso waterfront hotel and conference centre in St Julian’s. The official events page and meetings brochure state capacity for up to 1,330 delegates in the Grandmaster Suite (1,100 theatre on the flat surface with stage, or 1,330 including the tiered balcony) in a room of 963.6 m². The Portomaso Suite is 436 m² combined (506 theatre / 280 classroom / 260 banquet / 500 reception on the official capacity chart; narrative text on the same brochure also says ‘capacity ranges from 480 people in theatre style’). The Spinola Suite is given as 780 m². Classified 5-star on the Malta Tourism Authority hotels licence CSV (licence HTL-14697 / H/0005, HILTON INTERNATIONAL, 2026 licence year). Coordinates are a Nominatim result for the named hotel.
