About

MTA official 5-star hotel on St George's Bay, St Julian's (licence HTL-34041 / H/0489, 397 bedrooms). The official meetings page and breakout brochure publish 1,350 m² of flexible event space. Melodia Ballroom is 466 m² with 380 theatre and 310 round-table (standard rounds 260; elegant spacing 200). Sonata Conference 1-3 combined is 140 m² / 115 theatre and can be divided into three sections. Contact on the official brochure and meetings page:.
