About

Malta’s largest exhibition and convention venue, in the Millennium Stand at the National Stadium, Ta’ Qali, and part of the Corinthia Group since 2016. Official pages give 8,500 m² of indoor space in the Tension Fabric Structure (170 m by 50 m, 16.5 m maximum height) plus outdoor grounds; combined indoor and outdoor area is described as over 22,000 m² on the home and technical pages, or 20,000 m² as the ‘entire area’ on the TFS capacity table. exhibition_sqm is the official indoor TFS figure, not the combined total. The TFS capacity table lists 10,300 theatre / 4,000 banquet / 14,500 reception for the main area; a separate TFS page sentence says the structure can hold 13,000 seated or 25,000 standing, which is noted as a discrepancy. Headline capacity_max_delegates follows the table’s 10,300 theatre figure. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
