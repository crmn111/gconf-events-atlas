About

Rwanda’s national convention centre in Kigali, officially described on kcc.rw. Official homepage: the main auditorium under the dome seats up to 2,600 people (stored as capacity_max_delegates). The official wording “more than 5,000 delegates at once” is not stored as an exact campus maximum. No official street address or exhibition_sqm figure was published on the retrieved official homepage, and /contact paths returned 404, so those fields are null. Coordinates are the named conference-centre Nominatim pin, matching the atlas precise pin.
