About

Named historic harbour venue for the Antigua Charter Yacht Show 2026, officially managed by the National Parks Authority, with public email and official address English Harbour, Antigua. The atlas pin is imprecise and coincides with the Nominatim national-park pin for a large protected area, so coordinates are left null rather than using a park centroid. No separate official convention centre with confirmed figures was added in this tight pass; the American University of Antigua convention facility was not confirmed on a current official capacity page.
