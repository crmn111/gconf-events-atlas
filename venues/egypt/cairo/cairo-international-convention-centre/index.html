About

Nasr City convention and exhibition campus operated by the Egypt Expo & Convention Authority (EECA), the official governmental body that integrated the Cairo International Convention Centre in 2008. Official how-to-get and about pages give the address as Salah Salem St., Nasr City; the EECA homepage also lists Nasr Road, Nasr City, with postal code 19019. Public email on the how-to-get page is. Hall areas and a maximum delegate figure were not published on the retrieved EECA pages. Used by the Egypt International Boat Show.
