About

Downtown Cairo entrepreneurship and events campus, officially at 171 El Tahrir Street, Bab El Louq, with public email. Meeting-space capacities were not copied in this pass. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Official site also mentions a forthcoming New Cairo hub, which is not this record.
