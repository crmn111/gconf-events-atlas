About

New Cairo exhibition campus on El Moshir Tantawy Axis in the 5th Settlement, also associated with the National Company for International Exhibitions and Conferences (NCIEC / nc-iec.com). The official venue homepage is news-heavy and did not yield a precise indoor exhibition total in this pass. The official EDEX 2027 venue page, allowed as an organiser source, describes four halls providing over 40,300 m² of exhibition space; that “over” figure is recorded only in halls_or_rooms, and exhibition_sqm is left null. Used by the Cairo International Book Fair, Cairo ICT and AI Everything MEA Egypt; Insomnia Egypt is linked as last confirmed at this campus (atlas pin not marked precise for that edition). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin used by the named-centre events; Nominatim did not return a named exhibition-centre pin in this pass.
