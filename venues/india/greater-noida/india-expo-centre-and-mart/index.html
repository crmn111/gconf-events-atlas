About

Greater Noida exhibition, convention and mart complex. Official address: Plot No. 23–25 & 27–29, Knowledge Park-II, Gautam Buddha Nagar, Greater Noida 201306. Public email. A dedicated official exhibition-hall total was not confirmed from the captured official pages (the homepage counters were script-rendered as zero), so exhibition_sqm is null. capacity_max_delegates is the official Multipurpose Hall 2 theatre figure of 3,250 from the official convention-centre table, which is internally consistent with its 3,456 sqm area. Larger official ‘Convention Hall 9, 10, 11, 12’ figures (25,000 / 23,500 / 13,500 / 26,000, all labelled sqm) are noted but not used as headline capacities because the column units are ambiguous. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
