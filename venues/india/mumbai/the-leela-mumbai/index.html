About

Official meetings page and July 2024 factsheet (retrieved 2026-11-24). The factsheet and meetings page agree that the Grand Ballroom reception / max guests figure is 1,000 (stored as capacity_max_delegates); theatre for the combined Grand Ballroom is 700. Areas on the factsheet are in square feet and were converted. Official copy places the hotel in Sahar / Andheri East, five minutes from the international airport; a fuller street address was not printed on the retrieved factsheet or meetings page. Hotel marketing language that the property is five-star is not used as star_rating: no property-level NIDHI+ / HRACC classified listing was confirmed on a fetched official row in this pass, so star_rating is null. The official image-based capacity-chart PDF is stored; extractable layout figures used here come from the official factsheet table, which labels the columns explicitly.
