About

Medellín’s official convention and exhibition centre at Calle 41 N° 55-80. Official espacios page: 23,726 m² covered area, 20 spaces, five exhibition pavilions totalling 13,142 m² (stored as exhibition_sqm) and 23 convention salones totalling 10,584 m². The largest official website capacity is Pabellón Amarillo up to 5,724 people, stored as capacity_max_delegates. An official service portfolio PDF lists the same five pavilions with areas summing to 16,705 m² (Amarillo 4,912; Verde 4,323; Blanco 2,989; Azul 2,349; Rojo 2,132) and a higher Amarillo aforo of 6,664; the current website totals and capacities are preferred, and the PDF figures are noted on the spaces. Gran Salón website capacity is 2,900, while the PDF lists 2,240 m² / aforo 4,386. The official outdoor Plazoleta Descubierta is not included in exhibition_sqm. The atlas Startco pin is not marked precise, so coordinates are left null.
