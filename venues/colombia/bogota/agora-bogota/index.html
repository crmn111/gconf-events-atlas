About

Bogotá international convention centre included as a remaining major congress venue next to the Corferias district. Official design page: total area 64,883 m² (the building footprint is described as an 81 × 81 m square, 49 m high); that total building area is not stored as exhibition_sqm. Official copy: a multifunctional building on five floors with more than 18 meeting rooms, and a top-floor capacity of 4,000 people, stored as capacity_max_delegates. A complete official street address and a confirmed Nominatim named-venue pin were not obtained in this pass, so address and coordinates are left null. An official 2023 capacity table PDF is stored; current webpage figures are preferred for the headline fields.
