About

Bogotá’s international business and exhibition centre, officially at Carrera 37 # 24-67, with public email serviciocliente@corferias.com. The retrieved official recinto and home pages did not publish a current indoor exhibition total or a campus delegate maximum, so those fields are left null rather than taken from UFI or third-party figures. An official 12 March 2026 announcement describes a modernisation that, after new column-free Pabellón Norte (20,000 m²) and Pabellón Sur (15,000 m²) halls plus the Gran Salón, would maintain a total exhibition capacity of 50,000 m²; that is a planned post-works figure and is not stored as current exhibition_sqm. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin; Nominatim returned only a bus-stop pin of the same name, which was discarded.
