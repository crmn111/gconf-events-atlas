About

National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai), the ‘four-leaf clover’ at Hongqiao, named for Auto Shanghai, SNEC PV+ES, CIIE and Comicup. Atlas strings that add ‘(Four-leaf Clover)’ are this campus. exhibition_sqm is the official indoor exhibition figure of 400,000 m²; official outdoor 100,000 m² is not included. A detailed official English venue page did not return usable HTML in this pass; figures follow official NECC Shanghai pages/materials cited in source_urls. Official people capacities for a campus headline were not confirmed here, so capacity_max_delegates is null. A street number was not confirmed from the captured official text.
