About

Bao'an, Shenzhen exhibition and convention complex adjacent to Shenzhen Airport, at official address No. 1 Zhancheng Road, Fuhai Street, Bao'an District. exhibition_sqm is the official Phase I indoor exhibition area of 400,000 m². The official Phase II figure of 500,000 m² is a future total and is not used. Official people capacities for a campus headline were not confirmed from the captured official brochure/organiser text, so capacity_max_delegates is null. The venue official website is largely script-rendered; figures are taken from the official venue brochure text and the official CHTF venue-info page, which match. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
