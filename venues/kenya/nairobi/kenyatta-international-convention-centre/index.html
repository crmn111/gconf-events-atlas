About

Kenya’s national convention centre officially on Harambee Avenue, Nairobi, with public emails and. Official Tsavo Hall page: Tsavo Ballroom 2,444 m² and up to 4,000 guests, stored as capacity_max_delegates. Official pricing guideline also lists Amphitheatre 2,666 m² and several open areas (Courtyard 7,056; Upper COMESA 5,497; Lower COMESA 6,057; Lawns 7,500 m²), which are not stored as a combined indoor exhibition_sqm because no official indoor-only campus total was published.
