About

Zambia's national conference centre in Lusaka, officially owned by the Industrial Development Corporation (IDC) of Zambia. Official address Plot# 8025, Great East Road, Lusaka; [email removed]. The official about page states that the two main buildings provide a total of 4,478 sq. metres of designated conference space, with a maximum capacity of 6,680 delegates; the same page sidebar also says '5,200 Square Metres; 6,680 Delegate Capacity'. The 6,680 figure agrees and is stored as capacity_max_delegates. The 4,478 vs 5,200 m² figures conflict, so exhibition_sqm is left null and both official area figures are noted here. Official hall wording: Main Auditorium seating for up to 2,500; Main Hall accommodating up to 1,800; Banquet Hall capacity of 1,000 guests.
