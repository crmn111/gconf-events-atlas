About

Official SMX Convention Center Manila at Seashell Lane, Mall of Asia Complex, Pasay City 1300. Official page: four purpose-built halls totalling 9,130 square metres (stored as exhibition_sqm). The wider 17,170 square-metre total of halls, function rooms and meeting rooms is noted and not stored as exhibition_sqm. Coordinates are the named events-venue Nominatim pin.
