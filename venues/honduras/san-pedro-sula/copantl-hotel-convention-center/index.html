About

Hotel and convention centre in San Pedro Sula. Official English/Spanish sites give the address Res. Los Arcos / Los Arcos, Blvd. del Sur, San Pedro Sula 21104, with and events email. Official wording: capacity to host up to 5 thousand people in 19 rooms / capacity from 30 to 5 thousand people. The 5,000 figure is stored as capacity_max_delegates as an official campus 'up to' and is spread across 19 rooms, not a named single-hall maximum — that caveat is noted. A room-by-room official capacity chart was not extracted in this pass, so spaces[] is empty and exhibition_sqm is null. Press figures from unofficial newspapers are not used.
