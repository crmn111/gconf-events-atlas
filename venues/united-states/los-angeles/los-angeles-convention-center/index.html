About

Los Angeles's downtown convention centre at 1201 South Figueroa Street. Official laconventioncenter.com venue-maps page: 720,000 square feet of exhibit space (used for exhibition_sqm), 64 meeting rooms and four outdoor plazas. Official exhibit-halls page and 2024 floor-plans PDF: the largest published hall theatre capacity is 22,870 in South Hall (346,890 sq ft combined GHJK on the official PDF, described on the website as nearly 350,000 sq ft). West Hall is 210,685 sq ft / 15,000 theatre. The official Kentia Hall area is 160,000 sq ft on the current exhibit-halls page and 162,000 sq ft on the official PDF. Existing atlas event: OFC 2027.
