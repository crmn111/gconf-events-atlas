About

Anaheim Convention Center at 800 W Katella Avenue, adjacent to the Disneyland Resort area. Official Visit Anaheim exhibit-hall capacities page: halls A-E total 813,607 square feet of exhibit space (used for exhibition_sqm). The largest published exhibit-hall theatre capacity is 15,000 in Hall D (221,284 sq ft). The Arena is a separate 28,140 sq ft space with 7,500 stadium-style seats and is not included in the 813,607 exhibit-hall total. Existing atlas event: NAMM Show 2027. The official venue website is anaheimconventioncenter.com; figures in this update come from the official Visit Anaheim capacity tables after the venue site was previously unreadable.
