About

Boston’s Seaport convention centre, operated as part of the Massachusetts Convention Center Authority / Signature Boston campus (the official site also refers to it as MCEC). Official figures give 516,000 sq ft of contiguous exhibit space (converted to 47,938 m²) and an 82-room meeting programme. The 2.1 million square feet figure is a building total, not stored as exhibition_sqm. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches a Nominatim result for the named exhibition centre at 415 Summer Street.
