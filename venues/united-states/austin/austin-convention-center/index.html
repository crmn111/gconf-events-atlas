About

Austin’s city convention centre. The official website states that the Austin Convention Center is closed for construction as part of a planned expansion from 365,000 to 620,000 square feet of rentable space. Those official rentable-space figures are not stored as exhibition_sqm because they are not published as exhibit-hall area, and the building is closed in 2026. No precise Nominatim pin for the closed site was recorded, so coordinates are null. SXSW and other atlas events without a published venue are not linked here.
