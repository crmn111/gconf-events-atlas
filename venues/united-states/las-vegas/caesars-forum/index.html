About

Purpose-built conference centre on the Las Vegas Strip, officially named by AWS as one of the venues for re:Invent 2026 (together with Caesars Palace, Encore, MGM Grand, The Venetian and Wynn). Official 2022 Caesars fact sheet: 550,000 sq ft, up to 10,000 participants at one time, and two pillarless ballrooms (Forum and Summit). exhibition_sqm is null because the official source describes meeting/conference space rather than a dedicated exhibit-hall total. Linked atlas event: aws-reinvent-2026. The US has no official government hotel star system; this is a convention venue, so star_rating is null.
