About

The main Las Vegas convention campus, owned and operated by the Las Vegas Convention and Visitors Authority. Official text describes approximately 2.9 million square feet of exhibit space; the page also displays 2,934,153 as ‘TOTAL Sq. Ft.’ next to a 623,058-square-foot largest room, which is treated here as the official exhibit total and converted to exhibition_sqm (272,592 m²). A single official maximum delegate figure for the whole campus is not published (meeting-room seating is given as 20 to 2,500), so capacity_max_delegates is null. The atlas event Money20/20 USA 2027 is listed at the Las Vegas Convention Center and Resorts World and is linked on both records. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. The United States has no official government hotel star-rating system; star_rating is null for all US records.
