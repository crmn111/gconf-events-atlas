About

Convention centre at Mandalay Bay on the Las Vegas Strip. Official pages describe 2.1 million gross square feet of meeting and exhibit space (a combined total, not stored as exhibition_sqm). exhibition_sqm is the sum of the official exhibit-hall figures in the capacities guide: Bayside Exhibition Halls 861,231 sq ft plus Shoreline Exhibit Hall 181,800 sq ft (the facilities page also rounds Shoreline to 181,000 sq ft). Headline capacity_max_delegates is the official Michelob ULTRA Arena seating figure of 12,000; the Mandalay Bay Ballroom chart lists 9,408 theatre. Atlas events at Mandalay Bay Convention Center and Mandalay Bay, Las Vegas are linked here. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. star_rating is null; the US has no official government hotel classification.
