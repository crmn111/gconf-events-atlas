About

Convention and expo complex at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas (including Venetian Expo, formerly Sands Expo). Official pages state 2.25 million square feet of exhibition and meeting space in total; exhibition_sqm is the official contiguous Expo Halls A–D figure of 655,600 sq ft (60,907 m²), not the combined meeting-and-exhibit total. Atlas events listed at The Venetian Las Vegas and Venetian Expo are linked here as one complex. A street-level official address string beyond the resort name was not copied from the meetings homepage in this pass.
