About

Strip resort with a large meetings campus, including the Rose, Lily, Blossom, Jasmine, Orchid, Lotus and Perdana spaces, Resorts World Theatre and The Complex. Official pages give 250,000 square feet of meeting and event space and a theatre for 5,000 guests in 65,000 square feet; headline capacity_max_delegates is that official theatre figure. The Complex is described both as 72,000 sq ft and as 70,000 sq ft of indoor space plus an outdoor plaza, so it is not stored as a single exhibition_sqm total. The atlas event Money20/20 USA 2027 is listed at the Las Vegas Convention Center and Resorts World and is linked on both records. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches a Nominatim result for the named resort. star_rating is null.
