About

Rebuilt Miami Beach convention centre (renovation completed 2020). Official quick facts publish both imperial and metric figures: 45,676 m² of contiguous exhibit space in four halls, which is stored as exhibition_sqm, plus a 5,665 m² grand ballroom and up to 84 meeting rooms. A single official maximum delegate figure for the exhibit halls was not taken from that page. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches a Nominatim result for the named centre.
