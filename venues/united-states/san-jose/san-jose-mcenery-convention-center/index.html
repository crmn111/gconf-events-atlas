About

San Jose's convention centre at 150 W San Carlos Street (address confirmed on the official Visit San Jose convention-centre page and on NVIDIA's official GTC FAQ). Official at-a-glance PDF and official webpage: 214,760 sq ft of convention-centre exhibit space and 165,000 sq ft contiguous; the exhibit-halls narrative also cites 143,000 sq ft opening to 165,000. Official page: 43 meeting rooms with capacities from 50 to 3,900 theatre style (used as headline capacity). Linked atlas event: nvidia-gtc-2027, which the official GTC FAQ places here (plus venues throughout San Jose) for 14-18 March 2027 (the GTC homepage also gives 15-18 March). The US has no official government star system; star_rating is null.
