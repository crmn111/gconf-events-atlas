About

Rosemont, Illinois convention centre, listed in the atlas as the venue for the Chicago Boat Show. Address and coordinates are a Nominatim result for the named conference centre at 5555 North River Road, matching the atlas precise pin (a bus-stop pin at the same name was not used). Official exhibit-area figures were not recorded in this pass after the convention-centre page failed to load, so numeric fields are left null.
