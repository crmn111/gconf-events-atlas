About

North America’s largest convention centre campus, on Chicago’s Near South Side. Official pages state 2.6 million square feet of exhibit space, including 1.2 million square feet on a single level, but do not publish a more precise integer, so exhibition_sqm is left null rather than implying false precision. Headline capacity_max_delegates is the official assembly seating figure of 18,000 (Wintrust Arena is separately given as 10,000 seats and Arie Crown Theater as 4,192). Address follows the official event listing used in the atlas. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
