About

Atlanta’s principal convention centre, operated by the Georgia World Congress Center Authority. Official text gives 1.5 million square feet of contiguous exhibit space and more than 4 million square feet of flexible meeting and event space, without a more precise exhibit integer, so exhibition_sqm is left null. The October 2025 official floor-plan booklet is stored. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
