About

San Francisco’s main convention campus (Moscone North, South and West). Official pages state more than 790,000 square feet of flexible exhibit space; because the figure is not a precise integer, exhibition_sqm is left null. Atlas events at Moscone Center, Moscone North & South and Moscone West are linked here as one campus. Administrative office address is 747 Howard Street. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for Moscone Center.
