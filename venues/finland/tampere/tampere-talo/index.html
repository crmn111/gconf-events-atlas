About

Tampere-talo, Finland’s official congress and concert hall at Yliopistonkatu 55 (postal PL 16, 33101 Tampere). The official Main Auditorium page lists 1,800 seats, size 1,855, classroom 680, dining 480 and party 550; 1,800 is stored as capacity_max_delegates. A campus exhibition-area total was not taken from the retrieved official pages.
