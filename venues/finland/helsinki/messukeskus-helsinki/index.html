About

Finland’s biggest expo and convention centre, officially at Messuaukio 1, 00520 Helsinki. Official materials give a gross exhibition area of 58,000 m² (stored as exhibition_sqm) across seven halls, plus 40 meeting rooms and 13 seminar halls. The official 2026 brochure also states a total area of 114,000 m², which is not stored as exhibition space. Official seating includes the Amfi Hall (Hall 1) at 4,373 seats (also described as an auditorium for 4,400) and a Hall 7 grandstand of up to 7,500, stored as capacity_max_delegates. Individual hall floor areas in the brochure extract were not unambiguous for Halls 6 and 7, so those areas are not itemised.
