About

Canberra’s national convention centre on Constitution Avenue, overlooking Glebe Park. Official Exhibition Hall page gives 2,000 square metres and a host range of around 10–2,000 attendees; capacity_max_delegates and exhibition_sqm use those official figures. An official 2025 capacity-chart PDF is stored; the file did not yield a reliable text extract in this pass, so no additional room figures were taken from it. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
