About

South Bank convention and exhibition centre owned by South Bank Corporation and managed by Legends Global. Official exhibitions page: four pillarless halls totalling 20,000 sqm, with an additional 5,000 sqm when used with the Great Hall. Each hall is described as 72×72 m totalling 5,040 sqm, while the official foyer-level capacity table lists 5,000 m² per hall; spaces use the table’s 5,000 m² and note the 5,040 figure. capacity_max_delegates is 8,000 from the official Great Hall narrative (capability to extend to 8,000 for conventions with raiseable seating, divisible into four). The official table’s standard Great Hall theatre figure is 3,958. Official floor-plan brochure is dated September 2025. Competitor directory figures were not used. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
