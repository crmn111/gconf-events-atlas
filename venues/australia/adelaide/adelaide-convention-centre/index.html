About

South Australia’s principal convention centre on North Terrace, operated by Adelaide Venue Management. Official footer address is North Terrace, Adelaide, South Australia 5000. Official hall pages (not used as a summed campus total in this pass, because not every hall area was verified together from official pages) include published areas such as Hall F 1,060 m², Hall G 990 m², Hall H 2,980 m², Hall J 1,150 m² and Halls LMN combined 2,655 m²; those individual official hall figures can be backfilled into spaces[] in a later pass. The official 2025–26 Exhibition Handbook is stored; it is primarily an operational handbook and did not yield a usable official campus exhibition total. AIPC member figures were not used. Coordinates are the Nominatim named conference-centre pin for Adelaide Convention Centre on North Terrace.
