About

Hotel in Sydney’s former General Post Office building. Official address on the meetings-and-events PDF: No. 1 Martin Place, Sydney, New South Wales 2000. Official page and PDF: The Grand Ballroom banquet capacity up to 1,000; official PDF chart also gives theatre 1,400, classroom 660, cocktail 1,200, cabaret 800, and 1,058 m². capacity_max_delegates is the official Grand Ballroom theatre figure of 1,400.
