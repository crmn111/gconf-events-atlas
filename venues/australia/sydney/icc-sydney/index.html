About

NSW Government convention and exhibition centre at Darling Harbour, managed by Legends Global. Official total internal exhibition capacity is 35,000 sqm. The official About page states 2,400 sqm of multipurpose space plus 32,600 sqm in the exhibition halls. The official FAQ gives both 2,400 sqm and 2,600 sqm for that multipurpose component on the same page; exhibition_sqm is the agreed 35,000 sqm internal total, and the 2,400/2,600 discrepancy is not resolved into a third figure. capacity_max_delegates is 9,000, the official general-admission capacity of the TikTok Entertainment Centre / premier tiered theatre (8,000 seated in the January 2026 general fact sheet and the official capacity summary). The official FAQ also states that if all four theatres were at capacity the centre could host up to 12,000 delegates at one time; that concurrent figure is not used as the headline. Official capacity-summary Grand Ballroom (B1+B2+B3) cocktail is 2,880, while the general fact sheet says the ballroom can host more than 3,500 in cocktail mode; spaces follow the capacity-summary chart. The atlas string 'ICC Sydney and Cockle Bay, Darling Harbour' is linked here: Cockle Bay Room is an official ICC meeting room, and the boat show also occupies the Darling Harbour foreshore, which is not recorded as a separate venue. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin. Australia has no current official government hotel star scheme, and this is not a hotel, so star_rating is null.
