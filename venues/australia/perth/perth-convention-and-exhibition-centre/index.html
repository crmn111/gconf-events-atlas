About

Perth’s purpose-built convention and exhibition centre on the Swan River. Official exhibitions page: six pavilions can be combined into up to 16,644 square metres of uninterrupted floor space, accommodating over 10,000 people; exhibition_sqm is 16,644 and capacity_max_delegates is 10,000, with the official wording ‘over 10,000 people’ noted. Official FAQ/our-spaces text: the Riverside Theatre seats up to 2,500 in a tiered configuration and is the largest tiered theatre in Perth; the campus also includes the BelleVue Ballroom and 23 meeting rooms. Official pages locate the main entrance on Mounts Bay Road, Perth, but do not publish a street number in the text captured for this pass, so the stored address is that official road description only. No precise Nominatim pin was stored in this pass; lat/lon left null rather than using an imprecise city pin. Public sales email used is info@pcec.com.au (named staff mailboxes on the same pages were not stored).
