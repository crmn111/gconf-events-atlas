About

Victoria’s principal convention and exhibition centre at South Wharf. Official address on the 2026 Floor Plans and Space Capacities Guide is 1 Convention Centre Place, South Wharf; some atlas event strings use 2 Clarendon Street, which is an adjoining frontage of the same campus and is not recorded as a second venue. exhibition_sqm follows the current official exhibitions webpage (40,000 sqm pillarless). The official 2026 capacity guide, whose footer is dated 31 March 2026, instead states 39,000 sqm of pillar-less Exhibition Bays; that figure is kept on the Exhibition Bays space and is not averaged with the webpage. capacity_max_delegates is the official Entire Plenary theatre figure of 5,564 from that guide (AIPC and other third-party sheets quoting 5,500 were not used). A virtual-tour line on official material mentions 70,000 square metres of space; that is a campus-wide marketing figure, not the exhibition total. Contact email is the public sales address on the official guide. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
