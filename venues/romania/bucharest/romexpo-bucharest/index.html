About

Bucharest exhibition centre officially at 65-67 Mărăști Blvd, Bucharest 011465, with public email. Official Central Hall (A) page: 17,250 m². Official B halls page: B1 exhibition area 11,750 m² and B2 15,222 m². Those official exhibition areas sum to 44,222 m², stored as exhibition_sqm; other pavilions were not added because official areas were not copied in this pass. Used by GoTech World and Gaudeamus Bucharest (Pavilion B2). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin; Nominatim did not return a named pin in this pass. Romania is not a Hotelstars Union member in the retrieved membership list, and no official star listing was applied.
