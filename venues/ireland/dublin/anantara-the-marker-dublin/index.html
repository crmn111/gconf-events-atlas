About

Dublin conference hotel officially listed on Fáilte Ireland’s Q4 2026 registered hotels register as Anantara The Marker Dublin Hotel, 5 Star, Grand Canal Square, Docklands, Dublin 2, D02 CK38. Official hotel and meetings pages: nine meeting and event spaces across 700 sqm, with capacity for up to 350 guests, stored as capacity_max_delegates. Official Marker Suite: 336 sq.m (28.0 × 12.0 × 4.7 m), theatre 300, classroom 180; divisible into Marker Suite 1 and 2 of 168 sq.m each (theatre 150 and 130). Coordinates are Nominatim’s hotel pin.
