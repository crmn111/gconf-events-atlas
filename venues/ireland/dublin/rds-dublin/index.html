About

Royal Dublin Society campus in Ballsbridge, officially at Merrion Road, Ballsbridge, Dublin 4, D04 AK80. Official Simmonscourt pages describe Hall 8 (five halls, 8A–8E) as the largest complex, with over 10,000 sq m and 6,500 theatre-style, stored as capacity_max_delegates. Official individual areas: Hall 8A 1,990 m², 8B 2,145 m², 8C 4,754 m², 8D 864 m² and 8E 1,094 m², which sum to 10,847 m², stored as exhibition_sqm for the Simmonscourt exhibition complex (consistent with “over 10,000 sq m”). Other official halls such as Hall 1 (Main Hall) 3,503 m² are additional and are not included in that Simmonscourt total. Official Hall 8C theatre capacity is also 6,500.
