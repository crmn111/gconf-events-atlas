About

Dublin’s purpose-built convention centre officially at Spencer Dock, North Wall Quay, Dublin 1, D01 T1W6. Official exhibition-space page: The Forum 2,721 m² (theatre 3,597, banquet 2,020), stored as capacity_max_delegates; The Liffey 1,650 m² (theatre 2,151, banquet 1,290), divisible into Liffey A 746 m² (710 conference delegates / theatre 750) and Liffey B 897 m² (1,002 conference delegates). Exhibition_sqm 4,371 is the sum of the official Forum and Liffey areas; no official six-hall campus total was published on the retrieved page.
