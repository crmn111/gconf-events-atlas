About

Dublin conference hotel officially listed on Fáilte Ireland’s Q4 2026 registered hotels register as The Morrison Dublin, Curio Collection by Hilton, 5 Star, 15-19 Ormond Quay Lower, Dublin 1, D01 K5X5. Official meetings page: meeting and conference spaces for 2 to 40 delegates, dinners or lunches for up to 50, and receptions for up to 70 guests in Halo, stored as capacity_max_delegates; The Georgian Room holds up to 40 delegates theatre-style; The Morrison Boardroom holds up to 8 delegates. Official events email events@morrisonhotel.ie. Coordinates are Nominatim’s hotel pin.
