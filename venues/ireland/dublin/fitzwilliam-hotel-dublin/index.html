About

Dublin conference hotel officially listed on Fáilte Ireland’s Q4 2026 registered hotels register as Fitzwilliam Hotel, 5 Star, 127/128 Saint Stephen's Green, Dublin 2, D02 HE18, matching the official hotel site address. Official meetings page: The Fitzwilliam Room is the largest space, 82.8 m², theatre 74, classroom 20, banquet 60, stored as capacity_max_delegates 74; The Pembroke Room 57.63 m² / theatre 40; The Emmet Room 30.59 m² / boardroom 10.
