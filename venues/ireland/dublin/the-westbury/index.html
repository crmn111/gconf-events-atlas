About

Dublin conference hotel officially listed on Fáilte Ireland’s Q4 2026 registered hotels register as The Westbury Hotel, 5 Star, Balfe Street, Dublin 2, D02 CH66. Official 2026 fact sheet: 11 event spaces and a maximum capacity of 200, stored as capacity_max_delegates, with the postal address given there as Grafton Street, Dublin 2, D02 CH66; the Fáilte register street (Balfe Street) is stored, and the fact-sheet Grafton Street wording is noted. Official Grafton Suite page: up to 200 guests theatre-style or 150 for banqueting. Official emails and. Coordinates are Nominatim’s hotel pin.
