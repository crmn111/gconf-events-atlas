About

Dublin conference hotel officially listed on Fáilte Ireland’s Q4 2026 registered hotels register as Dylan Hotel, 5 Star, Eastmoreland Place, Dublin 4, D04 W521, matching the official meetings-page address and email. Official meetings page publishes four named event rooms (The Library, The Eddison, The Nurserie and The Ruby Room) for meetings, private dining and receptions; individual official capacities were not copied from the retrieved page. Coordinates are Nominatim’s hotel pin (Nominatim’s eircode label was D04 V210; the official Fáilte and hotel address D04 W521 is stored).
