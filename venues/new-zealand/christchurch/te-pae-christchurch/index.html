About

Christchurch convention centre at the official contact address 188 Oxford Terrace. exhibition_sqm is 2,845, the official Halls 1–4 floor area (the same official page also rounds this as 2,800 sqm). capacity_max_delegates is 3,600, the official exhibition-hall capacity when Dobson rooms are included, matching the official capacity-table theatre figure for Halls 1–4 including Dobson 1–4. Official event-enquiry email addresses are hidden in the contact-page script, so contact_email is null. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
