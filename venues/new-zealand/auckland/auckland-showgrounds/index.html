About

Auckland exhibition grounds at the official footer address 217 Greenlane Road West, Epsom, Auckland, described on the official site as sitting by Cornwall Park in Greenlane. The official 'over 18,000 sqm' figure is indoor and outdoor combined, so exhibition_sqm is null. A people-capacity headline was not published on the homepage captured here, so capacity_max_delegates is null. Official contact. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches the Nominatim pin. Both Auckland Armageddon Expo editions and the Hutchwilco NZ Boat Show are linked here.
