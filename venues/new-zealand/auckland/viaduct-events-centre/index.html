About

Waterfront events centre in Wynyard Quarter at the official address 161 Halsey Street. The atlas lists the Auckland Boat Show at Viaduct Events Centre, Jellicoe Harbour and Wynyard Wharf; only the named events centre is recorded here, with the harbour and wharf noted as additional outdoor sites rather than combined into one invented venue. Official contact. The atlas pin is not marked precise; coordinates are the Nominatim conference-centre pin for 161 Halsey Street, which matches that atlas point.
