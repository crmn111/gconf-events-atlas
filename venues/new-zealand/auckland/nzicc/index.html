Official about page: footprint over 30,000 sqm and 32,500 sqm gross floor area; 33 meeting spaces; 2,852-seat theatre (divisible into two 1,235-seat theatres); large events up to 4,500. Headline exhibition_sqm is Ariki Hall 6,708 sqm, not the 32,500 sqm GFA.

About

Auckland international convention centre, officially opened in February 2026 according to the official media centre, and included as New Zealand's largest remaining purpose-built centre. Official address 101 Hobson Street, Auckland Central, between Hobson Street and Nelson Street. exhibition_sqm is 6,708, official Ariki Hall. capacity_max_delegates is 4,500, the official Ariki Hall / large-events figure; official opening copy also cites more than 4,000 delegates and a 3,150 convention capacity, which are noted rather than averaged.
