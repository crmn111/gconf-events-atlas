About

Osaka’s international exhibition centre (Intex Osaka), named for Japan IT Week Osaka 2027 and also used for festival events including AnimeJapan 2027 and Radio Crazy 2026. Official about/pamphlet URLs did not respond in this research pass, so exhibition_sqm and people capacities are left null rather than taken from unofficial directories or from an unverified secondary citation of 70,078 m². Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches the Nominatim named conference-centre pin for インテックス大阪. A detailed official street address was not captured from a live official page in this pass.
