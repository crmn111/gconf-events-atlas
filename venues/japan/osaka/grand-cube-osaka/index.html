About

Osaka International Convention Center (Grand Cube Osaka) on Nakanoshima, named for EDIX Osaka 2027. Official English facility/capacity figures were not extracted from a successfully parsed official English facility page in this pass (the official site loaded, but the captured homepage did not publish hall areas or theatre totals in the text used here), so capacity and exhibition fields are null. Official pages place the venue in Nakanoshima; a street number was not confirmed from that captured text. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, which matches the Nominatim named conference-centre pin for 大阪府立国際会議場.
