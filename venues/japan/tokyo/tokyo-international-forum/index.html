About

Tokyo Metropolitan Government convention and arts centre in Marunouchi, administered by Tokyo International Forum Co., Ltd. Official address: 5-1 Marunouchi 3-chome, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo. Official overview also gives a site area of approximately 27,000 m² and total floor area of approximately 145,000 m²; those campus/building figures are not used as exhibition_sqm. capacity_max_delegates is the official Hall A seating figure of 5,012. Coordinates are the Nominatim named pin for 東京国際フォーラム.
