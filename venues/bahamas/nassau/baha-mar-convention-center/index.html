About

Official meetings page: 'Operated by Grand Hyatt, the Baha Mar Convention Center'. Official copy also markets 'over 300,000 sq ft of luxurious indoor and outdoor meeting space' and '200,000 square feet of flexible indoor and outdoor spaces' — those combined indoor/outdoor totals are not stored as exhibition_sqm. The largest officially labelled theatre figure retrieved is the Baha Mar Grand Ballroom at 2,940 (stored as capacity_max_delegates). An unnamed 40,000 sq.ft space is published with an unlabelled 4,000-people figure; that is not used as a campus maximum because the layout is unlabelled and the room is unnamed in the extract. Official address on the retrieved pages is given as Nassau, The Bahamas (no street line was clearly published on the meetings page). Areas converted from official sq.ft. Official bahamar.com pages publish a Google Maps place link for ‘The Baha Mar Casino & Hotel’ at 25.0702748, -77.3968784 (!8m2 pin). Those official resort coordinates are stored, with the caveat that the pin is labelled for the Casino & Hotel on the same campus, not a separate convention-centre doorway.
