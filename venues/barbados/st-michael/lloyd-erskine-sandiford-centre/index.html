About

Official site: the largest stand-alone conference facility in Barbados (formerly Sherbourne Conference Centre), managed by Barbados Conference Services Limited. Official address Two Mile Hill, St. Michael, BB11093. Official venue page: 11 meeting rooms ‘with a maximum capacity of 1400 in the largest room’ (stored as capacity_max_delegates); ‘70,000 sq. ft. exhibition space’ (converted and stored as exhibition_sqm). Official ‘Over 100,000 sq. ft. of air-conditioned usable floor space’ is an ‘over’ figure and is not stored. A room-by-room official capacity table was not retrieved, so spaces[] is empty pending a labelled chart. Official Google embeds on the site use a wide (~10 km) viewport and are not stored as a building-level pin.
