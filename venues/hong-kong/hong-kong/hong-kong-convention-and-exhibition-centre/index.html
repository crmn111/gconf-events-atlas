About

Wan Chai convention and exhibition centre (HKCEC). Official exhibitions page: more than 91,500 sqm of rentable space, including six purpose-built exhibition halls totalling 66,000 sqm, stored as exhibition_sqm. The 91,500 sqm figure is rentable space, not stored as exhibition area. Official Hall 1 is 19,890 sqm, divisible into Halls 1A–1E. Official Hall 3B–3E is 14,982 sqm, divisible into four halls. A published campus delegate maximum and a complete official street address were not copied from the retrieved official pages (the contact page returned 403). Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching the Nominatim exhibition-centre pin.
