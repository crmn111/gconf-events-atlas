About

Exhibition, convention and events venue at Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau. Official copy offers more than 70,000 sqm of exhibition space across 10 ground-level, column-free halls; that “more than 70,000” headline is not stored as an exact exhibition_sqm. Official capacity table: AsiaWorld-Arena (Hall 1) 10,880 sqm / theatre 12,500 or 14,000 with standing zone; AsiaWorld-Summit (Hall 2) 8,200 sqm / theatre 5,000; Runway 11 4,400 sqm / theatre 3,800; Halls 6, 8 and 10 combined 17,040 sqm / theatre 8,400; typical Halls 3–11 3,500–45,440 sqm / theatre 2,800–22,400. The official 22,400 upper figure for connected typical halls is stored as capacity_max_delegates. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching the Nominatim exhibition-centre pin (not the station or bus-stop pins of the same name).
