About

Official Uzexpocentre NEC (АО НВК «Узэкспоцентр») English homepage lists pavilion and hall areas in kv.m (square metres). Indoor pavilion areas are published individually and are not summed here because no official combined indoor exhibition total was found. The cinema-lecture hall is officially 600 kv.m / 370 seats; that 370-seat figure is stored on the space and is not treated as a campus maximum. Official outdoor area 7,000 kv.m is not included in indoor exhibition_sqm. Official contact address 107 Amir Temur str., Tashkent, 100084;. Coordinates are from the official site's Google Maps pin (/@41.3368077,69.2752235).
