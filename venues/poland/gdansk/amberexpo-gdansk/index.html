About

Gdańsk exhibition and congress centre, published as the venue for Infoshare 2027. Official pages state five halls with a total area of 16,130 m² (divisible operating spaces from 1,000 m² to 16,130 m²), five conference rooms, and a conference-centre foyer of 2,030 m² for 1,242 participants. The official address is ul. Żaglowa 11, 80-560 Gdańsk. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
