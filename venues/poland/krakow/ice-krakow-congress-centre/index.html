About

Kraków's purpose-built congress centre. Official about pages list the Auditorium Hall (S1) at 1,915 people, Theatre Hall (S2) at 594, Chamber Hall (S3) at 400 and the Conference Hall Complex (S4) at 350, with a glazed three-storey foyer of 2,650 m² and comfortable space for a total of 3,200 visitors. The official address is ul. Marii Konopnickiej 17, 30-302 Kraków. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin (the two atlas address variants for this centre were merged).
