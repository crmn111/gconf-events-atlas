About

Warsaw exhibition and conference venue at ul. Prądzyńskiego 12/14. Official technical-data pages give Hall 1 5,250 m², Hall 3 3,600 m² and Hall 4 3,600 m², with a conference floor in Hall 2 and a paved outdoor exhibition area of up to 2,000 m². A single official indoor total was not copied from the technical-data page (Hall 2 has no published area there), so exhibition_sqm is left null and the hall figures are stored in spaces. The published sales address is. The two atlas address variants were merged. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
