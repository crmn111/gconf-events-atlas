About

High-rise hotel in central Warsaw with a published official ballroom and meetings offer. Official IHG Poland ballroom pages describe the Opera Ballroom as 438 m² (41 by 12 metres) and able to accommodate up to 500 guests for large-scale events, galas and congresses.
