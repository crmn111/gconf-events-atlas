About

Large exhibition complex in Nadarzyn, near Warsaw. Official about-complex pages state 153,000 m² of exhibition space in six pavilions and 500,000 m² outdoors. Detailed official rentable areas are published for Halls B–F (B 20,535 m², C 20,588 m², D 20,288 m², E 17,210 m², F 21,139 m²); those five halls sum to less than the 153,000 m² headline, and Hall A is not given a full rentable-area breakdown on the same page, so the 153,000 official total is stored for the estate. The Poland Boat Show is linked here because the atlas places it at this complex. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin.
