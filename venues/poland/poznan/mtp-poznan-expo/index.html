About

Poland's historic international fair grounds in Poznań, including the Poznań Congress Center on the same official address. Official MTP venue pages state 150,000 m² of exhibition space. Atlas conference and expo events at both the Expo and the Congress Center are merged into this single record. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin for the Głogowska 10 complex.
