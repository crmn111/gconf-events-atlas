About

Budapest exhibition and congress campus officially at Albertirsai út 10, 1101 Budapest, with public email. Official hosted-events page: indoor event area of 75,000 sqm (including a 15,000 sqm Congress Centre) and congresses of up to 15,000 delegates (stored as capacity_max_delegates). Official current halls page gross areas: Hall A 19,528; B 6,536; D 5,240; E 7,231; F 3,838; G 9,504; H 5,650 sqm, summing to 57,527 m², stored as exhibition_sqm (the 75,000 figure is broader covered event space). Used by Educatio Expo, Mondocon Winter and the Budapest Boat Show. Coordinates are the atlas precise pin, matching Nominatim’s exhibition-centre pin.
