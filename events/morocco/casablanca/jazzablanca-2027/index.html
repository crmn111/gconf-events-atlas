About

20th anniversary edition. Official home page (fetched 24 Nov 2026): 'Rendez-vous du 1er au 10 Juillet 2027 pour fêter les 20 ans de Jazzablanca.' Official history and experience pages place the festival at Anfa Park from 2022 onward (with an additional free stage historically at Place des Nations-Unies). The official history page does not publish a 2026 attendance total. Morocco dates are local (Africa/Casablanca; typically UTC+1, with occasional Ramadan clock changes).

Topics: jazz, soul, world music, live music.
