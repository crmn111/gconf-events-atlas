About

Dates are local civil dates for the official 9-23 January 2027 window. The event starts in Loulé-Faro, Portugal, then runs almost entirely in Morocco and finishes in Marrakech; the pin is the official finish city because Morocco hosts 13 of 15 days. Country is Morocco (Africa), not Saudi Arabia.

Topics: rally raid, Dakar Rally, World Rally-Raid Championship.
