About

Fifth anniversary edition, billed as the largest tech and startups event in Africa. Official title and dates: 26–28 April 2027, Marrakech. Venue on official plan-your-trip and getting-there pages is Place Bab Jdid, Boulevard Al Yarmouk, opposite La Mamounia Hotel, with no on-site parking. Organised by Kaoun International under the authority of Morocco’s Ministry of Digital Transition, in partnership with the Agence de Développement du Digital. Morocco dates are local (Africa/Casablanca; typically UTC+1, with occasional Ramadan clock changes).

Topics: artificial intelligence, startups, digital economy, connectivity, smart cities.

Official GITEX AFRICA homepage (fetched 24 Nov 2026) lists '2027 1,600+ Exhibitors & Startups 60,000 Visitors & Buyers' (2026 edition: 1,500+ exhibitors and 55,000 visitors & buyers). Used the official 2027 visitor figure.
