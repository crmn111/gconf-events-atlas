Official season is 17 January–10 February 2027. Highlight days published on the same official Visit Rijeka pages: Queen of the Rijeka Carnival 22 January, Children’s Carnival Parade 30 January, International Carnival Parade 7 February. Dates are local to the host city (Central European Time, UTC+1 / UTC+2 in summer).
