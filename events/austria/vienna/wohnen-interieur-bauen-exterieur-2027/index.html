About

Dates are local to the host city. Official umbrella: from 2027 the two shows are held together at Messe Wien, Halls A & B. Hours 10–13 March 10:00–18:00 and 14 March 10:00–17:00. Official: free admission on Wednesday and Thursday. The related WIM – Wiener Immobilien Messe (13–14 March) is a third official platform with a ticket combination and was not folded into this record. Children under 14 only with an accompanying adult trade visitor.

Topics: interiors, furniture, building, renovation, energy.
