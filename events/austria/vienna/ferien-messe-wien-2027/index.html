About

Dates are local to the host city. Hours: 14–16 January 10:00–18:00 and 17 January 10:00–17:00. Official visit page also lists co-located Vienna Drive (mobility show) at a different foyer of the same complex, with a combined ÖBB ticket; Vienna Drive is noted here and was not staged as a second record. Children under 14 only with an adult. A standalone corporate organiser line was not printed on the visit page; contacts are the Messe Wien team (same numbering pattern as other VIECON fairs).
