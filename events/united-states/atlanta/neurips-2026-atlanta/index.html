About

NeurIPS 2026 is multi-site: Sydney is the main site (6-12 Dec); Atlanta and Paris are satellite sites (9-13 Dec). Only the Atlanta site is listed here. US dates are local to the host city. Official NeurIPS 2026 Atlanta at the Conference page (and the official 2026 Venue Information page) names the Georgia World Congress Center, 285 Andrew Young International Blvd NW, Atlanta, GA 30313 (Exhibition Hall C2).
