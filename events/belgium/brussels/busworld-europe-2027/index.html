About

Dates are local to the host city. Official FAQ: 16–20 October 10:00–19:00 and 21 October 10:00–18:00, Brussels Expo, Belgiëplein 1, 1020 Brussels. Children under 16 admitted free; adult ticket prices were not listed on the FAQ. Official 2025 actual: 45,427 visitors. Same calendar week as Prowood in Ghent, a different event and city.

Topics: buses, coaches, public transport, mobility.

Official 2025 actual: 45,427 visitors from 116 countries (and 559 exhibitors), from the official Busworld Europe 2027 preview news dated 15 October 2026.
