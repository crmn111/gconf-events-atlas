About

Two official weekends at De Schorre: 16–18 July and 23–25 July 2027. start_date/end_date span both weekends; it is not a continuous ten-day run. Pre-registration opens 2 December 2026 at 15:00 CET. Dates are local to the host city (Central European Time, UTC+1 / UTC+2 in summer).

Topics: electronic, EDM, dance.

Official Tomorrowland Belgium press page describes the festival as welcoming “over 400,000 People of Tomorrow … across two extraordinary summer weekends.” That is standing about-the-festival wording, not a clearly worded 2027 expected-attendance figure, so it is not stored as organiser attendance.
