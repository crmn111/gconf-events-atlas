About

Dates are local to the host city. Official GR Ljubljana current-events calendar: Alpe-Adria Fair and GASTexpo HoReCa Fairs of Flavors both run 2–5 February 2027 at Gospodarsko razstavišče, Dunajska cesta 18, Ljubljana, and are folded into this umbrella record. Opening hours, ticket prices and a labelled previous-edition attendance figure were not listed on the official current-events fetch. Same venue as slovenian-book-fair-2026 and na-meji-nevidnega-2027, different events. The official Dom Fair (9–13 March 2027, same venue) was looked at and not added, to avoid padding this smaller market.

Topics: tourism, hospitality, gastronomy, HoReCa.
