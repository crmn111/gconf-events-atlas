About

The official site describes it as the biggest boating event in Northern Europe, a 10-day indoor show. The Helsinki Outdoor & Hunting Expo (12-14 Feb) and Nordic Marine Expo (19-21 Feb) run alongside.

Topics: boat show, yachting, marine.
