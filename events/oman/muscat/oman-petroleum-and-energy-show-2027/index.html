About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 26–28 April 2027 at the Oman Convention & Exhibition Centre, Muscat. Official OCEC venue listing: 09:00–18:00, exhibition halls 2 and 5. Officially co-located with Oman Sustainability Week, folded into this record. Official OPES 2026 in numbers: 41,691 visits (stored as visits, not unique visitors), 207 exhibitors, 18 countries. Held under the patronage of the Ministry of Energy and Minerals. A longer legal organiser name beyond the official contact / Connect was not clearly labelled. COMEX 2027 (1–4 February, same venue) is already in the Atlas and was not re-added. Hours on the official COMEX show site (10:00–21:00) differ from the official OCEC COMEX listing (10:00–19:00); that existing record was not edited here.

Topics: oil, gas, energy, sustainability.
