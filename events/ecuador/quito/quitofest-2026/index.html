About

Free municipal festival of independent music held during the Fiestas de Quito: Quitofest Rock (Fri 4 Dec), Música Nacional (Sat 5 Dec) and Alternativo (Sun 6 Dec). Line-up includes Lolabum, Machaka, Papaya Dada, Margarita Laso and Pakul. Ecuador dates are local (mainland Ecuador time, UTC-5 all year, no daylight saving).

Topics: rock, independent music, Ecuadorian music, alternative.
