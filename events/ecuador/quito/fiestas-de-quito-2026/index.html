About

Annual festivities for the city's foundation (6 December): the official agenda includes the Desfile de la Confraternidad (6 Dec, Av. Amazonas), the Sesión Solemne (6 Dec), the Mascarada Nocturna (7 Dec), the Mundial del 40, pasacalle festivals and QuitoFest (listed separately as quitofest-2026). Ecuador dates are local (mainland Ecuador time, UTC-5 all year, no daylight saving).

Topics: city foundation festivities, parades, theatre, music, neighbourhood fiestas.
