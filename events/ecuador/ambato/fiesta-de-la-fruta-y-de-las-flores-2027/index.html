About

Ambato's carnival-season festival (Carnaval 2027 falls on 8-9 Feb). La Hora (19 Oct 2026) reports preparations for the 76th edition. A non-official portal (viveambato.com) lists 5-9 Feb 2027; not used.

Topics: parade, floats, folklore, music, flowers.
