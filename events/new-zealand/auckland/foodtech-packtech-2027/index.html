About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 21–23 September 2027 at Auckland Showgrounds, 217 Green Lane West, Epsom. Official hours: Tuesday 21 September 09:00–17:00; Wednesday 22 September 09:00–17:00; Thursday 23 September 09:00–16:00. Badge registration; no ticket price was listed. Held biennially. A corporate organiser name is referred to in the official FAQ as 'XPO events'; the longer legal entity was not clearly labelled. Same venue as armageddon-expo-auckland-winter-2027 / spring-2027 and hutchwilco-nz-boat-show-2027, different events. The official Auckland Home Show (9–12 September 2027, same venue) was looked at and not padded in.

Topics: food manufacturing, packaging, processing, food safety, automation.
