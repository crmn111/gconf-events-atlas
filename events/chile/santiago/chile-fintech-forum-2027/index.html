About

Billed as Chile's largest financial-technology event; covers crypto assets and open finance. Chile dates are local (mainland Chile time: UTC-3 in the southern summer, UTC-4 in winter; Easter Island is two hours behind the mainland).

Topics: fintech, open finance, payments, AI, cybersecurity, crypto assets.

No organiser attendance figure found on the official pages checked (24 Nov 2026). Ranked by an estimated size tier 'medium' (about 1,000-10,000 attendees; our judgement from format, venue and scale, not an official number).
