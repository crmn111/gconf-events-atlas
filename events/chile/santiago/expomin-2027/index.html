About

Dates are local to the host city. Official tickets page and official 2027 brochure: 20–24 April 2027 at Espacio Riesco, Av. El Salto 5000, Huechuraba, Santiago. Opening hours and on-page ticket amounts were not extracted from the tickets page (the shop widget did not yield prices in the text fetch) and are not stored. Official brochure 'KEY FIGURES 2025': 83,498 visitors, source given as the Expomin 2025 exhibitors and visitors survey — stored as the previous-edition actual (the show is on a 2025/2027 cycle). Same venue as comic-con-chile-2027 and chile-fintech-forum-2027, different events. FIDAE (next official edition 4–9 April 2028) is an air/defence show and was skipped.

Topics: mining, mineral processing, mining equipment.
