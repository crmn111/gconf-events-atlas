About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 30 June–3 July 2027, exclusively at MITEC (26th edition), an event of the Kuala Lumpur Architecture Festival. Official organiser marketing kit: 10:00–19:00. Official 2026 kit figures include 860 exhibitors; a clear labelled total-visitor figure was not stored (the kit breaks out 'over 22,780 industry specifiers' and 'over 4,480 international attendees' as separate lines). Ticket prices were not listed. Same venue as several existing Atlas tech expos (semicon-southeast-asia-2027, smart-nation-expo-2027, and others), different event.

Topics: architecture, interior design, building, construction.
