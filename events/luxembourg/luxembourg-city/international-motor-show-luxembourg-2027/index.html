About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: Friday 12–Sunday 14 November 2027 at Luxexpo The Box, open to the public. Organised in odd-numbered years by Pole Position. Opening hours and ticket prices were not listed on the official 2027 preview article. A prestigious themed exhibition and gala evening is scheduled for Thursday 11 November; that evening is described as a backdrop event and is not stored as a show day. Same venue as nexus-luxembourg-2027 (March), different event.

Topics: cars, motorcycles, motorsport, automotive.
