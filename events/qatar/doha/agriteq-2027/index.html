About

Dates are local to the host city. Official AgriteQ exhibitor portal: 14–18 January 2027 at Katara Cultural Village (14th edition). Official English visit/FAQ pages describe the 14th edition but did not repeat the dates in the text fetched; dates and venue are taken from the official exhibitor-registration portal. Opening hours and a labelled previous-edition visitor figure were not listed in the pages fetched. The official home page names the Ministry of Municipality as organiser, in partnership with public and private bodies. Visit Qatar FAQ text that dates and venue 'will be announced' was not used, because it contradicted the official AgriteQ portal. Family and trade audiences are both described; the record is not marked trade-only.

Topics: agriculture, food security, livestock, fisheries, food industry.
