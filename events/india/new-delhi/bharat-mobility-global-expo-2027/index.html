About

Dates are local to the host city. Official platform spans two Delhi venues: Bharat Mandapam 4–9 February (including Auto Expo – The Motor Show, India International Tyre Show, Bharat Battery Show, India Cycle Show and pavilions) and Yashobhoomi, Dwarka, 4–7 February (Components & Technology Show and other official components), all folded into this record. Media Day 4 February; Media + Dealers' Day 5 February at Bharat Mandapam. A leftover 2025 India Expo Mart block on the official page was ignored. On-page 'Lakh Visitors' counters rendered without a usable figure.

Topics: automotive, mobility, auto components, electric vehicles.
