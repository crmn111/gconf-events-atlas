About

Official Visit Rwanda festivals page (fetched 24 Nov 2026) describes KigaliUP as Rwanda’s premiere music festival, held every year usually during the northern summer months, with 'thousands of fans' — a qualitative phrase, not used as an organiser headcount. Official kigaliup.rw / kigaliup.com did not respond from this box and no 2027 (or remaining 2026) dates were published on the official tourism page. Record is dates TBA. Rwanda dates are local (Central Africa Time, CAT, Africa/Kigali, UTC+2, no daylight saving).

Topics: live music, reggae, hip-hop, world music.

No organiser attendance figure found on the official pages checked (24 Nov 2026). Ranked by an estimated size tier 'medium' (our judgement from format, venue and scale, not an official number).
