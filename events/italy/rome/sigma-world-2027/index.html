About

Dates are local to the host city. Official SiGMA World page, official Global Tour and official 5–10 November 2026 SiGMA news: 24–27 May 2027 at Fiera Roma, Via Portuense 1645 (East Entrance), third edition in Rome (moved to May after the 2026 edition). Officially one event and four communities: SiGMA, AIBC, FX World and AGS share the Fiera Roma site, with dedicated exhibition zones; AIBC World (aibc.world/rome/) now also lists 24–27 May 2027 at the same venue and is folded in. Official agenda: exhibition 10:00–18:00 on exhibition days and conference 11:00–16:00; 24 May includes a delegates-arrival programme. Official SiGMA news (10 November 2026) stated the previous (2026) Rome edition brought together 31,000 delegates — stored as the previous-edition actual. The 2027 line '35,000 expected' / 'connect with 35,000 delegates' is a forecast and was not stored. Leftover 'SiGMA Central Europe Speakers' and 'This November' wording on sister pages was not used for dates. Conference + expo ('both'): category tech, also_categories expo.

Topics: igaming, gaming, AI, fintech, affiliates.

Official SiGMA news, 10 November 2026: the second edition of SiGMA World in Rome (immediately preceding this announcement) 'bringing together 31,000 delegates'. Stored as the previous-edition official actual. The 2027 '35,000 expected' figure in the same article is a forecast and was not stored.
