About

Official AI Salon Gdańsk #2 on Tuesday 1 December 2026, 17:30–21:00 at STARTER, Lęborska 3b, with live AI product demos (Specsight, PilotGo) and an open stage. The series is the Gdańsk chapter of the global AI Salon community, organised by Patryk Raba and powered by vibecoding.pl. Free, registration required.
