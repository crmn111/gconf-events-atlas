About

Dates are local to the host city. Co-located exhibitions folded: AUTOMA, MACH-TOOL, SURFEX, WELDING, FOCAST, plus DREMA and Subcontracting (same dates, official visitor page). Professionals admitted free.

Topics: industry, machine tools, automation, robotics, metalworking.

Official about page: 12,518 industry professionals at the 2026 edition (500+ exhibitors from 27 countries). Not a 2027 forecast.
