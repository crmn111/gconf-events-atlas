About

Official site title and 'SHOW DAYS | 5-7 OCT 2027'. Official day breakdown: 5 Oct 2027, Lagos, Government Leadership & AI Summit; 6–7 Oct 2027, Landmark Centre, Lagos, Tech Expo & Startup Festival. The same homepage still also contains leftover 2026 dates (31 Aug–3 Sep 2026, now past) and a residual '28 - 30 SEP 2027' line that conflicts with the 5–7 Oct 2027 show days; 5–7 Oct is used because it is the page title and the detailed 2027 day-by-day breakdown.
