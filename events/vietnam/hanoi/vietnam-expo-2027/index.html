About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 13–15 May 2027, 36th Vietnam International Trade Fair, at the Vietnam Exhibition Center (VEC), Đông Anh, Hanoi. Official visitor hours 09:00–17:00; official opening ceremony 09:00 on 13 May, hosted by the Ministry of Industry and Trade, directed by VIETRADE, organised by VINEXAD JSC. Forecast wording ('expected scale of over 10,000 m²', 'more than 420 exhibitors', 'expected to welcome over 20,000 visitors... over the four days') was not stored; the dated run is three days (13–15 May), and that 'four days' line is noted as an inconsistency. Ticket prices were not listed. Different event and city from smart-tech-vietnam-2027 (6 May, SECC, Ho Chi Minh City) and vietnam-ictcomm-2027 (29 June, SECC).

Topics: trade, export, investment, food, household electronics.
