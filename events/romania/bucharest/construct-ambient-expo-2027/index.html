About

Dates are local to the host city. Official ROMEXPO 2027 Exhibition Calendar (last updated 1 October 2026): 15–18 April 2027 at ROMEXPO, Bd. Mărăști 65-67, Bucharest. Official concurrent Romtherm (installations and heating, cooling and air-conditioning) on the same dates is folded into this record. Official calendar also lists Mobila Expo (furniture fair with on-site sales) on the same dates; it is noted as concurrent but not folded. Opening hours and ticket prices for 2027 were not listed on the official calendar. Same venue as gotech-world-2027 (November) and the staged Indagra 2027 record (October), different events.

Topics: construction, building materials, interior design, HVAC.
