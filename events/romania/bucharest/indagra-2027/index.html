About

Dates are local to the host city. Official ROMEXPO 2027 Exhibition Calendar (last updated 1 October 2026): 27–31 October 2027 at ROMEXPO. Official concurrent Indagra Food (international food industry fair) on the same dates is folded into this record. The official Indagra visitor-info page still carried leftover 28 October–1 November 2026 hours (09:00–18:00, last day 09:00–16:00) and a 30 lei/day ticket; those leftover 2026 hours and prices were not stored for 2027. Same venue as the staged Construct Ambient Expo 2027 record and as gotech-world-2027, different events.

Topics: agriculture, horticulture, viticulture, animal husbandry, food industry.
