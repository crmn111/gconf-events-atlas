About

Official tickets page is titled still “GoTech World 2026 Tickets” in the HTML title, but the body is “Get Your Pass for GoTech World 2027” with “Standard Pass - 2027” and “Date: 10 - 11 November” plus the Romexpo address. The 2026 homepage dates (10–11 November 2026) were not used. Dates are local to the host city (Eastern European Time, UTC+2 / UTC+3 in summer).

Topics: IT, AI, cybersecurity, digital transformation.

Official 2027 ticket types include the marketing line “Networking with 15.000+ visitors.” The homepage’s “15,000+ Business Visitors” is standing about-the-event copy (and the homepage itself still presents the ended 2026 edition). Neither is a clearly worded 2027 expected-attendance sentence, so the figure is not stored as organiser attendance. Ranked by our estimated size tier 'large' (30,000–100,000).
