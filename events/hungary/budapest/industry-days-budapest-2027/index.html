About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 4–7 May 2027 at HUNGEXPO, the 14th INDUSTRY DAYS. Official co-event AUTOMOTIVE HUNGARY is folded into this record. MACH-TECH was named as a 2026 co-event in the throwback and is not listed as a 2027 co-event on the official 2027 header, so it was not folded. Opening hours and ticket prices were not listed. The 2026 recap labels 370 exhibitors from 22 countries but no 2026 visitor figure; a 2025 recap of 11,000 trade visitors was not stored because 2026 is the previous edition. The official 2027 page says defence industry and an Economic Forum will be introduced as new thematic areas of this industrial exhibition. Slug is industry-days-budapest-2027 because industry-days-2027 is already used in the Atlas for a different event (Industry Days by CfC St. Moritz, crypto).

Topics: industry, manufacturing, automotive suppliers, automation.
