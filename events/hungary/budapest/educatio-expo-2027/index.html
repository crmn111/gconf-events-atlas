About

27th Educatio Expo. Official page: Hungary’s largest higher education event; free entry with registration. Dates are local to the host city (Central European Time, UTC+1 / UTC+2 in summer).

Topics: higher education, admissions, career guidance, edtech.

Official 2027 page uses standing “TRADE FAIR IN NUMBERS” copy of “60 thousand visitors.” That is not a clearly worded 2027 expected-attendance sentence, so it is not stored as organiser attendance. Ranked by our estimated size tier 'large' (30,000–100,000).
