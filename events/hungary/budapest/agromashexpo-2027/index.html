About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 27–30 January 2027 at the HUNGEXPO Budapest Congress and Exhibition Centre, Albertirsai út 10. Official 2027 event page does not list opening hours, ticket prices, or a labelled previous-edition visitor figure. The throwback refers to the 44th AGROmashEXPO and AgrárgépShow (21–24 January, implied 2026) but does not officially name AgrárgépShow as a 2027 co-event, so it was not folded. Same venue as educatio-expo-2027 (14 January) and budapest-boat-show-2027 (11–14 February), different events.

Topics: agriculture, agricultural machinery, viticulture, livestock.
