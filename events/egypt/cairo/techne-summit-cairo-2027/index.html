About

Official 2027 homepage header (fetched 24 Nov 2026): '25 - 26 Sep 2027 Ghurnata' with a 'Techne Summit Cairo, happening this September' section. Ghurnata is a Heliopolis community venue (used for the 2026 Cairo edition), not Granada, Spain. The page also quotes a combined 55,000+ attendees figure covering Techne’s city editions; that combined figure is not applied to the Cairo record. Egypt dates are local (Africa/Cairo).
