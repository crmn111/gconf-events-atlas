About

Dates are local to the host city. Official Fi Africa page: 1–3 June 2027 at the Egypt International Exhibition Center (EIEC), Cairo. Officially co-located with ProPak MENA; ProPak MENA is folded into this record. Official hours listed for 1 June 10:00–19:00 and 2 June 10:00–19:00; hours for 3 June were not in the page extract and are not stored. A '20000+' counter on the page was unlabelled in the extract (and sat near 'exhibiting companies') and was not stored as attendance. Official contact: fiandpropakcs@informa.com.
