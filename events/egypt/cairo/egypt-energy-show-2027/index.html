About

Dates are local to the host city. Official homepage: 30 March–1 April 2027 at the Egypt International Exhibition Centre, Cairo (held under the patronage of the President of Egypt). Official hours published for Wednesday 31 March 10:00–19:00 and Thursday 1 April 10:00–18:00; hours for the first day (Tuesday 30 March) were not listed in the homepage extract and are not stored. The 2027 marketing line that companies 'will connect with 60,000 attendees' is a forecast and was not stored. A corporate organiser name was not clearly labelled on the homepage fetch. Same venue as cairo-ict-2027 and cairo-international-book-fair-2027, different events. cairo-ict-2027 (7–10 November 2027, category tech) is already in the Atlas and was not re-added.
