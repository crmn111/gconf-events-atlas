About

Dates are local to the host city. Official SiGMA South Asia page and official Global Tour: 30 November–2 December 2026, second edition, at Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams, No. 15 Justice Akbar Mawatha, Colombo. Official agenda lists exhibition hours 10:00–18:00 on exhibition days (Lumina Ballroom); the first calendar day also includes a delegates-arrival and pre-registration programme. Forecasts ('expected to welcome 1,000 / 3,500 delegates') and the unlabelled 'Our Attendees 5K Delegates' block were not stored. A passage on the official page still refers to Bangkok/Thailand and to Affiliate World Bangkok (3–4 December); that is leftover or travel-adjacent copy and was not used as the venue. Affiliate World is not a SiGMA event and was not staged. The SiGMA Poker Tour evening on 2 December is a side event, folded into this record. Conference + expo ('both'): category tech, also_categories expo.

Topics: igaming, gaming, affiliates, regulation.
