About

Dates are local to the host city. Official Afreximbank host-agreement announcement (9 March 2026): the fifth Intra-African Trade Fair takes place in Lagos, 5–11 November 2027, organised by Afreximbank, the African Union Commission and the AfCFTA Secretariat. The official IATF 2027 site (2027.iatf.africa) was still a holding page when fetched and did not itself repeat the dates or name a venue, so the venue is recorded as to be announced and the pin is city-level Lagos. Forward-looking targets (100,000 visitors, 2,500 exhibitors, US$50 billion in deals) are forecasts and were not stored. Cumulative visitor figures across earlier editions were not assigned to this record. The official Lagos International Trade Fair site still confirmed only 6–15 November 2026 and was not used to invent 2027 LITF dates; a later LITF 2027 announcement could overlap this week.

Topics: intra-african trade, AfCFTA, investment, industry.
