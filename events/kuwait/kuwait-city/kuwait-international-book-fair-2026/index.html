About

Running now (opened 18 Nov, runs until 28 Nov 2026), slogan 'We Read... We Rise'; Qatar guest of honour; about 600 exhibitors from 34 countries (KUNA). Press guides place it at the Kuwait International Fairground in Mishref; the venue is not stated in the KUNA text, so venue is left blank. Kuwait dates are local (Arabia Standard Time, UTC+3).
