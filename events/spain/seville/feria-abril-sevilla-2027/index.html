About

Included as a culture festival (the official city tourism calendar lists it among Seville’s fiestas). The Real and casetas include food and drink, but this is not treated as a food/wine festival. Dates are local to the host city (Central European Time, UTC+1 / UTC+2 in summer; Canary Islands are UTC+0 / UTC+1 in summer).

Topics: feria, andalusian culture, caseta, flamenco.
