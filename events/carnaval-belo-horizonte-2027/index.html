About

Official carnival period 23 Jan-14 Feb 2027 (street blocos across the city). Brazil dates are local (Brasília time, UTC-3, unless the venue is in another Brazilian time zone, e.g. Manaus UTC-4).

Topics: carnival, street blocos, music.

Official PBH news: the 2026 edition 'movimentou 6,6 milhões de foliões' (cumulative revellers over the season, incl. 349,000 tourists).
