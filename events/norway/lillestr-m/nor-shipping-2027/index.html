About

Dates are local to the host city. Official homepage states the exhibition is 8–11 June 2027 at NOVA Spektrum, Lillestrøm; those are the dates stored here. The visitor FAQ lists opening hours from Tuesday 7 June 10:00–17:00 through Thursday 10 June 10:00–17:00 and Friday 11 June 10:00–15:00, so 7 June appears in the FAQ hours, but the homepage does not clearly treat 7 June as an exhibition day. Official 2025 recap: 63,698 total number of visits (visits, not unique visitors). This is a maritime industry exhibition, not a leisure boat show.

Topics: shipping, maritime, ocean industries.

Official 2025 recap on the 2027 homepage: 63,698 total number of visits (visits, not unique visitors) and 1,012 exhibiting companies.
