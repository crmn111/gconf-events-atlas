Dates are local to the host city. Official: 20–23 October 2027, NOVA Spektrum, Lillestrøm. Hours were not printed on the official 'Where, when and what' page. A 27 October 2025 post-show article is titled '34,992 THANK YOU!' but the body does not explicitly label that figure as visitors, so it was not stored.
