About

Official site (fetched 24 Nov 2026) still presents PANAFEST 2025, 25 July–2 August 2025, at Heritage House / Ghana, West Africa — past. Official about text: a biennial festival ('Every two years') that gathers 'thousands from Africa and the diaspora' on the coasts of Ghana. The next biennial edition would fall in 2027; no official 2027 dates were published. Tour-operator packages with invented 2027 windows were not used. Ghana dates are local (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT, Africa/Accra, UTC+0, no daylight saving).

Topics: Pan-African culture, heritage, performing arts, Emancipation Day.
