About

Official homepage (fetched 24 Nov 2026) still presents the 11th edition, 24–27 September 2026, Kumasi, theme 'Film and Global Culture: The New Wave of African Cinema', and already announces the 2026 award winners — that edition is past. No official 2027 dates for the main festival. Ghana dates are local (Greenwich Mean Time, GMT, Africa/Accra, UTC+0, no daylight saving).
