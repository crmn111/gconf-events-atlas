Dates are local civil dates for the official Australian Open 2027 window at Melbourne Park, including Opening Week (10-16 January) and the main draw through the singles finals on 30-31 January, as published by Tennis Australia on ausopen.com. Official 2027 ATP and WTA calendars list the main-draw start as 17 January.
