About

Official 2027 edition marks 30 years of Boom, at Boomland, Idanha-a-Nova. Dates are local to the host city (Western European Time, UTC+0 / UTC+1 in summer).

Topics: psytrance, transformational, art.

Official Boom Festival tickets-info page (fetched 24 Nov 2026): “Boom Festival 2027 tickets sold out in 2 hours. 40,000 tickets.” Stored as organiser expected attendance of 40,000 ticketed visitors for the 2027 edition.
