About

Opens the Montevideo carnival season, which runs more than 40 days. The samba schools parade on Fri 22 Jan, and the Concurso Oficial starts on Mon 25 Jan 2027 at the Teatro de Verano Ramón Collazo (official Teatro de Verano page). Uruguay dates are local (Uruguay time, UTC-3 all year, no daylight saving).

Topics: carnival, murgas, parade, floats.

No organiser attendance figure found on the official pages checked (24 Nov 2026). Ranked by an estimated size tier 'very large' (about 100,000-1,000,000 attendees; our judgement from format, venue and scale, not an official number).
