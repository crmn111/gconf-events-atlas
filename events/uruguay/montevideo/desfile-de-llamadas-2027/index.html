About

Candombe parade of the comparsas, official since 1956. The 2027 edition is named after Ruben Rada (Intendencia launch note). Uruguay dates are local (Uruguay time, UTC-3 all year, no daylight saving).

Topics: candombe, comparsas, Afro-Uruguayan culture, parade.
