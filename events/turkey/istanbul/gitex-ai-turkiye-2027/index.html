About

Official homepage title and countdown are “GITEX AI TÜRKİYE | 26–27 October 2027” and “See you in Istanbul! 26 October 2027 … 10:00 AM Istanbul, Türkiye.” The same homepage lists “Venue Istanbul Expo Center.” The dedicated plan-your-visit page title still shows leftover “9-10 Sept 2026” and names Halls 2–4 / opening hours; those hall numbers are not treated as confirmed for 2027. Dates are local to the host city (Turkey Time, UTC+3 year-round).

Topics: artificial intelligence, technology, startups, investment.

No clearly worded expected-attendance figure for the 2027 edition was found on the official pages fetched 24 Nov 2026. Ranked by our estimated size tier 'medium' (10,000–30,000), a judgement from the two-day Expo Center format, not an official number.
