About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 20–22 March 2027 at Metropolitan Expo, Spata. Trade only (official 2026 recap: 'a purely B2B exhibition'). Official concurrent wine-and-spirits section OENOTELIA is fully integrated into FOOD EXPO and is folded into this record (same official dates and venue on the FORUM SA trade-shows calendar). Opening hours and ticket prices were not listed on the official homepage or 2026 recap fetches. Homepage marketing lines of 1,300 exhibitors and 40,000 key buyers were not stored; the labelled 2026 actual is stored instead. Same venue as beyond-expo-2027 (April) and the staged HORECA 2027 record (February), different events.

Topics: food, beverages, wine, spirits, F&B trade.

Official 2026 actual: 39,969 visitors from 80 countries, from the official FOOD EXPO recap article. The same article also labels 1,300 exhibitors (including 200 international), 3,797 international buyers and 837 Hosted Buyers.
