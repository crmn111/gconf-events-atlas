About

Dates are local to the host city. Official FORUM SA trade-shows calendar: 12–15 February 2027 at Metropolitan Expo, Spata. Described as the largest international trade show for the provisioning and equipment of hospitality and foodservice companies in Greece. Opening hours, ticket prices and a labelled previous-edition attendance figure were not listed on the official trade-shows calendar fetch; the dedicated HORECA organiser page timed out in this pass and hours were left blank. Same venue as the staged FOOD EXPO 2027 record and as beyond-expo-2027, different events.

Topics: hospitality, foodservice, tourism, HoReCa.
