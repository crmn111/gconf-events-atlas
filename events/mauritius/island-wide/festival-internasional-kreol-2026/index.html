About

Press (Le Mauricien 23 Nov 2026; Radio One 20 Nov 2026) report 14-20 Dec 2026 under the theme 'Kiltir Kreol amenn linite': opening at the Nelson Mandela Centre for African Culture (14 Dec), Grand Konser at Grand-Gaube (19 Dec), closing at Mahébourg Waterfront (20 Dec). Not used as official dates.

Topics: sega, slam, Creole culture, gastronomy, concerts.
