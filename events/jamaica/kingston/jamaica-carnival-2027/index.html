About

Carnival season runs from January; the main weekend is Bacchanal J'ouvert (Fri 2 Apr 2027) and the Road March (Sun 4 Apr 2027). Xodus Carnival's 2027 theme is 'REIGN'. Jamaica dates are local (UTC-5 all year, no daylight saving).

Topics: carnival, soca, road march, J’ouvert, masquerade.
