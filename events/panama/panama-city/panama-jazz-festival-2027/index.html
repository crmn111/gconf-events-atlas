About

Artists include Paquito D'Rivera, Danilo Pérez, Victor Wooten & Steve Bailey. Other stages: Teatro Dorado (Santa Ana) and Rock & Folk, plus jam sessions. The festival says it has drawn more than 500,000-600,000 people since it began (cumulative, not used). Panama dates are local (Panama time, UTC-5 all year, no daylight saving).

Topics: jazz, Latin jazz, music education.
