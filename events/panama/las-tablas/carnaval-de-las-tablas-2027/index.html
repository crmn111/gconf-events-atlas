About

Panama's best-known carnival. The calendar Carnival is Sat 6-Tue 9 Feb 2027; after the 9 Oct 2026 earthquake press reports each district decides whether to hold it, and the Las Tablas carnival board had not confirmed when checked (press, not used).

Topics: carnival, parades, folk music, street celebration.

No organiser attendance figure found on the official pages checked (24 Nov 2026). Ranked by an estimated size tier 'very large' (about 100,000-1,000,000 attendees; our judgement from format, venue and scale, not an official number).
