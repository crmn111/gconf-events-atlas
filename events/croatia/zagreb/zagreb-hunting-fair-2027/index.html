About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 4–7 February 2027 at Zagreb Fair, Avenija Dubrovnik 15, 10020 Zagreb, 'for the first time with its own dedicated dates'. Opening hours and ticket prices were not listed on the official about page. Official previous-edition figures: 27,200 visitors, 91 exhibitors from 9 countries, exhibition space exceeding 1,800 m². The official Zagreb Boat Show (17–21 February 2027, same venue) was looked at and not staged here because of its nautical/yachting character (Biograd Boat Show is already in the yachting vertical).

Topics: hunting, outdoor equipment, hunting equipment.
