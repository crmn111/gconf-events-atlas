About

Official FAQ: music on the main Garden site starts around midday on 7 July and finishes in the early hours of 14 July at 02:00; the published festival run is 7–13 July. Official ticket page lists the same span (the ticket platform’s “British Summer Time” label is the sales site’s clock, not the event timezone). A separate tickets address at Petrića Glava 28b was not used in place of the official FAQ address at no. 34. Dates are local to the host city (Central European Time, UTC+1 / UTC+2 in summer).

Topics: electronic music, house, The Garden.
