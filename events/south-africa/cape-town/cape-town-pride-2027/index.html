About

Theme: Loud in Every Colour. Pride Parade and Mardi Gras: Saturday 27 February 2027, beginning in Alfred Street, De Waterkant, marching to Green Point Track. Cape Town is due to host WorldPride from 30 Sep–14 Oct 2028 (outside this file’s mid-2028 window). South Africa dates are local (SAST, UTC+2, no daylight saving).

Topics: Pride, LGBTQ+, parade, Mardi Gras.
