About

24th edition, restyled as part of the Month of the Mother City (4–26 March 2027). Homecoming weekend 12–13 March; main festival weekend 19–21 March (family day on 21 March). New city-centre model, not a single arena booking. South Africa dates are local (SAST, UTC+2, no daylight saving).

Topics: jazz, live music, community concerts.
