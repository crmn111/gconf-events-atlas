About

Dates are local to the host city. Official Grain SA NAMPO page: 11–14 May 2027 at NAMPO Park, Bothaville. Opening hours and ticket prices were not listed on that page. Grain SA is the official organiser of NAMPO; the page also links out to the NAMPO website. A separate September 2027 listing for Bredasdorp Park (NAMPO Cape) was not folded in. A 2–3 October 2026 NAMPO Park line on the same page was treated as a leftover/other listing and was not used for 2027 dates. City-level Bothaville pin only (NAMPO Park is not in OSM).
