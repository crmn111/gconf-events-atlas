About

South Africa’s longest-running music festival (since 1990), on the Splashy Fen farm in the Drakensberg. South Africa dates are local (SAST, UTC+2, no daylight saving).

Topics: live music, camping festival, Drakensberg.

No organiser attendance figure for a recent edition was found on the official pages checked (24 Nov 2026). The official ticket page says tickets are limited to 7,000 (a capacity cap, not used as attendance). Ranked by an estimated size tier 'medium' (about 1,000–10,000; our judgement from the published cap and festival format, not an official number).
