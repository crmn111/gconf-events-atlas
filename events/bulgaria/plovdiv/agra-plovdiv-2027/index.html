About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 23–27 February 2027 at International Fair Plovdiv, Tsar Boris III Obedinitel Blvd 37. Official visitor hours 09:00–18:00. Official ticket prices: regular €7.60 (BGN 14.86); reduced tickets for retired, disabled people and students €4 (BGN 7.82). Official concurrent VINARIA, FOODTECH and TASTES FROM ITALY are folded into this umbrella record (the official page states AGRA is part of that concurrent forum). Official AGRA 2026 key figures: more than 52,000 local and international visitors (stored as 52,000). A separate general line that 'every year, approximately 700 companies from 30 countries take part in the forum' was not stored. Official exhibitor working time 08:00–19:00 is not the visitor hours.

Topics: agriculture, wine, food industry, agribusiness.

Official AGRA 2026 key figures: more than 52,000 local and international visitors (and 530 exhibitors from 15 countries). Stored as 52,000 with that 'more than' wording. The 52,000 figure is labelled as AGRA 2026 key figures; the official page also describes AGRA as part of a concurrent forum with Vinaria, Foodtech and Tastes from Italy.
