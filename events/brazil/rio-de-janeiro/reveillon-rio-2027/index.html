About

Free event; Palco Rio in front of the Copacabana Palace (Djavan, Simone Mendes and others). Brazil dates are local (Brasília time, UTC-3, unless the venue is in another Brazilian time zone, e.g. Manaus UTC-4).

Topics: New Year, fireworks, Brazilian music, free concerts.

Official Riotur 'Réveillon em dados 2026-2027' report: about 2.5 million people expected in Copacabana (organiser projection; 13 stages city-wide).
