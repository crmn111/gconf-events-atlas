About

Dates are local to the host city. Official BiS SiGMA South America page and official Global Tour: 5–7 April 2027, fifth edition, at the Transamerica Expo Center, Av. Dr. Mário Vilas Boas Rodrigues 387, Santo Amaro, São Paulo. Official AIBC Americas (aibc.world/americas/ and the current AIBC upcoming-summits list) is the officially co-located sister event at the same venue and dates (5–7 April 2027) and is folded into this record. Official AIBC and SiGMA agendas describe a shared exhibition (10:00–18:00 on exhibition days) and conference (11:00–16:00); the first calendar day includes a delegates-arrival / golf / awards programme. Unlabelled '17,500 delegates' marketing (and a typo on the AIBC home page still calling it a summit 'in Europe') was not stored. BiS merged with SiGMA in 2023; the official organiser is SiGMA Group. Conference + expo ('both'): category tech, also_categories expo.

Topics: igaming, gaming, affiliates, sports betting.
