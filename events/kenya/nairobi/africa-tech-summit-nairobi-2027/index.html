About

9th edition. Official homepage: 'Where African Tech Connects – February 10 & 11, 2027' and FAQ: 'The 9th edition will convene in Nairobi, Kenya, at the Sarit Expo Centre on February 10th – 11th 2027.' Official pages also mention a 9 February pre-day/welcome reception for Pro pass holders, not counted in the main two conference days. Kenya dates are local (East Africa Time, EAT, UTC+3, no daylight saving).

Topics: startups, investment, artificial intelligence, fintech, climate tech.

Official Africa Tech Summit Nairobi homepage (fetched 24 Nov 2026) lists '2 k+ Delegates', '1 k+ Companies' and '170 + Speakers'. Used the official 2,000+ delegates floor.
