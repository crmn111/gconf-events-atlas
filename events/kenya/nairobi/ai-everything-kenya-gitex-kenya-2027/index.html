About

Official organiser registration page title (fetched 24 Nov 2026): 'AI EVERYTHING KENYA x GITEX KENYA | 12-14 May 2027'. The expo is named at Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC). A 2026 FAQ still on the same page describes a two-venue format (Inclusive AI Summit at Sarit Expo Centre; expo at KICC); 2027 pages checked do not restate a 2027 Sarit summit day, so the record uses KICC as the named 2027 expo venue. The aieverythingkenya.com homepage currently redirects to GITEX Africa (Marrakech); the /register page is the Kenya event site. The Kenyan Office of the Special Envoy on Technology lists 18–20 May 2027; the organiser title date 12–14 May 2027 is used. Kenya dates are local (East Africa Time, EAT, UTC+3, no daylight saving).

Topics: artificial intelligence, startups, digital economy, investment.

No organiser attendance figure found on the official pages checked (24 Nov 2026). Ranked by an estimated size tier 'medium' (our judgement from format, venue and scale, not an official number).
