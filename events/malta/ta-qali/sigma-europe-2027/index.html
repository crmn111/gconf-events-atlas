About

Dates are local to the host city. Official SiGMA Europe ticket page (re-fetched 24 Nov 2026): 3–5 November 2027 at the Malta Fairs and Conventions Centre (MFCC), Millennium Stand, National Stadium, Ta' Qali ATD 4000. Official Global Tour lists the same stop as 1–5 November 2027; the event-specific ticket page still prints '03-05 November 2027' and those ticket-page dates are stored, with the tour-page 1–5 window noted as unresolved (it may include arrival or adjacent days). Official Europe page: daily opening hours have shifted to 10:00; a closing time was not stated. Official Standard ticket prices: sale €294, door €419 (other paid tiers exist; Platinum perks are described but no second price was extracted). Unlabelled '12,000 delegates' / '12K Delegates' marketing was not stored. Expo, conference, awards and side events ('Malta Week'). Same venue period as aibc-malta-2027 (3–5 May 2027, already in the published Atlas), different event.
