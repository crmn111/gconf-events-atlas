About

Dates are local to the host city. Hours: 09:00–18:00 on 24–26 March and 09:00–15:00 on 27 March. 24–25 March trade only; general public admitted with purchased tickets on 26–27 March. Online ticket purchase opens from January 2027; prices were not yet listed. Overlaps the opening day of the already-published ai-expo-taiwan-2027 (24 March, Expo Dome), a different event and venue.
