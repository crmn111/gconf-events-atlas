About

Dates are local to the host city. Umbrella record for the five concurrent official shows: FOOD TAIPEI, FOODTECH TAIPEI, BIO/PHARMATECH TAIWAN, TAIPEI PACK and TAIWAN HORECA, all at TaiNEX 1 & 2. Hours: 10:00–18:00 on 23–25 June, trade only; 10:00–17:00 on 26 June, also open to the general public with paid admission. Persons under 13 not admitted. Public ticket prices were not listed on the fact sheet.

Topics: food, food technology, packaging, HORECA, pharma.
