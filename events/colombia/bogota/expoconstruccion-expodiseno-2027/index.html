About

Dates are local to the host city. Official site: 11–16 May 2027 at Corferias, Bogotá. Official hours: 11 May 12:00–20:00; 12–16 May 10:00–20:00. Officially one event (Expoconstrucción y Expodiseño), organised by Corferias and Camacol. Described as a mixed (feria mixta) fair. Tickets at Corferias box offices or the official site; prices were not listed in the fetch. 'Muestra comercial aprox. 400 expositores' is an approximate marketing figure and was not stored. Same venue as agroexpo-2027, different event.

Topics: construction, architecture, interior design, building materials.
