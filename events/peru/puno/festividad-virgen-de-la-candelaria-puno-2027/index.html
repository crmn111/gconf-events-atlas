About

On UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. Peru's foreign ministry reports the safeguarding committee is preparing the 2027 edition and seeking UNESCO patronage.

Topics: folk dance, music, UNESCO intangible heritage, costumes.

No organiser attendance figure found on the official pages checked (24 Nov 2026). Ranked by an estimated size tier 'very large' (about 100,000-1,000,000 attendees; our judgement from format, venue and scale, not an official number).
