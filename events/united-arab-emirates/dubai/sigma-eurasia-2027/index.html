About

Dates are local to the host city. Official SiGMA Global Tour: 1–4 December 2027, labelled 'RAK – Dubai'. Official SiGMA Eurasia and official AIBC Eurasia pages name the same event at Festival Arena, Dubai Festival City, Al Badia Blvd, Dubai; AIBC Eurasia is folded in as the co-located sister brand. Opening hours on the AIBC Eurasia timeline: exhibition 10:00–18:00 on exhibition days. The AIBC Eurasia timeline tabs are still numbered 15–18, which do not match December 2027; the weekdays (Wed–Sat) do match 1–4 December 2027, so dates are taken from the official Global Tour, not from those leftover day numbers. The named venue is in Dubai, not Ras Al Khaimah. Conference + expo ('both'): category tech, also_categories expo.

Topics: igaming, gaming, AI, fintech, affiliates.
