About

Official organiser site titles the 2027 edition GISEC GLOBAL I 4-6 May 2027 I Dubai. The official DEC venue listing JSON-LD instead gives 22-24 Jun 2027; the organiser dates are used and the conflict is recorded here. Venue is DEC, Expo City Dubai (the official site states the show moved there in 2026; the DEC listing also places the 2027 edition in the South Halls). UAE dates are local (Gulf Standard Time, UTC+4).

Topics: cybersecurity, information security, technology.
