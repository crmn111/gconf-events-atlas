About

Official 2027 event page and WGS news set dates 1-3 Feb 2027 in Dubai. A building-level venue for 2027 was not named on the official pages fetched, so the pin is city-level. The official news figure of more than 6,250 participants refers to the 2026 edition and was not stored as 2027 attendance. UAE dates are local (Gulf Standard Time, UTC+4).

Topics: government, public policy, innovation.
