About

Dates are local to the host city. Official Pamerindo Exhibition Calendar 2027 (file dated 20 Oct 2026): 1–4 December 2027, Manufacturing Indonesia Series, officially co-located with Machine Tool Indonesia, Tools & Hardware Indonesia, Industrial Automation Indonesia, Production Logistics Indonesia, Industrial Paint & Coatings Indonesia and Fastener Indonesia, all folded into this record. The official 2027 calendar line does not print a venue; the official show venue page (fetched 24 Nov 2026) names Jakarta International Expo, Kemayoran, with no contrary 2027 venue, and that is the venue used. Official 2025 recap: over 41,000 trade attendees (and more than 1,300 exhibiting companies from 29 countries). The 2026 edition (2–5 December 2026, still upcoming at the time of this fetch) had not yet taken place, so 2025 is the previous completed official actual. Opening hours and ticket prices for 2027 were not listed. Same venue as art-jakarta-2027 and data-center-indonesia-2027, different events. JS '100' counters on the homepage were not stored.

Topics: manufacturing, machine tools, industrial automation, logistics.
