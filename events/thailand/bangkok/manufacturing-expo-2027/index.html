About

Dates are local to the host city. Official fact sheet: 16–19 June 2027, BITEC, Bangkok. Hours 10:00–18:00 from the official Thai conference page (Buddhist year 2570). Trade only; formal dress; visitors under 15 not admitted. Seven concurrent official shows (plastics, mould, automotive parts, surface treatment, industrial automation, electronics manufacturing, factory construction) are folded into this umbrella record. The event homepage still showed leftover 17–20 June 2026 day tiles in this fetch; 2027 dates were taken from the official fact sheet and conference page. 'Over 2,000 exhibitors' and 'over 90,000 participants' are marketing/future wording and were not stored.

Topics: plastics, moulds, automotive parts, automation, electronics, coatings, factory design.
