About

Dates are local to the host city. Official: 14–17 June 2027 at JIEC Halls, next to Mecca Mall, Amman, 22nd edition, UFI approved. Official concurrent SONEX 2027 (20th Solar Near East Trade Show) and JOPEX 2027 (7th Jordan Packaging, Printing & Plastic Industries Trade Show), plus the JIMEX-SONEX forum, are folded into this record. Opening hours were not listed (the official page includes an 'Event Schedules - Draft' heading). Ticket prices were not listed. Lines such as '25,000+ trade visitors' and '250+ exhibitors' are unlabelled 2027 scale wording and were not stored. SOFEX (defence) was not added. INTERBUILD / Jordan Build were official 2027 construction events and were not padded in beside this industrial show. Amman is not treated as a war-zone skip.

Topics: machinery, electricity, energy, solar, packaging.
