About

Dates are local to the host city. Official Belgrade Fair 2027 calendar: 10–13 May 2027, 69th International Fair of Technics and Technical Achievements (UFI), Halls 1, 1A, 2, 3 and 3A. Opening hours and ticket prices for 2027 were not stored: the official show statistics page still showed leftover 19–22 May 2026 copy, a zeroed exhibitor counter, 10:00–18:00 hours and 800/500 RSD tickets. Official facts & figures 2025 on the 2027 calendar page (17,548 visitors; 592 exhibitors) were not stored as the previous-edition actual because a 2026 edition took place and a labelled 2026 visitor figure was not confirmed on the official pages fetched. Different event and site from expo-2027-belgrade (EXPO complex, Surčin). Same venue as gitex-ai-belgrade-2027 (25–27 May) and belgrade-book-fair-2027 (October), different events.

Topics: industry, manufacturing, automation, energy, Industry 4.0.
