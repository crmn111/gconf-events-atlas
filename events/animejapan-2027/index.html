About

Public days 27-28 March; business days 26-27 March 2027. First edition in Osaka (2027 and 2028 move from Tokyo). Dates are local to the host city (Japan Standard Time, UTC+9).

Topics: anime, manga, voice actors, merchandise.

Official AnimeJapan release (Kyodo News PR Wire): AnimeJapan 2026 drew 'a record total of about 156,000 visitors' (Tokyo Big Sight, 28-29 March 2026); 2027 is the first Osaka edition.
