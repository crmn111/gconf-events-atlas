About

Dates are local to the host city. Official visitor hours at Baku Expo Center, 515 H. Aliyev ave., AZ1050, Baku: Wednesday 2 June 2027 13:00–18:00; Thursday 3 June 10:00–18:00; Friday 4 June 10:00–16:30 (access limited 30 minutes before closing). Official professional-visitor registration only; no ticket price was listed on the official ticket/registration pages fetched.
