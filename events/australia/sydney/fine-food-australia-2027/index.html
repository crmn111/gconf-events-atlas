About

Dates are local to the host city. Trade event at ICC Sydney. Free if registered online before 6 September 2027; AUD 55 + GST for registrations made on show days. Children under 15 not admitted. The FAQ's '23,000+ qualified industry professionals' and '900+ exhibitors' wording is general scale, not a labelled previous-edition actual, and was not stored. A Fine Food visit page still mentioning 2026 Melbourne dates was not used.

Topics: foodservice, hospitality, food, beverage.
