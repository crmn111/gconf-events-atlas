About

Inaugural Australian International Boat Show, launched by Informa in Sydney's Darling Harbour. The about page describes the 2027-2029 windows as 'proposed show dates'; the official media release confirms the 29 July 2027 opening, and the published 29 July-1 August 2027 window is used here.

Topics: boat show, yachting, marine.
